🧰 15.2 — Semrush Prompt Research - AI Search Intent

Dabbo 15 · Semrush Tools · ✅ Done

15.2.A Aa stop su chhe

Loko AI tools ma su puchhe chhe - e prompts research karva. Keyword research jem, AI prompts ni pan volume ane intent hoy chhe. Ahiya thi AI-optimized content topics male. Real run: Skyline Stone, 19 Nov 2026.

15.2.B Semrush Prompt Research — /ai-seo/prompt-research/ (step-by-step)

Goal: loko AI tools ne su puchhe chhe e topics ane questions shodhva, pachhi nakki karvu kaya target karva.

Step 1: Research taiyar karo

Tool kholta PEHLA nakki karo: project brief mathi ek seed topic, target country ane AI platform view. Product ke service topic lo, brand naam nahi.

Working example, real run 19 Nov 2026: seed topic "diamond blades" for Skyline Stone, United States, All AI platforms, data as of Nov 18 2026. Jupiter Semrush Server 2 thi run karyu (aa report mate Server 1 ma upgrade prompt batyu).

Step 2: Saacho report kholo

Jupiter → Semrush (Server 1 upgrade maange to Server 2) → AI → AI Visibility → Prompt Research (/ai-seo/prompt-research/).

"Enter a topic to analyze" ma seed topic type karo ane Analyze click karo. Upsell window khule to bandh kari ne Analyze fari click karo. Results page par country tab (US, UK, DE, FR) ane platform filter (All AI platforms) check karo.

Step 3: Summary cards vacho

Note karo: Related Topics AI Volume, Topics ni count, Prompts ni count, Intent split, Brands Mentioned ane Source Domains.

Real run: AI Volume 4.7M, Topics 1K, Prompts 32.3K. Intent: Informational 48%, Commercial 25%, Navigational 12%, Transactional 11%, Task 4%. Brands Mentioned 15.8K (top: gia, brilliant earth, reddit). Source Domains 19.8K (top: youtube.com, google.com, reddit.com).

Aa AI activity na estimates chhe, Google search volume NATHI. Keyword Overview na volume sathe same unit tarike compare na karo.

Step 4: Related topics table juo

Columns: Topic, Relevance, AI Volume, Intent, Actions. AI Volume thi sort karo ane top rows kholo. Table ma 1K topics chhe (100 pages), etle fakt page 1 thi judge na karo.

Real run (page 1): Diamond Blades and Cutting Tools 25.6K; Diamond Knives and Blades 1.8K; Diamond and Granite Cutting Blades 1.6K; Diamond Blade Metal Cutting 1.1K; diamond blade types 588; diamond blade barbering 281; MK Diamond Products and Blades 148; diamond blade warehouse 135.

Step 5: Unrelated topics kadho

Darek row ma Relevance High dekhay, pan e word match chhe, business match nathi. Darek topic ne product range pramane Keep ke Reject mark karo.

Skyline Stone mate: Keep — "Diamond and Granite Cutting Blades", "Diamond Blade Metal Cutting", "diamond blade types". Reject — "diamond blade barbering" ane "Diamond Knives and Blades" (vaaL ane kitchen knives, stone tools nahi). Motu AI Volume e topic ne relevant nathi banavtu.

Step 6: Ek topic na prompts kholo

Topic row click karo — ena prompts khulse. Columns: Prompt, AI Response, Brands, Sources, Actions (Create Content, Monitor).

Real run, topic "Diamond and Granite Cutting Blades": "What are the best diamond blades for cutting granite?" (Brands 7, Sources 3); "blade to cut granite" (AI Overview answer, Brands 6, Sources 10); "diamond blades for granite" (Brands 6, Sources 7). "View full response" thi vacho ke AI e kaya brands ane sources naam lidha.

Step 7: Prompts pasand karo ane research save karo

E prompts lo je site par hati collection/product page sathe match kare, ke je page banavvi joie. AI already je brands ane sources cite kare chhe e note karo — e j competitors ane haravvana pages chhe.

Tracker columns: Research date | Seed topic | Country | Platform view | Topic | AI Volume | Intent | Keep/Reject | Prompt | Brands cited | Sources cited | Target page | Next action. Monitor fakt tyare vapro jyare plan Prompt Tracking ma prompts umeravani permission aape.

Step 8: Final quality check

Koi pan prompt ke topic e guarantee nathi ke AI tools site ne mention karshe.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Saachu seed, country, platform view ane date.
  • Unrelated topics reject thay gaya (barbering, kitchen knives).
  • AI Volume ne AI estimate tarike label karyu, Google volume nahi.
  • Prompts kholi ne responses vanchaya, fakt titles nahi.
  • Output: kept-topic list, 5–10 target prompts ane AI cite karta competitors sathe.

15.2.C Expected Output

Output: target prompts ni list - je prompts ma client no business relevant chhe + intent notes.
