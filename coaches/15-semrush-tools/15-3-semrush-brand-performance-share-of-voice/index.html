🧰 15.3 — Semrush Brand Performance - Share of Voice & Sentiment

Dabbo 15 · Semrush Tools · ✅ Done

15.3.A Aa stop su chhe

AI platforms ma brand nu share of voice ane sentiment kevu chhe - positive, negative ke neutral mentions. Brand reputation AI search ma track karvi. Real run: Skyline Stone, 19 Nov 2026.

15.3.B Semrush Brand Performance — 4 reports (step-by-step)

Reports: Brand Performance (/ai-seo/brand-performance/), Perception (/ai-seo/perception/), Narrative Drivers (/ai-seo/narrative-drivers/), Questions (/ai-seo/questions/). Goal: AI tools aapni brand vishe su bole chhe e jovo.

Step 1: Brand profile taiyar karo

Project brief sathe confirm karo: domain, target country ane language. Brand Performance mate darek domain nu ek brand profile joie, ane account ma profiles limited hoy chhe.

Working example, real run 19 Nov 2026: skylinestoneproducts.com, United States, English, platform ChatGPT, data dated Nov 19 2026. Jupiter Semrush Server 2 thi run karyu (Server 1 ma upgrade prompt batyu). Profile bharva ma ~10 minutes lagi; tya sudhi Semrush "Come back in 5-10 minutes" batave chhe.

Step 2: Report kholo ane profile set karo

Jupiter → Semrush (Server 2) → AI → AI Visibility → Brand Performance (/ai-seo/brand-performance/) kholo. Domain nakho, Analyze click karo, pachhi "Set up new brand profile" ma Domain, Target location (United States) ane Target language (English) confirm karo ane Get started click karo.

Perception, Narrative Drivers ane Questions aa j profile vanche chhe. Profile ready thay pachhi AI Visibility menu thi kholo.

Step 3: Brand Performance overview vacho

Pehla Semrush e pasand kareli competitor list check karo, pachhi Share of Voice, Overall Sentiment ane Key Business Drivers juo. Platform ane date note karo.

Real run: competitors Granquartz, Bb industries, Alpha professional tools, Park industries. Share of Voice: Granquartz 8%, Bb industries 5%, Alpha professional tools 3%, Park industries 3%, Skyline 1%, Other 80%. Overall Sentiment: Favorable 78%, General 22%. Semrush e Skyline ne "Far behind" mark karyu.

Share of Voice = sample ma AI answer mentions no share, website traffic NATHI. Competitor list ne check sudhi "Candidate" j ganо.

Step 4: Key Business Drivers table vacho

Table batave chhe darek driver ketli vaar kayi brand sathe jodayo. Driver kholo — ena pachhal na answers dekhase.

Real run (Skyline vs Granquartz): Digital Commerce Reach 5 vs 6; Advanced Material Coverage 4 vs 16; Service Reliability and Guarantees 2 vs 12; Professional Market Penetration 2 vs 10; Technical Support Differentiation 1 vs 4; Procurement Simplification none vs 14.

Step 5: Insights cards vapro

Darek report AI-generated Insights thi chalu thay chhe. Ene ideas tarike lo, pachhi data ma verify karo. Koi AI overview box "Couldn't generate an AI-powered overview" sathe fail thay to Try again click karo ke ignore karo — numbers valid j rahe.

Real run insights: "CNC Microsite, Bigger Voice" ane "Dominate Porcelain Questions" (Narrative Drivers), "Beat 'Just Another Catalog'" (Perception).

Step 6: Perception — sentiment ane ena reasons vacho

Perception kholo. Juо: Competitive Perception by Platform, Favorable Sentiment ranking, Key Sentiment Drivers (Brand Strength Factors ane Areas for Improvement) ane Sentiment by Feature Category.

Real run: Favorable Sentiment Granquartz 88%, Park industries 85%, Skyline 78%, Bb industries 72%, Alpha professional tools 68%. Strengths: reliable delivery and service, curated third-party brands (Cyclone, Distar, Tenax), one-stop stone tools, chemicals and sinks, professional trade focus. Improvements: limited depth of Skyline-branded CNC tooling, seen as regional or mid-size, awareness barrier ("just another catalog"). Category-wise Skyline: pricing and value 100%, quality and service 100%, pan user experience and e-commerce 50% ane brand and market presence 50%.

Dhyan: ahiya fakt ChatGPT j data batave chhe. Google AI Mode, Perplexity ane Gemini ma "Paid plan" dekhay.

Step 7: Narrative Drivers — share of voice ane sources vacho

Narrative Drivers kholo. Juо: Share of Voice by Platform, Mentions, Average Position ane Top Domains by Citations.

Real run: Skyline share of voice 1% (Granquartz 8%). Semrush platform note: ChatGPT ma 13%, competitors ma lowest. Top cited domains: granquartz.com 39 citations, obsidian-industrial.com 30, stonify.io 24, bbindustriesllc.com 24, en.wikipedia.org 18, skylinestoneproducts.com 8.

Cited domains = AI je pages par bharoso kare chhe. AI already cite kare e domains mate content, PR ke partner mentions plan karo.

Step 8: Questions — loko su puchhe chhe e vacho

Questions kholo. Juо: Topic Distribution, Query Intent Distribution ane darek topic niche na questions.

Real run: intent split comparison 46%, research 46%, support 8%, education 1%. Topics: supplier discovery, brand reputation and vendor comparison, logistics and pricing, tooling performance (blades, pads, CNC), support and training, chemicals and sealers. Sample questions: "Where do most stone fabrication shops buy their diamond blades and polishing pads?", "Who offers the best balance of price and durability for diamond blades used in stone fabrication?", "Which brands offer polishing pads that deliver a high gloss on granite but won't burn quartz?"

Step 9: Findings ne actions ma convert karo ane record save karo

Darek gap mate ek action lakho, target page sathe. Example: low Advanced Material Coverage (4 vs 16) → porcelain ane UCS hub page banao; low CNC support mentions → 3–5 CNC case studies publish karo; "matching diamond tools to stone types" question → blade selection guide.

Tracker columns: Research date | Domain | Country | Language | Platform | Share of Voice | Favorable sentiment | Top competitor | Weakest driver | Top cited domains | Priority questions | Action | Target page | Owner.

Step 10: Final quality check

Brand Performance = ek date na AI answers no sample. Koi pan action e guarantee nathi ke AI tools brand ne mention karshe.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Saachu domain, country, language, platform ane date.
  • Competitors real product overlap mate check thay gaya.
  • Percentages ne AI sample shares tarike label karya, traffic ke GSC data nahi.
  • Paid-plan platforms "Not available" mark karya, zero nahi.
  • Output: ek brand baseline + questions ane cited domains sathe jodayela content actions ni list.

15.3.C Expected Output

Output: brand performance report - share of voice %, sentiment summary, improvement actions.
