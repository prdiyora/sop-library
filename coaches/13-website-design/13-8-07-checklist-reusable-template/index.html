13.8.G G. Final QA checklist

  • AI tells ≤ 2 (01)
  • 2 fonts, 5 colours, ek radius
  • Header: 7 items max, search, cart, sticky, mobile drawer
  • Hero real photo + 1 CTA
  • Section layouts alag
  • Reviews real
  • Footer legal pages + contact real
  • Contrast 4.5:1, tap target 44px
  • LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, images WebP
  • Mobile + tablet + desktop test
  • 5-second test 3 loko par pass
  • Alt text, H1 ek j, internal links
