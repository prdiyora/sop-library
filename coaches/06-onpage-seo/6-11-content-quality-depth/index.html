📄 6.11 — Content Quality & Depth

Dabbo 06 · On-Page SEO · ✅ Done

Google helpful content system pachhi thin content rank j nahi thay. Har page "koi pan AI 10 second ma lakhi shake" jeVu hoy to replace thai jashe. Depth + originality = survival.

6.11.A Quality na markers

  1. Search intent satisfy karo: user aa page par su expect kare te male - no clickbait mismatch
  2. Puri coverage: topic nu full answer - user ne pachho Google par javab shodhvu na pade
  3. Potanu original value: unique data, own photos, real examples, personal experience - je copy na thay
  4. Vanchi shakay evo format: nana paragraphs, subheadings, lists, tables, images
  5. Update karelu: juna facts = trust gumay. Dates + data regularly refresh karo

6.11.B Thin content na signals (avoid karo)

  • 300 words page je koi question fully answer na kare
  • Same content multiple pages par (duplicate)
  • Review/value-add vagar nu AI-generated text
  • Keyword stuffing je read karva ma awkward lage

6.11.C Test

Har page mate pucho: "Jo aa page internet par na hot, to koinu nuksan?" Answer "na" hoy to page improve karo ke remove karo.

6.11.D Checklist

  • Top 20 pages nu quality audit thayelu
  • Thin pages improve/merge/remove karela
  • Key pages par original elements (data/photos/experience)
