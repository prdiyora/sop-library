📄 6.5 — Must-Have Pages (About Us, Contact Us & More)

Dabbo 06 · On-Page SEO · ✅ Done

Notebook: "All pages must be creation - About us, Contact us, many more". Google ane users banne ne trust mate aa basic pages jaruri chhe. Ena vagar site incomplete lage ane EEAT score ghate.

6.5.A Essential pages list (koi pan website type)

  1. About Us: kon chho, story, mission, team photos (real!), experience
  2. Contact Us: form + phone
  • email + address + WhatsApp + map + kaam na hours
  1. Privacy Policy: forms, cookies ke analytics vaparta ho to mota bhaag na countries ma kayda mujab jaruri (India DPDP Act, EU/UK GDPR) — exact text client/lawyer thi confirm
  2. Terms & Conditions / Terms of Service
  3. Refund/Return Policy: ecommerce mate must, services mate cancellation policy
  4. Shipping Policy: ecommerce mate (charges, timeline, areas)
  5. FAQ page: common questions (AEO/GEO mate bonus)

6.5.B Trust pages (industry pramane)

  • Portfolio/Case Studies (services)
  • Testimonials/Reviews nu page
  • Certifications/Awards
  • Careers (jo hiring karta hoy)

6.5.C Rules (niyamo)

  • Badha pages footer ma linked hova joiye
  • Real content - "Lorem ipsum" ke thin 2-line page worst chhe
  • About Us page par real photos - stock photo ni team photo nahi

6.5.D Checklist

  • 7 essential pages live ane footer ma linked
  • Real content (placeholders vagar)
  • Contact info badhe consistent (NAP consistency)
