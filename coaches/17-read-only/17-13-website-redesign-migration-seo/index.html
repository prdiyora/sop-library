🛠️ 17.13 — Website Redesign & Migration SEO

Dabbo 07 · Technical SEO · ✅ Done

Navu design, navu platform (WordPress → Shopify) ke navu domain - aa SEO nu sauthi risky kaam chhe. Redirects bhuli gaya to mahinao ni ranking ek divas ma jay. Design & Web Development section sathe aa follow karvu jaruri.

17.13.A Launch pehla

  1. Old site no full crawl + GSC top pages + backlink vala pages export.
  2. Redirect map: old URL | new URL - dar important URL 1:1 relevant page par 301. Badhu homepage par redirect nahi.
  3. Titles, meta, H1, content ane internal links navi site ma carry karo - kharab design change ok, content loss nahi.
  4. Staging site noindex/password protected - pan launch time te hatavvu yaad rakho.

17.13.B Launch divas

  • robots.txt ma Disallow: / nathi te check
  • Noindex tags remove karo
  • Redirect map test karo (Screaming Frog list mode)
  • Navu sitemap GSC + Bing ma submit; domain change hoy to GSC Change of Address
  • GA4/GTM tracking live chhe te confirm

17.13.C Launch pachhi (6-8 week)

  • Dar week GSC: 404, indexing drop, top queries ni position
  • Thodo fluctuation 2–6 week normal; pan motu drop (20%+) ke 404 vadhe to tarat redirects/indexing check - raah na juo
  • Redirects ochama ochu 1 varas rakho

17.13.D Checklist

  • Redirect map 100% important URLs cover kare
  • Launch divas na checks thai gaya
  • Before/after traffic compare sheet taiyar chhe
