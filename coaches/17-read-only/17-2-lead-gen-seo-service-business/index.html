📖 17.2 — Lead Gen SEO (Service Business)

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Aa SOP plumber, agency, clinic, consultant, contractor jeva badha service business mate chhe.

Aa business ne fakta website traffic nathi joitu. Temne booked calls ane leads joie chhe.

Ecommerce SEO ane Lead Gen SEO ma thodo farak chhe. Ecommerce SOP sathe niche na rules pan apply karo.

Source: 2026 service-business playbooks (links niche aapeli chhe).

17.2.B 2. Page architecture (13.1 NAVI architecture sathe match kare)

1. Dar service mate alag page banao.

  • Customer je shabdo search kare, ena pramane page banao.

  • Company na technical shabdo pramane nahi.

  • Example: Customer "drain cleaning" search kare chhe, "hydro-jetting solutions" nahi.

2. Dar city / service-area mate alag page banao.

  • Fakta evi city mate page banao jya tame kharekhar service aapo chho.

  • Ek j template ma 50 city na naam badli ne pages na banavo.

  • Aava pages ne Google doorway pages gani shake chhe. Aa Google ni spam policy viruddh jai shake chhe.

  • Dar city page par alag ane useful content rakho:

    • Local work proof

    • Local customer reviews

    • E area ni khas mahiti

3. Proof pages banao.

  • Residential ane commercial work mate alag case studies.

  • Customer reviews mate Reviews Hub.

  • Kaam pehla ane pachhi na photos mate Before/After Gallery.

4. Website structure aa rite rakho:

Homepage → Services Hub → Service Pages → City Pages

Blog na related articles mathi service pages ne internal links aapo.

17.2.C 3. Service page must-haves (rank + convert banne mate)

Dar service page par aa 8 vastu hovi joie:

1. Headline: Service + City + USP lakho. Visitor ne 3 second ma samjai javu joie ke tame su aapo chho ane kem sara chho.

2. Scope: Service ma su include chhe ane su nathi, e clear lakho. Confusion hase to lead ochhi aavshe.

3. Pricing: Price range, "starting at" price ke quote melavvani process samjavo. Khotu price kyarey na lakho. Audit PDF no rule ahi pan same chhe.

4. Timeline: Kaam puru thata ketla kalak ke divas lagse, e janavo.

5. Service area + Payment: Kya area ma service aapo chho ane payment / financing na options su chhe, e lakho.

6. CTA: Ek clear action aapo — Call, Form ke WhatsApp. CTA page na uparna bhag ma (above the fold) ane end ma pan rakho.

7. Schema: Service + LocalBusiness + FAQ schema add karo (08 → 04).

8. Reviews / Proof: Customer reviews ane kaam na proof e j page par batavo.

17.2.D 4. Keyword strategy — P&L jevu vicharo

1. High-margin services pehla target karo.

  • Je service ma vadhu profit male, ene pehla priority aapo.

  • Fakta vadhu traffic aape eva keywords pachhal na dodo.

  • Search volume karta ek job ni value pan juvo.

2. Buyer-stage keywords shodho.

Aa keywords e loko search kare chhe je service leva mate ready hoi shake:

  • "cost"

  • "quote"

  • "installer"

  • "contractor near me"

  • "emergency + service + city"

3. Location keywords target karo.

  • "city + service"

  • "service + near me"

4. Low-margin emergency services alag rakho.

  • Emergency service ma profit ochho hoy to alag page ane messaging rakho.

  • Lead levata pehla qualification steps rakho, jethi khabar pade ke customer tamari service mate fit chhe ke nahi.

5. Keyword research mate same SOP follow karo.

  • 02 → 07

  • 03 → 06 (Semrush SOP)

17.2.E 5. Local SEO (Lead Gen nu 50% kaam ahi chhe)

Service business mate Local SEO khub jaruri chhe.

1. GBP complete ane optimize karo.

Google Business Profile ma aa badhu sachu ane complete rakho:

  • Categories

  • Services

  • Photos

  • Posts

  • Q&A

Full process: 06 → 04 Local SEO

2. Reviews ni system banao.

  • Dar job puri thay pachhi customer pase review mango.

  • Positive ane negative, badha reviews ne reply aapo.

3. NAP consistency rakho.

NAP = Name, Address, Phone Number.

Badhi business listings ane citations ma aa details same rakho.

4. Local keywords map karo.

  • Service keywords ne related Service Pages sathe jodo.

  • City keywords ne related City Pages sathe jodo.

17.2.F 6. Conversion path (traffic aave to lead bane)

Traffic aavya pachhi visitor call ke form bhare, e important chhe.

1. Ek page = ek primary CTA.

Ek page par 3 alag primary CTA hase to visitor confuse thashe ane lead ochhi aavshe.

2. Phone number easy rakho.

  • Phone number clickable hovo joie.

  • Desktop par top-right ma rakho.

  • Mobile par sticky phone button rakho, je scroll karta pan dekhay.

3. Form short rakho.

Fakta jaruri fields rakho:

  • Name

  • Phone

  • Service

  • Message

Lambo form hase to loko vachche thi chhodi deshe.

4. Response time lakho.

Example: "2 kalak ma call pacho karishu."

Pan je time lakho, e pramane kharekhar reply pan aapo.

5. Leads track karo.

  • Call tracking setup karo.

  • Form submission tracking setup karo.

  • GA4 ma aa banne ne key events tarike set karo (07 → 02).

Tracking nahi hoy to SEO thi ketli leads aavi ane ketlo faydo thayo, e client ne batavi nahi shako.

17.2.G 7. Blog nu role (Lead Gen ma)

Blog no main goal service pages ne support karvano chhe.

1. Service related blog topics lakho.

Example:

  • "How much does X cost in [city]?"

  • "X vs Y"

  • "Signs you need X"

Aa topics customer na questions na jawab aape chhe ane service pages sudhi lai jay chhe.

2. Internal links aapo.

  • Dar blog mathi related service page par 1–2 contextual links aapo.

  • Link article na relevant text ma natural rite mukvo.

  • Workflow same: 03 → 07.

3. AEO format follow karo.

  • Service related questions na clear ane direct answers lakho.

  • Aa questions Google AI Overviews ma pan aavi shake chhe.

  • AEO SOP: 05 → 07.
