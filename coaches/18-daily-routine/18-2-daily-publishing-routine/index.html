🌅 18.2 — Daily Publishing Routine (Site Ready Pachhi)

Daily Routine · Roj nu kaam · ✅

18.2.A Rule No. 1 — Roj MAX 10 navi pages

  • Site full ready + live thay PACHHI j aa routine chalu thay.
  • Roj maximum 10 navi pages publish karo — blog, service page, location page, koi pan type.
  • 10 thi vadhu ek divas ma NA. Ek sathe ghana pages = Google ne spam laage, indexing pan slow thay.
  • Quality first: 10 sarchi pages > 30 average pages.

18.2.B Publish PEHLA — QA must

  • Darek page publish karta PEHLA 6.16 Pre-publish QA pass karvu farjiyat.
  • QA fail = publish NA. Pehla fix, pachhi publish.

18.2.C Publish PACHHI — Request Indexing (max 10/day)

  • GSC ma URL Inspection kholine darek navi page mate Request Indexing karo.
  • Roj max 10 URLs j request karo — limit aa j rite 10 pages/day sathe match thay.
  • Full process: Dabbo 07 → 7.1 Indexing Check.

18.2.D Record — Tracking sheet ma note karo

  • Publish kareli darek page: date + URL + keyword — 9.7 Tracking Sheet SOP pramane bharo.
  • Weekly report ma "aathvadiye ketli pages publish thayi" item add karo.

18.2.E Related stops

Related stops: 6.16 Pre-publish QA; 7.1 Indexing Check; 9.7 SEO Tracking Sheet SOP; 18.1 Daily Report Reading.
