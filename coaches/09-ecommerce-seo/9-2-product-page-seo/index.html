🛒 9.2 — Product Page SEO

Dabbo 09 · Ecommerce SEO · ✅ Done

Product page long-tail ane brand/model keywords par rank kare chhe ane conversion nu last step chhe. Kharab product page = traffic aave pan sale nahi.

9.2.A Must-have elements (jaruri vastu-o)

  1. Title: Product name + main attribute ("Organic Kids Lip Balm - Strawberry, 4g"). Brand end ma.
  2. Unique description: manufacturer copy nahi - benefits, use-case, who it's for, ingredients/material, size.
  3. Images: multiple, real, alt text sathe (Image SEO doc).
  4. Price, stock, delivery time, return policy - fold ni upar ke najik.
  5. Reviews: real customer reviews page par visible.
  6. Product schema: name, image, price, currency, availability, brand (Schema doc).
  7. Related products + parent collection ni link.

9.2.B Merchant listings (free)

Google Merchant Center ma products add karo to Shopping tab ane free listings ma batay - organic product visibility mate. Shopify ma Google & YouTube app thi connect thay. Price/stock site ane feed ma same hova joiye.

9.2.C Common mistakes (aa bhool na karo)

  • 100 products ni same description (duplicate content)
  • Description fakt image ma - Google text vanchi na shake
  • Schema ma price/stock page thi alag

9.2.D Checklist

  • Top 20 sauthi vadhu vechata products: alag description
  • Product schema valid chhe (Rich Results Test)
  • Merchant Center connected chhe, feed errors 0
