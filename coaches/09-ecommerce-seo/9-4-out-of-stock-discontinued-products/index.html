🛒 9.4 — Out-of-Stock & Discontinued Products

Dabbo 09 · Ecommerce SEO · ✅ Done

Stock khatam thay tyare page delete karvu sauthi common bhool chhe - ranking, backlinks ane reviews badhu jay. Temporary ane permanent case alag handle karo.

9.4.A Temporary out-of-stock

  1. Page live rakho, index ma rakho.
  2. "Back in stock soon" + expected date + notify-me option.
  3. Similar in-stock products page par batavo.
  4. Availability update: site schema ma https://schema.org/OutOfStock ane Merchant feed ma out_of_stock (banne same hova joiye).
  5. Collection ma product niche khaso.

9.4.B Permanently discontinued

  • Navu version/replacement chhe → 301 replacement product par
  • Replacement nathi pan close category chhe ane page par traffic/backlinks chhe → 301 relevant collection par
  • Koi value nathi → 404/410
  • Badha discontinued products homepage par 301 nahi - Google ene soft 404 gane

9.4.C Seasonal products

Diwali/Christmas jeva products na URLs varas-varas same rakho (date vagar slug) - juni ranking aavta varase kaam aave.

9.4.D Checklist

  • Out-of-stock policy written ane team ne khabar
  • Discontinued list monthly review + redirects
  • Schema/feed availability accurate
