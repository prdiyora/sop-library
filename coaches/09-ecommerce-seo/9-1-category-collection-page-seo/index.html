🛒 9.1 — Category & Collection Page SEO

Dabbo 09 · Ecommerce SEO · ✅ Done

Ecommerce ma sauthi vadhare buying keywords ("kids makeup kit", "leather belts for men") category/collection pages par rank kare chhe, product pages par nahi. Etle category page = ecommerce nu main money page.

9.1.A Optimize kevi rite

  1. Keyword: category ne transactional keyword map karo (Keyword Mapping doc juvo). 1 keyword = 1 collection.
  2. Title + H1: "Kids Makeup Kits" jevu clear, generic "Shop All" nahi.
  3. Intro text: grid ni upar 1-3 line short intro (user mate); vadhare helpful content (buying guide, FAQ) grid ni niche.
  4. Product grid: best sellers/in-stock pehla - out-of-stock products last ma.
  5. Internal links: sub-collections, related collections ane 2-3 related blogs ne link.
  6. Breadcrumbs: Home > Category > Sub-category (BreadcrumbList schema sathe).

9.1.B Shopify mate khas

  • Collection description theme ma visible chhe te check karo - ghana themes ma hidden hoy
  • Automated collections ma pan manual SEO title + meta set karo
  • Tags/filters thi bani nava URLs index na thay (Ecommerce point 03)

9.1.C Common mistakes (aa bhool na karo)

  • Be collections ma same products + same keyword = cannibalization
  • Empty ke 1-2 product vali collection index ma - thin page
  • Blog thi product par link aapo pan collection par nahi

9.1.D Checklist

  • Har collection: alag title, H1, meta, shuruaat nu text
  • Collection-keyword mapping sheet ma
  • Blogs → collections na internal links active chhe
