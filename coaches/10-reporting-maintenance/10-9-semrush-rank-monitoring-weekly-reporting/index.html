10.9.B Semrush Position Tracking — Weekly Review & Reporting (step-by-step)

Step 1: Overview → Rankings Overview kholo

  1. Project ma "Overview" tab kholo.
  2. "Rankings Overview" section shodho.
  3. Akhi tracked keyword list review karo.
  4. Darek keyword ane badha available metric columns capture karo: Volume, Position, Potential Traffic, SF/SERP Features, Intent ane bija useful columns (je dekhay).
  5. Semrush export option aape to akhi table CSV/XLSX ma export karo. Table paginated hoy to BADHA pages/rows include karo.
  6. Export available na hoy to akhi table tracking spreadsheet ma copy karo. Fakt dekhata pehla rows J save na karo.

Step 3: Aa week vs pachhlu week compare karo

Dar somvar nu export ALAG dated snapshot tarike rakho. Juno data overwrite na karo.

  • Current Position ne pachhla somvar ni Position sathe compare karo.
  • Current position number NANo hoy to Improved; moto hoy to Declined; same hoy to Unchanged.
  • Keyword aa week na data ma chhe pan pachhla snapshot ma na hatu to New.
  • Pehla tracked keyword gayab thay ke position na hoy to, "Lost / Not ranking" mark karta PEHLA Semrush report verify karo.
  • Mota drops, nava keywords ane ranking URL changes review karo. Jarur pade tya content, internal-link ke technical follow-up actions note karo.

Step 4: Somvar 7:00 AM checklist

  1. Semrush → Position Tracking kholo.
  2. Saachu Client A project ane target market confirm karo.
  3. Overview → Rankings Overview kholo.
  4. Badha keyword rows export/capture karo — table na BADHA pages sathe.
  5. Volume, Position, Potential Traffic, SF, Intent ane bija available columns save karo.
  6. Dated file save karo, e.g. Client A-Stone-Position-Tracking-YYYY-MM-DD.xlsx.
  7. Snapshot master sheet ma umero — pachhla weeks delete karya vagar.
  8. Rankings compare kari ne improved, declined, unchanged ane new keywords mark karo.
  9. Important ranking movements ane actions nu short weekly summary lakho.

Aa example kevi rite vaparvu:

  • Keyword: exact tracked search query.
  • Intent: Semrush e batavelo search intent label (example: I).
  • SF: keyword mate dekhata SERP features ni count.
  • KD %: Keyword Difficulty percentage.
  • Your Pot. Traffic / Your Pot. Growth: report e batavela potential traffic ane potential growth values.
  • Pos. Nov 14 / Pos. Nov 19: banne comparison dates na ranking positions.
  • Diff: related metric bagde dekhato change value; report nu value/sign juvo rakho.
  • Visibility: project mate dekhato visibility percentage.
  • Est. Traffic: keyword/report row ne attributed estimated traffic.
  • Vol. / CPC: search volume ane cost-per-click values.
  • URL: e keyword mate hal rank karto landing page.
