Dabbo 10 · Reporting & Maintenance · ✅ Done

10.9.A Aa stop su chhe

Semrush Position Tracking ma dar week keyword ni ranking check karvani chhe.

Pachhla week ane aa week ni ranking compare karvani.

  • Kaya keywords upar gaya?

  • Kaya keywords niche gaya?

  • Kaya keywords same rahya?

  • Kaya keywords nava aavya?

  • Ranking sudharva mate su karvu joie?

Aa badhi mahiti weekly tracking sheet ma lakhvani ane client ne short report aapvano.

Real run: Skyline Stone weekly review — 19 Nov 2026.

10.9.B Semrush Position Tracking — Weekly Review & Reporting (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Overview → Rankings Overview kholo

  1. Semrush ma Skyline Stone project kholo.

  2. Overview tab par click karo.

  3. Rankings Overview section shodho.

  4. Badha tracked keywords ni list check karo.

  5. Darek keyword mate je metrics dekhay te save karo:

    • Volume

    • Position

    • Potential Traffic

    • SF / SERP Features

    • Intent

    • Bija badha useful columns

  6. Semrush ma Export option hoy to akhi table CSV/XLSX ma download karo.

  7. Table ma ghana pages hoy to badha pages na rows save karo.

  8. Export option na hoy to badhi mahiti tracking spreadsheet ma copy karo.

Important: Fakt pehla page na keywords save na karta. BADHA rows ane available columns leva jaruri chhe.

Step 2: Weekly tracking sheet columns

Weekly tracking sheet ma niche na columns rakho.

Step 3: Aa week vs pachhlu week compare karo

Dar somvar nu ranking data alag file ma save karvanu.

Juna week no data delete ke overwrite na karvo.

Ranking compare karvani rit:

  1. Aa week ni Position juvo.

  2. Pachhla somvar ni Position juvo.

  3. Banne positions compare karo.

  4. Position no number nano thayo hoy to Improved.

  5. Position no number moto thayo hoy to Declined.

  6. Banne positions same hoy to Unchanged.

  7. Aa week keyword chhe pan pachhla week ma na hato to New.

  8. Pehla keyword hato pan have dekhato nathi, ke position missing chhe, to Lost / Not ranking mark karta pehla Semrush ma verify karo.

StatusKevi rite olakhvu
ImprovedAa week no position number pachhla week karta nano chhe
DeclinedAa week no position number pachhla week karta moto chhe
UnchangedBanne week ni positions same chhe
NewAa week keyword chhe, pachhla snapshot ma na hato
Lost / Not rankingKeyword missing chhe ke current position nathi. Pehla verify karo

Example:

  • Position 10 → 5 = Improved

  • Position 3 → 8 = Declined

  • Position 4 → 4 = Unchanged

Pachhi aa vastu check karo:

  • Kaya keywords ni ranking ghani niche gai?

  • Kaya nava keywords aavya?

  • Kaya keyword no ranking URL badlayo?

  • Kaya page nu content improve karvu joie?

  • Kya internal links umerva joie?

  • Koi technical SEO problem chhe?

Jarur hoy tya follow-up action Notes column ma lakho.

Step 4: Somvar 7:00 AM checklist

Dar somvar savare 7:00 AM aa checklist follow karo.

Step 5: Important notes

1. Example data ane real project

learnosphere.in / India / 100 keywords screen fakt example chhe.

Real task ma Skyline Stone nu domain ane approved target market j use karo.

2. Semrush metrics

Semrush ma kaya metrics male ane data ketli vaar update thay, te plan ane project settings par depend kare chhe.

Je fields available hoy te j record karo.

3. Data update check

Export karta pehla check karo ke Semrush data update thayu chhe ke nahi.

Update na thayu hoy to:

  • Screen par dekhato last update time lakho.

  • Account nu tracking update schedule check karo.

4. Shared account safety

Shared Semrush account mathi koi project direct delete na karo.

Pehla confirm karo ke project have jaruri nathi. Pachhi approval lo.

Confirmation code fakt ek vaar na UI confirmation mate hoy chhe. Aa code SOP ma save na karvo.

5. Old weekly reports

Badha juna weekly exports safe rakho.

Ena thi ranking no trend joi shakay ane time sathe ranking ketli sudhari ke bagdi te sachi rite compare kari shakay.

10.9.C Expected Output

Final output:

  1. Weekly tracking sheet ma aa week no navo data add thayelo hoy.

  2. Pachhla week no data safe hoy.

  3. Improved, Declined, Unchanged ane New keywords mark thayela hoy.

  4. Important ranking changes ni notes lakhayeli hoy.

  5. Client ne short weekly summary aapeli hoy:

    • Kaya keywords upar gaya

    • Kaya keywords niche gaya

    • Have kaya SEO actions levana chhe

Final goal: Dar week Skyline Stone ni rankings track karvi, changes samajva ane client ne clear report aapvo.
