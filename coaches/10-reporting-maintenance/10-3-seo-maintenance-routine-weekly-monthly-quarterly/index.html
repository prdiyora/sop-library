📊 10.3 — SEO Maintenance Routine (Weekly / Monthly / Quarterly)

Dabbo 10 · Reporting & Maintenance · ✅ Done

SEO ek vaar karine bhuli javanu kaam nathi. Fixed routine hoy to problems nana hoy tyare j pakdaay - client pehla tame jaano.

10.3.A Dar hafta (darek site par 30-45 min)

  • GSC Performance: clicks/impressions trend, achanak ghatado?
  • GSC Pages: nava indexing errors?
  • Nava published pages: index thayela + 2-3 internal links (No Orphan doc)
  • Clarity: 5-10 recordings

10.3.B Dar mahine

  • Screaming Frog crawl: 4xx, redirects, missing titles
  • Core Web Vitals + PageSpeed key pages
  • 2-4 pages nu content refresh
  • Backlinks: nava links, lost links
  • Client report (11 → 06 Monthly Client Report Format)

10.3.C Dar quarter

  • Content audit (Refresh/Merge/Remove doc)
  • Keyword mapping + competitor gap fari check
  • Strategy review: su work karyu, su nahi, next quarter priorities
  • Google updates nu review (Search Status Dashboard, Search Central blog)

10.3.D Checklist

  • Har client mate routine calendar ma set
  • Maintenance log sheet: date | check | issue | action

Daily/weekly/monthly reporting routine: 11 Client Reporting System → 00 Start Here.

10.3.E Added 16 Nov 2026 - 10 sites rota + new links

10 sites rule: dar Monday 30 min x site (daily report joyu, 3 actions). Site ni list + owner + last-reviewed date ek sheet ma. Roj 5 min: live report check karo (roj 5:30 PM IST update thay chhe)

Update thayu hoy to maintenance sheet ma note karo ane next action decide karo

Dar mahine: brand check ane forecast review karo

Dar quarter: badha tools na access review karo (jyare member join/leave thay tyare pan)
