📈 9.8 — Tracking Sheet Template

Dabbo 09 · Reporting & Maintenance · ✅ Done

Aa Google Sheet template chhe — website par teni structure ane steps. Original Drive ma "Make a copy" kari ne darek client mate vaprvanu.

9.8.A Setup — 4 steps

  1. Template kholo (Make a copy link) — darek client mate alag copy. Copy pachi pehli data row ma CTR ane Total na formulas chhe ke nahi 1 var check karo.
  2. Dashboard tab ma C1 ma domain ane D2 ma pehlo mahino (e.g. 01-Nov-2026) bharo. Baki badhi dates ane months automatic bane.
  3. Blue text = tame bharvo chho. Gray italic row = automatic, touch na karvo.
  4. Password aa sheet ma KYAREY na lakho. Login details password manager ma rakho.
