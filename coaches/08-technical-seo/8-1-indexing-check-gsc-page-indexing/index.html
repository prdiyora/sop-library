🛠️ 8.1 — Indexing Check (GSC Page Indexing)

Dabbo 08 · Technical SEO · ✅ Done

Page Google na index ma nathi to rank thavano j nathi - content game etlo saro hoy. Etle SEO ma pehlo sawal hamesha: "Aa page index chhe ke nahi, ane nathi to kem?"

8.1.A Kevi rite check karvu

  1. GSC → Indexing → Pages: Indexed vs Not indexed count juvo. Trend achanak niche jay to red flag.
  2. Not indexed reasons vanchho: dar reason no matlab alag chhe, badha "problem" nathi.
  3. URL Inspection: important page nu URL nakho → "URL is on Google" chhe ke nahi, user-declared vs Google-selected canonical juvo.
  4. Live test: page render thay chhe, robots/noindex block nathi te confirm karo.

8.1.B Common reasons ane su karvu

  • Crawled - currently not indexed: Google e crawl karyu pan hamna index nathi karyu (mostly quality, duplicate ke ochi demand karan) - content improve karo, internal links vadharo, duplicate hoy to merge karo.
  • Discovered - currently not indexed: Google e crawl j nathi karyu - internal links, sitemap ane site quality check karo.
  • Duplicate without user-selected canonical: canonical tag set karo (Technical point 02 juvo).
  • Alternate page with proper canonical tag: mostly normal chhe (filters, variants) - kai karvanu nathi.
  • Excluded by noindex / Blocked by robots.txt: intentional chhe ke bhool thi? Money page hoy to tarat fix.
  • Not found (404) / Soft 404: Technical point 03 juvo.

8.1.C Indexing speed na 2026 facts

  • IndexNow: Bing, Yandex, Seznam, Naver, Amazon product search vanche — GOOGLE VAANTO J NATHI (Google e 2022 ma test kari ne adopt na karyu). Free; Cloudflare/Shopify/Wix/RankMath ma ek toggle. Enable karo, pan Google mate asar 0.

  • Google Indexing API: faqt JobPosting ke livestream (BroadcastEvent) pages mate — general pages par scope violation (quota revoke risk). 200 requests/day. Ecommerce/blog pages mate use nahi.

  • Google mate real channels: XML sitemap (primary), homepage/crawled pages thi internal links, URL Inspection → Request Indexing (key pages only, daily quota).

  • Realistic indexing time: established site = kalak thi 2-3 divas; navi site = divas thi 2-6 week. Google kyarey crawl/index ni guarantee nahi aapto (crawling-indexing FAQ).

  • Detail + fast ranking playbook: 03 → 10 Fast Ranking Strategy (11 Nov 2026).

8.1.D Senior ni soch

Badha pages index karvana nathi hota. Filter, tag, cart, thin pages index na thay te saru chhe. Focus: money pages (category, product, service, top blogs) index hova joiye — koi exclusion hoy to reason check karo.

8.1.E Checklist

  • Top 20 money pages URL Inspection thi indexed confirmed
  • Not indexed reasons ni sheet: karan | URL count | iradapurvak? | shu karvu
  • Monthly indexed pages trend note thay chhe
