🚂 16.3 — Ecommerce Blog SEO Workflow: Category thi Blog sudhi

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Koi pan category / collection par sales ane organic traffic laave evo blog banavva mate 3-step process. 04 Blog Strategy no full detail version.

16.3.A Aakho flow ek nazar ma

  • Step 1: Category page joi ne blog title options banavo, pachhi client pase review karavo.
  • Step 2: Final title par website mathi j Top 5 products ane target keywords levaa.
  • Step 3: Title, products, keywords ane 5 rules saathe final blog lakho.

Why aa order? Pehla title nakki thaay to j sacha products ane keywords male. Products ane keywords nakki thaay pachhi j blog sacha internal links ane sales laave.

16.3.B Step 1: Blog Title nakki karvu

  1. Category / collection page kholo: kaya products che, kaya problem solve kare che, kon kharide che (e.g. new parents) — note karo.
  2. GSC → Performance → Page filter = collection URL → Queries: aa collection par kaya informational/question queries impressions le che? Aa blog title na best ideas che.
  3. Semrush Keyword Magic Tool (target country) → collection no main keyword → Questions tab. Google PAA ane autocomplete pan juo.
  4. Search intent check: title nu keyword Google ma search karo — top results blogs/guides hova joiye. Products/collections j dekhay to e blog topic nathi (collection page j optimize karo).
  5. 5 title options lakho, dar ek sathe 1 line: kem saru che (traffic + sales + intent). Rule: title collection na products sathe naturally jodai shake.
  6. Client ne review mate mokalo. Approve thayelu title j Step 2 ma vaparo.

16.3.C Step 2: Products ane Keywords levaa

2A. Top 5 products

  1. Collection mathi title ne sauthi relevant 5 products pasand karo — in-stock, saru selling, ane blog na alag-alag sections ma fit thay evi.
  2. Dar product mate note karo: product name, product URL, blog ma kya section ma vaparvu.
  3. Product repeat na karvi, jarur hoy to j. Title related biji collection hoy to tena product/link pan add kari shako.
  4. Dhyan rakho: Dar product URL website par khoti ne check karo — URL kharekhar khule che ane product in-stock che. Check karya vagar koi URL blog ma na vaparo.

2B. Target keywords

  • Primary: 1 keyword (title no main) — Semrush ma volume + KD check (06 Semrush SOP pramane).
  • Secondary: sambandhit variations.
  • Topic-wise: blog na sections pramane (e.g. nail / hair / daily care).
  • Audience-intent: audience mate (e.g. new-parent keywords).
  • AEO / GEO long-tail questions: Semrush Questions + Google PAA thi — FAQ ane H2/H3 mate.

16.3.D Step 3: Final Blog lakhvo (writer brief)

Writer ne (ke tame pote lakhta ho to) aa brief aapo:

VigatShu aapvu
Blog titleClient dwara approve thayelu final title
Collection linkMain collection URL — blog ma 2 vaar internal link
ProductsStep 2 na 5 products: name + verified URL + section
KeywordsStep 2 na badha keyword groups
FormattingImportant text bold; blog ma real image na muko — fakt photo space (Rule 3)
Rules (niyamo)Niche na 5 rules strictly follow karvana

16.3.E Blog na 5 Rules

  • Product mention rule: Paragraph ma kadi pan full product name na lakhvu (bold ke plain, kai j nahi). Tena badle short, natural, lowercase anchor text (2 thi 5 words) vaparvu, jem ke "modular baby crawl mat". Aa anchor text j clickable link hoy, sentence ni andar naturally, alag line ma nahi.
  • Link rule: Fakt verified real product URL j vaparvo. URL na hoy to product text ma mention karo pan link na banavo. Fake ke guess URL kadi nahi.
  • Photo space rule: Je paragraph ma product link hoy, tena tarat pachhi aa 2 lines muko, badhe same format (niche joyo).
  • Alt text rule: Lowercase, natural spacing, "collection" ke "category" jeva words nahi.
  • Bullet punctuation rule: Badha bullet points ma ek j punctuation, ya badhe colon (:) ya badhe dash (-). Mix nahi.

Photo space format (Rule 3):

Alt text: [lowercase alt text] (photo mate jagya)

16.3.G Blog publish karta pehla: Quick Checklist

  • Title client e approve karyu?
  • Badha product URLs website par khoti ne check karya?
  • Collection link 2 vaar internal link chhe?
  • Full product name paragraph ma kyay nathi?
  • Dar product link pachhi alt text ane photo space chhe?
  • Bullets ma punctuation ek sarkhu chhe?
  • Primary keyword title, intro ane ek H2 ma chhe?
  • Publish pachhi collection page parthi pan blog ne link (No Orphan Pages)?
