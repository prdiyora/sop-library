🚂 16.2 — Semrush Keyword to Content SOP

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Version 1.2 | 20 Nov 2026 Applicable: Blog, Category, Collection, Product, Service ane Resource Pages

Aa SOP ma shu shikhvanu chhe?

Aa SOP thi team no navo member pan Seed Keyword thi sharu kari ne complete SEO Content Workflow follow kari shakshe.

Full Process:

Keyword Research → Shortlist → Topic Groups → Keyword Mapping → SERP Analysis → Content Brief → Writing → Internal Linking → Performance Tracking.

Pehla 3 rules samjo

1. Ranking ni guarantee nathi.

SOP follow karvathi ranking ni tak vadhe chhe. Pan Google ranking Content Quality, Competition, Backlinks, Technical SEO ane Search Intent par pan depend kare chhe.

2. Competitor nu content copy na karvu.

Competitor nu 70–90% content same rakhi ne thoda words badalva e khoti practice chhe. Google Spam Policies nu risk chhe.

Top Results study karo. Pachhi original examples, data ane useful information sathe better page banavo.

3. Semrush numbers estimates chhe.

Volume, KD% ane Traffic estimates chhe. Existing website mate Google Search Console (GSC) na real performance data ne priority aapo.
