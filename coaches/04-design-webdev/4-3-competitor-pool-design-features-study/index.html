🎨 4.3 — Competitor Pool - Design & Features Study

Dabbo 04 · Design & Web Development · ✅ Done

Design reinvent karva ni jarur nathi - competitors ma su work kare chhe te study karo, pachhi te thi better banao.

4.3.A Steps

  1. Top 5 competitors ni sites kholo (mobile
  • desktop banne).
  1. Screenshot + notes: homepage nu layout, navigation, product/service page nu structure, checkout/inquiry flow.
  2. Features list: kaya tools/features offer kare chhe? (calculators, WhatsApp chat, comparison tables, size guides, EMI option...)
  3. Trust signals note karo: reviews shu batave, guarantees, certificates, payment badges.
  4. Temni UX mistakes note karo: slow pages, confusing menu, lamba forms - aa tamara opportunity chhe.

4.3.B Output

  • Feature comparison table: Feature | Comp 1-5 | Tamara ma chhe? | Priority
  • "Must have" (badha pase chhe) vs "Nice to have" (1-2 pase) vs "Differentiator" (koi pase nathi)

4.3.C Checklist

  • 5 competitors na design notes + screenshots
  • Feature comparison table taiyar chhe
  • 1 week ma thai shake eva 3 quick improvements identify karo
