Senior golden rules (badha SOP par lagu)

  1. Data pehla, opinion pachhi: GSC/GA4 thi decision; Semrush/Moz = estimate.

  7. Claim label: CONFIRMED (Google official) / LIKELY (industry) / SPECULATION — client ne kyarey guarantee nahi.
  8. Kyarey number banavvo nahi: period + source dar report ma.
  9. Security: passwords/tokens sheet, doc ke chat ma nahi — password manager.

13.1.G [6] 02 Domain Research & Selection/01 Keyword Volume Research

﻿Keyword Volume Research Domain ke website mate saacho keyword shodhvo etle foundation. Keyword ni search volume (ketla loko mahine search kare) ane difficulty (rank karvu ketlu hard) be jovo padse. Steps

  1. Seed keywords lakho: business sathe related 10-15 words/topics.
  2. Tool ma nakho: Google Keyword Planner (free), Ubersuggest, Ahrefs free tools, AnswerThePublic. Semrush hoy to best.
  3. Volume
  • Difficulty note karo: High volume (vadhu search) + Low KD (Keyword Difficulty, ochi) = best target.
  1. Long-tail prefer karo: 3+ words vala keywords (da. "eggless cake delivery surat") - volume ochu pan rank easy ane buyer intent high.
  2. Intent check karo: keyword informational (blog mate) chhe ke transactional (product/service page mate)? Practical rule
  • Navi site: KD 0-30 vala keywords thi chalu karo
  • Volume 50-500/month pan chale jo intent strong hoy
  • Ek page = ek primary keyword + 3-5 secondary keywords Example "cake" (volume bahu high, KD high - skip karo). "eggless chocolate cake surat" (volume 200, KD low - target karo!). Checklist
  • 20-30 keywords ni list volume + KD sathe ready
  • Har keyword nu intent (info/buy) marked chhe
  • Easy long-tail keywords pehla target ma chhe [Added] Nava page mate full 7-step process ane 5 final checks: 02 Domain Research → 07 Keyword Research Guide (Nava Page mate).
