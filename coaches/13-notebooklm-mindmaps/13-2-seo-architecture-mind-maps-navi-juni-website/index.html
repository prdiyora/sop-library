13.2.A 1. NAVI Website — New Site Flow (pehli vaar site thi sharuat)

  • Complete SEO Architecture for a New Website
  • Golden Rules & Core Principles
  • Data over Opinion: Decision-making via GSC and GA4
  • Intent over Focus: Prioritize user intent and business value
  • One Keyword Intent per URL: Check Master Keyword Map
  • White-Hat Only: No paid links, PBNs, or copied content
  • Claim Labeling: Use CONFIRMED, LIKELY, or SPECULATION
  • Security: Store passwords in password managers, never in sheets
  • Phase 0: Business Goals & Domain Selection
  • Business Goal Understanding
  • Primary Goal: Sales, leads, traffic, or branding
  • Business Model: B2B vs B2C distinction
  • Quantified Success: Set 6-12 month target numbers
  • Target Customer & Location
  • Customer Persona: Umar, search terms, and pain points
  • Location Strategy: Local city, India-wide, or international
  • USP Discovery
  • Competitor Analysis: Review competitor homepage claims
  • Placement: Apply USP in H1, titles, and meta descriptions
  • Domain Name Finding Strategy
  • Naming Rules: Brand and keyword mix, easy to spell
  • Extensions: .in for India, .com for global markets
  • Phase 1: Research (Keyword, Competitor, Country)
  • Keyword Volume Research
  • Seed Keywords: 10-15 topic-related terms
  • Keyword Length: Head, body, and long-tail terms
  • Intent Classification: Informational, commercial, transactional
  • Master Keyword Map: Central tracking sheet format
  • Competitor Pool Analysis
  • Competitor Identification: Find consistent SERP leaders
  • Content & Gap Analysis: Identify missing keywords and gaps
  • Target Country Selection
  • Market Selection: Evaluate competition, budget, and shipping
  • Localization: Hreflang tags and currency adjustments
  • Phase 2: Strategy & 6-Month Plan
  • Strategy Formulation
  • Money Pages: Focus on high business value pages
  • Impact vs Effort: High impact and low effort first
  • Six-Month Execution Roadmap
  • Month 1: Audit and quick wins implementation
  • Month 2: On-page fixes and must-have pages
  • Month 3: Content engine and weekly blogging
  • Month 4: Off-page backlinks and social signals
  • Month 5: Content refresh and schema optimization
  • Month 6: Review results and adjust strategy
  • Phase 3: Design & Web Development
  • UI/UX Human Feel
  • Readability: Font 16px+, strong contrast, short paragraphs
  • Anti-AI Design: Real photos, testimonials, and brand voice
  • Mobile Friendly Design
  • Responsive Tests: Touch targets min 44px, no horizontal scroll
  • Mobile Speed: Pass Core Web Vitals and load under 3s
  • Theme, Motion & Consistency
  • Animation Rules: 300-500ms limit, consistent style
  • Visual Consistency: 1 primary color, max 2 fonts
  • Phase 4: Setup & Tracking
  • Google Search Console Setup
  • Domain Property: Verify via DNS
  • Sitemap Submission: Submit sitemap.xml to GSC
  • GA4 & GTM Setup
  • Enhanced Measurement: Auto-track scrolls and clicks
  • Key Events: Mark form submissions and purchases
  • Performance Views: Combine GA4 and GSC metrics
  • Microsoft Clarity
  • Behavioral Insights: Heatmaps and session recordings
  • Bing & Search Engines
  • Webmaster Tools: Import from GSC and verify
  • Phase 5: Technical SEO Foundation
  • Indexing & Crawlability
  • Robots.txt: Control crawl access and block admin paths
  • Indexing Check: Monitor GSC Pages index report
  • Canonical & Duplicate Content
  • Self-Canonical Tags: Point to preferred clean URL version
  • Redirects & Broken Links
  • Status Codes: Use 301 for permanent moves, fix 404s
  • Schema Markup
  • Structured Data: JSON-LD for Organization, Breadcrumb, Product
  • Phase 6: On-Page SEO Page by Page
  • EEAT Implementation
  • Trust Markers: Author bios, secure HTTPS, contact info
  • Internal Linking
  • Contextual Links: 3-5 internal links per page with varied anchors
  • Page Speed & Core Web Vitals
  • Optimization: WebP images, caching, LCP under 2.5s
  • Must-Have Pages
  • Essential Pages: About Us, Contact Us, Privacy Policy
  • Slug & Meta Optimization
  • Slug Structure: Short, lowercase, keyword-rich
  • Titles and Headings: 50-60 char titles, clean H1-H6 hierarchy
  • AEO & GEO Content
  • AI Search Readiness: Direct answers first, FAQ sections
  • Phase 7: Ecommerce SEO (If Shop)
  • Category & Collection Page SEO
  • Collection Optimization: Transactional keywords, short intro text
  • Product Page SEO
  • Product Elements: Unique descriptions, real images, product schema
  • Filters & Variants Management
  • Crawl Budget Control: Canonicalize filters, avoid index bloat
  • Out-of-Stock Handling
  • Stock Policy: Keep temporary out-of-stock live, 301 discontinued items
  • Phase 8: Off-Page SEO
  • Backlink Submission
  • Quality Focus: Relevant sites, natural anchor text mix
  • Local SEO & Listings
  • Google Business Profile: Complete profile, NAP consistency
  • Global Business Submission
  • Entity Building: Submit to verified international directories
  • Social Media & Video
  • Platform Presence: Distribute content, embed YouTube videos
  • Phase 9: Tracking Sheet & Client Reporting System
  • Tracking Sheet (Report 1)
  • Daily Metrics: GSC clicks, impressions, position updates
  • Live Dashboards (Report 2)
  • Vercel GSC Dashboard: Automated daily snapshot checks
  • Weekly Live Report (Report 3)
  • Action Plan: Weekly review of rewrites and fixes
  • Visual Reporting: GA4 and GSC data combined for clients
  • Phase 10: Ongoing Maintenance
  • Routine Audits
  • Weekly Checks: GSC trends and indexing errors
  • Monthly Crawls: Screaming Frog for 4xx and broken links
  • Quarterly Reviews: Content audits, refresh, merge, or remove
  • Traffic Drop Diagnosis
  • Investigation Steps: Check GA4 tracking, seasonality, and Google updates

13.2.B 2. JUNI Website — Existing Site Flow (client ni chalu site)

  • Complete SEO Architecture for Existing Websites
  • Senior Golden Rules & Architecture Foundations
  • Data over Opinion: GSC/GA4 for decisions, Semrush/Moz estimates
  • Intent over Value: Search intent and business value before volume
  • One Keyword-Intent per URL: Check Master Keyword Map first
  • White-Hat Only: No paid links, PBNs, or copied content
  • Claim Labels: CONFIRMED, LIKELY, and SPECULATION distinctions
  • Security: Passwords and tokens stored in password managers only
  • Step 1: Onboarding, Access & Baseline Snapshot
  • Client Info & Access: GSC, GA4, GTM, CMS, and tool collection
  • Baseline Snapshot: Recording clicks, impressions, sessions, and rankings
  • Previous SEO History: Reviewing penalties, drops, and redesigns
  • Step 2: Setup & Tracking Verification
  • Google Search Console: Domain property and sitemap submission
  • GA4 & GTM Setup: Conversion key events and enhanced measurement
  • Auxiliary Tools: Microsoft Clarity heatmaps and Bing Webmaster
  • Tracking Sheet: Copying Report 1 daily and monthly templates
  • Step 3: Technical SEO Audit & Fixes
  • Screaming Frog Crawl: Comprehensive site crawl and issue logging
  • Indexing Check: Resolving GSC page indexing and excluded reasons
  • Redirects & Broken Links: Fixing 404s, chains, and loops
  • Schema Markup: Implementing valid JSON-LD structured data
  • Step 4: Keyword & Competitor Research
  • Keyword Volume Research: Semrush target country database analysis
  • Competitor Analysis: Studying content gaps, rankings, and backlink pools
  • Quick Win Finding: Identifying striking distance position 5-20 pages
  • Step 5: Strategy & Optimization
  • Title & Description Quick Wins: Improving CTR on top 4-10 pages
  • Content Improvement: Enhancing depth and structure on 10-20 position pages
  • Keyword & Content Gap Analysis: Building 3-6 month content roadmaps
  • Monthly SEO Plan: Prioritizing high-impact tasks and off-page efforts
  • Step 6: On-Page & Ecommerce Execution
  • EEAT Implementation: Author bios, transparency, and trust signals
  • Internal Linking: Adding 3-5 contextual links per page
  • AEO & GEO Optimization: Direct answers and AI search visibility
  • Ecommerce SEO: Category collections, product descriptions, and filters
  • Step 7: Off-Page SEO & Authority
  • Backlink Submission: Quality outreach and competitor gap analysis
  • Local & Global Listings: GBP verification and NAP consistency
  • Social & Community: Reddit, Quora, and social channel integration
  • DA/PA & Health Monitoring: Tracking unique linking root domains
  • Step 8: Client Reporting System
  • Report 1: Daily and monthly tracking sheets
  • Report 2: Vercel automated click-impression-goal dashboard
  • Report 3: Weekly live SEO report and update plan
  • Step 9: Maintenance & Content Refresh
  • Traffic Drop Diagnosis: Investigating GSC and GA4 anomalies
  • Quarterly Content Audit: Decision rules to keep, update, merge, or remove
  • Routine Maintenance: Weekly, monthly, and quarterly SEO checklists
