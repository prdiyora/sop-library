13.4.B CHATGPT BUSINESS HUB – COMPLETE SOP

Indian Business & US Business – Step-by-Step Gujlish Guide

Purpose: Business Claim karvo, Website Information manage karvi, Product Feed connect karvo, Website Clicks ane Mentions track karva tatha eligible business mate AI Business Agent publish karvo.

SOP 1: INDIAN BUSINESS – BUSINESS HUB SETUP

Applicable for: India ma registered company athva India mathi operate thato eligible online business.

Goal: ChatGPT par Business Profile claim kari ne Business Information, Website Clicks ane Mentions manage karva.

STEP 1 – Business Hub open karo

Browser ma https://chatgpt.com/business-hub open karo.

Tamara ChatGPT account thi login karo.

Business Hub ma jao.

Search ma tamari Company / Brand nu naam nakho.

STEP 2 – Business Profile search karo

Tamari website ane brand name pramane listing search karo.

Jo tamari listing dekhay to tene select karo.

Jo listing na male to Add Business no option dekhay to teno upyog karo.

Confirm karo ke tame aa business na owner athva authorized representative chho.

Important: Local Google Maps listing hovi darek online business mate jaruri nathi.

STEP 3 – Business Verification karo

ChatGPT tamne available verification options batavshe.

Possible options:

Business Email Verification

Business Phone Verification

Website Domain Verification

Shopify Store Verification

Je option uplabdh hoy te pasand karo.

Website Domain Verification:

ChatGPT mathi DNS TXT Record copy karo.

Tamara domain provider ma login karo.

DNS Management ma jao.

TXT Record add karo.

ChatGPT ma pachha jai Verify Domain karo.

Shopify Verification:

Shopify Store option pasand karo.

Connect Shopify par click karo.

Shopify Admin ma login karo.

Permissions review karo.

Authorize kari ne verification complete karo.

STEP 4 – OpenAI Approval ni raah juo

OpenAI tamara Business Claim ne review karshe.

Approval athva additional information mate email aavshe.

Approval male pachhi Business Hub ma tamaro Business Profile access kari shaksho.

STEP 5 – Persona Identity Verification

Aa khub mahatvano step chhe.

Business Profile open karo.

Complete Verification with Persona pasand karo.

Continue to Persona par click karo.

Government ID aapo.

Business Registration Details aapo.

Tame business represent karva authorized chho teno puravo aapo.

Verification submit karo.

Approval malya pachhi tamaro profile verified thai shake chhe ane blue checkmark dekhai shake chhe.

STEP 6 – Business Information update karo

Business Profile ma nicheni details update karo:

Business Name

Official Website URL

Business Description

Business Category

Logo

Cover Image

Shipping / Delivery Policy

Return & Refund Policy

Customer Support Information

Badhi information sachi ane website sathe consistent rakhvi.

STEP 7 – Product Feed connect karo

Jo tamari pase Ecommerce Website chhe to:

Product Feed section open karo.

Shopify Feed athva uplabdh Feed Option pasand karo.

Products ni information connect karo.

Product Name, Price, Availability ane Product URL check karo.

Feed submit karo.

STEP 8 – Insights check karo

Business Hub ma Insights section open karo.

Ahin tamne nicheni information mali shake chhe:

Business Mentions

Website Clicks

Discovery Topics

Insights ma aggregate data male chhe. Individual customer conversations athva prompts malta nathi.

Reporting samanya rite chhella 28 divas ni activity batave chhe. Jaruri activity threshold puro na thayo hoy to ketlik metrics blank hoi shake chhe.

INDIAN BUSINESS mate FINAL RESULT

Tamne eligible approval malya pachhi:

Business Profile manage kari shaksho.

Business Information update kari shaksho.

Product Feed connect kari shaksho.

Website Clicks track kari shaksho.

Business Mentions track kari shaksho.

Discovery Topics joi shaksho.

Business Agent: Indian-based business mate hal US Business Agent eligibility lagu padti nathi. US customers ne target karvathi aa restriction dur thati nathi.

SOP 2: US BUSINESS – BUSINESS HUB + BUSINESS AGENT SETUP

Applicable for: US ma registered ane eligible Ecommerce / Online Service Business.

Example: Tamari US registered company + Shopify Store + USA Target Customers.

Goal: ChatGPT par Business Claim karvo ane AI Business Agent banavi ne Publish karvo.

STEP 1 – Documents ane Information taiyar karo

Business Hub chalu karta pehla aa information taiyar rakho:

US Registered Company Name

Official Business Address

Business Registration Documents

Official Website URL

Business Email

Business Phone

Shopify Store Admin Access

Domain Access

Government-issued ID

Business Ownership / Authorization Proof

Dhyan rakho ke badha details actual business records sathe match thava joie.

STEP 2 – ChatGPT Business Hub open karo

https://chatgpt.com/business-hub open karo.

ChatGPT account thi login karo.

Business Hub ma jao.

Search ma tamari US Company nu naam nakho.

Existing Profile hoy to select karo.

Profile na male ane Add Business option male to teno upyog karo.

STEP 3 – Shopify Store connect karo

Tamara Shopify Store mate:

Shopify Store Verification pasand karo.

Connect Shopify par click karo.

Shopify Admin ma login karo.

Correct store select karo.

ChatGPT ne aapvama aavti Permissions review karo.

Authorize karo.

Verification Process complete karo.

Shopify Catalog mathi eligible product information, pricing ane availability share thai shake chhe.

Important: Shopify connect karvathi Business Agent apmele live thato nathi.

STEP 4 – Business Claim Approval

OpenAI Business Claim review karshe.

Email dwara Approval athva additional information mangvama aavi shake chhe.

Approval malya pachhi Business Profile access karo.

STEP 5 – Persona Identity Verification

Business Hub ma Business Profile open karo.

Verify with Persona pasand karo.

Continue to Persona par click karo.

Government ID submit karo.

US Company Registration Details submit karo.

Business Owner athva Authorized Representative hovano puravo aapo.

Verification submit karo.

OpenAI tarafthi Approval malvani raah juo.

Note: Tame India mathi authorized representative tarike kaam karta ho to tamari actual identity ane company sathe no sambandh sacho darshavvo.

STEP 6 – Business Profile complete karo

Tamari US Website pramane nicheni details update karo:

Brand Name

Website URL

Business Description

Business Category

Logo

Product Information

Shipping Policy

Return Policy

Refund Policy

Customer Support Details

Badhi information US customers mate accurate hovi joie.

STEP 7 – Business Agent create karo

Persona Verification approved thaya pachhi:

Business Hub ma tamari US Business Profile open karo.

Business Agent section shodho.

Shopify Setup darmiyan Draft Agent banyo hoy to tene open karo.

Nahitar Create Agent option uplabdh hoy to tena dwara Agent banavo.

Jo Agent no option dekhato nathi to eligibility ane verification status fari check karo.

STEP 8 – Business Agent ne Information aapo

Agent ne nicheni information review / configure karo:

A. Business Information

Brand Introduction

About Us

Product Categories

Brand Guidelines

B. Product Information

Product Names

Product Descriptions

Product Prices

Ingredients

Product Benefits

Product Usage Instructions

Product Availability

C. Customer Support Information

Shipping Time

Shipping Charges

Return Policy

Refund Policy

Order Support Information

Frequently Asked Questions

D. Agent Guidelines

Agent kevi rite javab aapshe teni guidelines taiyar karo.

Example:

Customer ne helpful ane friendly javab aapvo.

Product Information sachi aapvi.

Ingredients vishe khota claims na karva.

Product Availability khoti na batavvi.

Customer ne yogya Product Page ni link aapvi.

Return ane Refund Policy pramane j javab aapvo.

Medical athva unsupported claims na karva.

STEP 9 – Business Agent testing karo

Agent publish karta pehla testing jaruri chhe.

Aa prashno puchhi ne check karo:

What is your best-selling product?

Which product is suitable for my needs?

What are the ingredients in this product?

How much does shipping cost?

How many days does delivery take?

What is your return policy?

Can I get a refund?

Where can I purchase this product?

Check karo ke:

Product Information correct chhe?

Pricing correct chhe?

Website Links correct chhe?

Policy Answers correct chhe?

Agent khota claims to nathi karto?

Koi javab khoto hoy to Information athva Agent Guidelines sudhari ne fari test karo.

STEP 10 – Business Agent publish karo

Testing complete thaya pachhi:

Business Agent Preview open karo.

Badha answers final review karo.

Publish button par click karo.

Publish confirmation check karo.

Customer Experience test karo.

Have customers tamari Business Profile mathi Business Agent sathe chat chalu kari shaksho.

Business Agent customers ne products samjavi shake chhe, comparisons karvama madad kari shake chhe ane Website taraf mokli shake chhe.

Hal ma Agent potani rite checkout athva payment complete karto nathi. Customer tamari Website par jai ne Purchase complete kare chhe.

STEP 11 – Business Hub ma Regular Management karo

Dar athvadiye nicheni vastuo check karo:

Product Prices

Product Availability

New Product Updates

Website Links

Shipping Policy

Return Policy

Agent Responses

Customer Questions

Jo Product athva Policy badlay to Agent mate ni information pan update karo.

US BUSINESS mate FINAL RESULT

Tamne jaruri eligibility ane approval male to:

Business Profile verified thashe.

Product Catalog connect thashe.

Business Agent create kari shaksho.

Agent test kari shaksho.

Agent publish kari shaksho.

Customers Agent sathe chat kari shaksho.

Insights ma Mentions ane Website Clicks joi shaksho.
