🧰 13.1 — Semrush Rankings 2.0 & AI Visibility Research

Dabbo 13 · Semrush Tools · ✅ Done

13.1.A Aa stop su chhe

Semrush nu navu Rankings 2.0 ane AI Visibility toolkit - AI search (ChatGPT, AI Overviews) ma brand kevo dekhay chhe ane competitors ni compare ma kevu. AI era ma ranking fakt Google nathi. Real run: Client A, 19 Nov 2026.

13.1.B SEMrush Organic Rankings 2.0

Step-by-step: measure topic visibility across Google search and AI answers

1. Open the exact tool

This tab covers Organic Rankings 2.0, the label currently shown under Competitive Analysis. It is not Position Tracking or the legacy Organic Rankings report.

Open SEO → Organic Rankings 2.0, enter a domain and click Get started/Analyze. Working example: client-a-store.com.

Real run on 19 Nov 2026 (US, month October 2026): Topics 133 (-9), Organic Traffic 224 (+489.5%), AI-Influenced Traffic 0, Keywords 689 (-6.8%), Mentions 15 (+50%), Citations 85 (-14.1%). Largest topics by keyword count: Coatings & Adhesives (18.3M total volume, 66 keywords), Construction & Power Tools (72.2M, 64), Sanders & Grinders (7M, 49), Granite Countertops (4.6M, 42).

2. Fix country and month

Select the US or UK database currently supported by the report and select the reporting month. Use the same month and country for competitor comparisons.

Record the month shown, not just the date you opened the tool. In the checked interface the report offered US/UK and a month selector. Let loading indicators finish before recording any figures.

3. Record the domain summary

Capture Topics, Organic Traffic, AI-Influenced Traffic, Keywords, Mentions and Citations, including displayed changes.

Organic Traffic is estimated Google organic traffic. AI-Influenced Traffic is a modeled estimate of visits from any channel influenced by AI mentions, not a GA4 count of direct ChatGPT referrals. Do not add it to organic traffic as if the two were disjoint totals.

4. Review every relevant topic

Use Filter by topic and All/Branded/Non-Branded. Sort by the metric relevant to the task. Record topic name, Total Volume, Organic Traffic, AI-Influenced Traffic, AI Visibility, Keywords, Mentions, Citations and Intent.

For Client A, search for relevant cutting-tool or stone-care topics if the report contains them. Do not invent a topic when the domain has no data.

5. Open topic details

Click a relevant topic. Review Main Metrics and AI Visibility, and compare 6M/1Y trends where available.

Inspect platform-specific visibility for ChatGPT, AI Overview, AI Mode and Gemini where reported. Keep a brand mention separate from a citation of the website. One can happen without the other.

6. Inspect keywords and prompts

Select relevant Subtopics. In Keywords, use position, KD, volume and intent filters. In Prompts, inspect Mentioned/Cited, New/Lost and AI platform filters.

Example: a relevant topic with Google rankings but few citations may need useful original reference material. Read the actual cited pages and responses before proposing a fix. An AI answer is research evidence, not a source for unverified product claims.

7. Compare and save

Repeat the same topic with a validated competitor using the same database and month. Record both sets of metrics and the visible gap.

Export keywords/prompts to CSV using Export above the relevant table. Tracker: Topic | Subtopic | Domain | Country/month | Organic estimate | AI-influenced estimate | Mentions | Citations | Evidence URL | Proposed page/action | Status.

8. Final quality check

The official manual explains that the new organic traffic model differs from legacy Organic Rankings. Treat the two as separate baselines.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Topic and domain are relevant to the real business.
  • Same country, month and tool version used in comparisons.
  • Mentions, citations and modeled traffic are not confused.
  • Empty/loading/error states were not recorded as zeros.
  • Output: topic-level visibility comparison and evidence-based content actions.

Additional Step — Prompt Research: Related Topics & High-Volume Keywords

Aa process thi apdi website na products/services sathe relevant ane overall high-search-volume keywords mathi vadhu topic ane prompt opportunities identify karvani chhe. Aa workflow Topic Opportunities research ne complement kare chhe; banne sections ma malela relevant topics cover karvana.

Step 1 — Home mathi AI Visibility open karo

Tool/dashboard na Home page par jao.

AI Visibility section open karo.

Current project ni apdi website/domain select karo.

Project mate je target country use karo chho te country select karo. Specific country na hoy to project ni existing process pramane Worldwide use karo.

Step 2 — Prompt Research ma Related Topics select karo

AI Visibility ni andar “Prompt Research” section open karo.

“Related Topics” option/tab select karo.

Ahiya apdi website ane overall high-volume keyword themes sathe related topics ni list review karo.

Darek topic nu relevance check karo: topic apda products, services, audience ane target market sathe sambandhit hovo joie.

Relevant topics select/list down karo. Irrelevant topics ne content opportunity tarike select na karo.

Step 3 — Topic open kari dropdown mathi badha prompts check karo

Related Topics ni list mathi ek relevant topic open karo.

Topic ni details ma dropdown control open karo.

Dropdown ma aa topic sathe related jetla prompts available hoy te badha display thase.

Darek prompt ne ek-ek kari review karo; fakt pehlo prompt lai ne aagal na vadhvu.

Topic ni andar available badha relevant prompts ni list spreadsheet/document ma note karo.

Step 4 — Darek prompt nu full AI response open karo

Dropdown mathi ek prompt select karo.

Prompt sathe show thato AI response/preview check karo.

“View Full Response” par click karo.

Complete AI response open thaya pachhi prompt ane response banne capture karo.

Aa j process topic na darek prompt mate repeat karo.

Ek topic ma multiple prompts hoy to darek prompt nu alag record banao; ek response ne bija prompt sathe mix na karo.

Step 5 — Prompt ane full response mathi content brief taiyar karo

Prompt ne exact wording sathe save karo.

Full AI response nu complete content save karo — headings, paragraphs, bullet points, product/brand recommendations, comparison points ane useful details chhodva nahi.

Response ma aavela important subtopics, user questions, entities/brands ane content gaps alag note karo.

Same prompt ane ena response ne ek j record/page brief ma link karo, jethi content banavti vakhte source context clear rahe.

Prompt + full response ne research input tarike use kari apdi website mate complete, original ane helpful content taiyar karo.

AI response ne word-for-word copy/paste na karo. Facts verify karo, competitor wording copy na karo, ane apdi brand/product information pramane content lakhvo.

Step 6 — Topic ane prompt coverage track karo

Darek topic mate total available prompts ane review karela prompts ni sankhya note karo.

Darek prompt mate prompt text, AI platform/country (jo dashboard ma dekhata hoy), full response, brands ane sources save karo.

Content page banavyo hoy to teno URL ane publication date note karo.

Badha relevant topics ane topic ni andar na badha relevant prompts cover na thay tya sudhi process repeat karo.

Expected output: Related Topics mathi relevant topic list, darek topic ni andar na badha available prompts, darek prompt no complete AI response, ane darek prompt/response par aadharit apdi website mate content brief athva complete content page.

Dermatologists and pediatricians agree that teenage skin is young, resilient, and does not need complex, multi-step adult routines. According to the American Academy of Pediatrics via HealthyChildren.org, harsh viral trends involving retinoids, heavy acids, or 10-step regimens can damage the skin barrier and trigger irritation.

The Ideal 3-Step Routine for Teens

1. Gentle Cleanser: Wash the face twice a day with a mild, non-comedogenic (pore-friendly) cleanser from brands like CeraVe, La Roche-Posay, or Bubble. If dealing with mild breakouts, a low-strength salicylic acid cleanser (0.5% to 2%) can help clear pores.

2. Lightweight Moisturizer: Hydrate morning and night with an oil-free, fragrance-free moisturizer to keep the skin barrier balanced without clogging pores.

3. Broad-Spectrum SPF 30+ (Morning): Apply a daily sunscreen to protect developing skin from UV damage and prevent dark post-acne marks.
