🧰 13.3 — Semrush Brand Performance - Share of Voice & Sentiment

Dabbo 13 · Semrush Tools · ✅ Done

13.3.A Aa stop su chhe

AI platforms par aapni brand vishe ketli vaat thay chhe ane loko ne brand kevi lage chhe, e check karvanu.

  • Share of Voice: AI answers ma aapni brand nu naam ketli vaar aave chhe.

  • Positive (Favorable): Brand vishe saari vaat thay chhe.

  • Negative: Brand vishe kharab vaat thay chhe.

  • Neutral (General): Brand vishe normal vaat thay chhe.

Aa data thi khabar pade ke AI search ma aapni brand ni reputation kevi chhe ane kya sudharo karvo joie.

Real example: Client A, 19 Nov 2026.

13.3.B Semrush Brand Performance — 4 reports (step-by-step)

Semrush ma aa 4 reports check karvana chhe:

ReportPathSu jovanu
Brand Performance/ai-seo/brand-performance/AI ma brand ketli vaar mention thay chhe
Perception/ai-seo/perception/AI brand vishe saaru ke kharab su kahe chhe
Narrative Drivers/ai-seo/narrative-drivers/Brand kem mention thay chhe ane kaya sources use thay chhe
Questions/ai-seo/questions/Loko AI ne brand ane products vishe su puchhe chhe

Goal: ChatGPT jeva AI tools aapni brand vishe su kahe chhe, e samajvu.

Step 1: Brand profile taiyar karo

Pehla client na Project Brief ma aa 3 details confirm karo:

  • Domain: Client ni website.

  • Target country: Kaya desh mate report joie.

  • Language: Kayi language ma report joie.

Brand Performance mate darek domain nu alag brand profile joie. Semrush account ma profiles ni limit hoy chhe, etle jarur hoy tyare j profile banao.

Step 2: Report kholo ane profile set karo

Aa rite jao:

Jupiter → Semrush (Server 2) → AI → AI Visibility → Brand Performance

Path: /ai-seo/brand-performance/

  1. Website nu domain nakho.

  2. Analyze click karo.

  3. Set up new brand profile kholo.

  4. Domain confirm karo.

  5. Target location ma United States select karo.

  6. Target language ma English select karo.

  7. Get started click karo.

Profile ready thay pachhi AI Visibility menu ma bija 3 reports pan kholi shakay:

  • Perception

  • Narrative Drivers

  • Questions

Aa badha reports ek j brand profile no data use kare chhe.

Step 3: Brand Performance overview vacho

Report khule pachhi pehla Semrush e select karela competitors check karo.

Pachhi aa 3 vastu juo:

  1. Share of Voice: Brand ketli vaar AI answers ma aave chhe.

  2. Overall Sentiment: Brand vishe kevi vaat thay chhe.

  3. Key Business Drivers: Brand kaya kaaran thi mention thay chhe.

Sathe platform ane data date pan note karo.

Step 6: Perception — sentiment ane ena reasons vacho

AI Visibility ma Perception report kholo.

Aa 4 section check karo:

  1. Competitive Perception by Platform: Alag AI platforms par brand ne kevi rite jovama aave chhe.

  2. Favorable Sentiment ranking: Kayi brand vishe vadhu saari vaat thay chhe.

  3. Key Sentiment Drivers: Brand ni strengths ane improvement areas.

  4. Sentiment by Feature Category: Pricing, quality, service jeva topics par sentiment.

Step 7: Narrative Drivers — Share of Voice ane sources vacho

  1. Share of Voice by Platform: Kaya AI platform ma brand ketli mention thay chhe.

  2. Mentions: Brand ketli vaar mention thay chhe.

  3. Average Position: AI answers ma brand ni average position.

  4. Top Domains by Citations: AI kaya websites ne source tarike use kare chhe.

Step 8: Questions — loko su puchhe chhe e vacho

Questions report kholo.

Aa 3 vastu check karo:

  1. Topic Distribution: Questions kaya topics par chhe.

  2. Query Intent Distribution: Loko kaya purpose thi question puchhe chhe.

  3. Questions by Topic: Darek topic ma actual questions kaya chhe.

Real run — Query Intent

IntentShare
Comparison46%
Research46%
Support8%
Education1%

Aa report ma mota bhag na questions comparison ane research related hata. Percentages report ma batavya pramane j record karo.

Main topics:

  • Supplier discovery

  • Brand reputation and vendor comparison

  • Logistics and pricing

  • Tooling performance (blades, pads, CNC)

  • Support and training

  • Chemicals and sealers

Sample Questions:

  1. "Where do most stone fabrication shops buy their diamond blades and polishing pads?"

  2. "Who offers the best balance of price and durability for diamond blades used in stone fabrication?"

  3. "Which brands offer polishing pads that deliver a high gloss on granite but won't burn quartz?"

Aa questions thi khabar pade chhe ke loko ne shu janvu chhe.

Example: Jo loko diamond blade ni price ane durability compare kare chhe, to aapde ena mate useful comparison guide banavi shakiye.

Aa questions ne future blog, FAQ, product guide ane category content mate use karo.
