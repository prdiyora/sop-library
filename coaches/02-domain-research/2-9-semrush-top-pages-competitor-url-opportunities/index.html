🌐 2.9 — Semrush Top Pages - Competitor URL Opportunities

Dabbo 02 · Domain Research & Selection · ✅ Done

2.9.A Aa stop su chhe

Competitor na sabthi vadhare traffic lavta pages kayo chhe - e joiye to khabar pade ke aapde kaya type na page banavvana. Traffic share, keywords count ane backlinks joi ne opportunity pages shortlist karo. Real run: Client A, 19 Nov 2026.

2.9.B SEMrush Top Pages

Step-by-step: find the URLs driving Google and AI visibility

1. Choose the correct Top Pages

Open SEO → Competitive Analysis → Top Pages. This is the SEO Top Pages report, not Traffic & Market → Top Pages, which uses a different dataset and traffic metrics.

Enter the domain, subdomain or subfolder required by the task. Working example: client-a-store.com. Select the correct country before Search; the checked screen retained UK by default.

Real run on 19 Nov 2026 (US, Nov 18 2026): Organic Traffic 205 (-28.6%), Organic Pages 268 (+0.75%), Cited Pages 36 (+24.1%), 296 URLs listed. Top URL: /collections/porcelain-sinks with 52 estimated visits (25.4% share), 45 keywords, 1 LLM prompt, 3 referring domains, top keyword 'porcelain kitchen sinks' (commercial intent). Next: a Distar granite diamond blade product page with 29 visits from 'cutting granite' (informational), and the blog /kitchen-sink-cabinet-combo-buying-guide with 21 visits, marked new.

2. Set consistent report settings

Record domain/scope, selected database and month. For a competitor comparison, use identical settings.

Wait until the table is populated or an explicit no-data state appears. A blank or loading area is not evidence that the website has no pages.

3. Capture the URL-level metrics

For each relevant URL record Traffic, Traffic difference, Traffic share, Keyword count, Main keyword, Referring domains, LLM prompts and Search intent where displayed.

Keep traffic estimates and prompt-related metrics in separate columns. A URL with many prompts is not proven to produce sales.

4. Filter by source and page type

Use Sources to inspect Organic, AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT and Gemini separately or in combination.

Use URL search/Advanced filters to focus on relevant product, collection or guide sections. Use Top Keyword filters to remove branded queries or examine commercial/transactional intent where offered.

5. Review winners and declines

Sort by Traffic to find large pages, Traffic difference to spot changes, and LLM prompts to find AI-visible references. Review the trend for organic pages, organic traffic and cited pages.

A decline needs investigation. Check date range, redirects, indexing, seasonality and page changes before blaming content quality.

6. Inspect useful competitor pages

Open the top relevant competitor URLs. Record page type, title, headings, useful information, product details, evidence, FAQs and internal links. Review the ranking intent, not just the design.

Example: if a relevant diamond-blade collection leads, note its filters, specification guidance and related guides. Create an original plan using Client A's verified products, not copied text or unsupported claims.

7. Build the page opportunity tracker

Tracker: Research date | Country/month | Domain | Exact URL | Page type | Main keyword | Estimated traffic/change | Keywords | Referring domains | LLM prompts | Sources | Gap | Our existing/planned URL | Action/status.

Match opportunities to an existing page first. Create a new page only when the intent is genuinely different. Save exports as PDF, Excel or CSV where available.

8. Final quality check

Do not report competitor traffic as actual analytics or assume AI visibility means direct referral traffic.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • SEO Top Pages selected, not the similarly named Traffic & Market dashboard.
  • Every shortlisted URL opened and checked for relevance.
  • Country, month, scope and selected sources logged.
  • Our content uses real products and original explanations.
  • Output: ranked page opportunities and an update/new-page decision for each.

2.9.C Expected Output

Output: competitor na top pages ni list, je page aapde banavvo/improve karvo e sathe mark.
