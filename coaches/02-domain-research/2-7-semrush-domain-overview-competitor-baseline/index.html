🌐 2.7 — Semrush Domain Overview - Competitor Baseline

Dabbo 02 · Domain Research & Selection · ✅ Done

2.7.A Aa stop su chhe

Semrush ma client ke competitor nu domain nakho etle e no full health card male - authority, traffic estimate, keywords, backlinks, ane Semrush Rank. Aa j baseline numbers chhe, je pachhi progress compare karva mate vaprashe. Real run: Client A (diamond blades, US), 19 Nov 2026 - numbers doc ma jeva teva j rakhela chhe.

2.7.B SEMrush Domain Overview

Step-by-step: establish the domain baseline before choosing competitors or keywords

1. Prepare the analysis

Confirm the website, target country, device and reporting month with the project brief. Use the same settings for every competitor.

Working example, real run on 19 Nov 2026: client-a-store.com, Root Domain, United States database, desktop, report date Nov 18 2026. Observed results: Authority Score 8 (Low authority), Organic Traffic 205 (-29%), Organic Keywords 725 (+5.2%), Paid Traffic 0, Paid Keywords 0, Backlinks 6.5K, Referring Domains 1.7K. AI Visibility showed 14 Mentions and 9 Cited Pages. Google SERP positions distribution: Organic 94.3%, AI Overviews 5%, other features 0.7%.

2. Open the correct report

Open Jupiter → Semrush → SEO → Competitive Analysis → Domain Overview. Enter the domain without an unrelated page path. Select Root Domain; use Subdomain, Subfolder or Exact URL only when that narrower scope is intended.

Choose the target database and click Search/Analyze. The landing page may default to Worldwide or retain another person's database, so check it every time.

3. Record the headline metrics

Record the report date, database, device and scope, then Authority Score, estimated Organic Traffic, Organic Keywords, Paid Traffic, Paid Keywords, Backlinks and Referring Domains where displayed.

Keep AI Visibility, Mentions and Cited Pages in separate columns if present. Do not add unlike metrics together. A blank widget is unavailable data, not zero.

4. Read trends and intent

Review organic traffic and keyword trends over consistent periods. Note sudden changes and check report notes before assigning a cause.

Review country distribution, branded/non-branded traffic and keyword intent. A large global total does not prove that the site reaches the client's target country. Some widgets remain root-domain level even when a narrower scope is selected; read the widget note.

5. Open the detailed reports

Open Top Organic Keywords and Main Organic Competitors using View details. Save relevant keywords and competitor domains for validation.

Open Top Pages/Organic Rankings for page-level research and Backlinks for link research. Do not decide the whole content plan from the first five preview rows.

6. Work through an example

For Client A, check whether visible competitors and keywords concern stone tools, diamond blades, polishing pads or the actual product range. Reject unrelated similarly named stone businesses.

If the tool suggests a competitor, record it as Candidate until its website, products and target market have been checked. No competitor is approved only because it has a high Authority Score.

7. Save the baseline

Tracker columns: Research date | Domain | Scope | Database | Device | Report month | Authority Score | Organic traffic estimate | Organic keywords | Referring domains | Trend | Relevant competitor candidates | Next action.

Export the report where the current plan allows it. Save under the project's dated research folder. Keep unavailable fields marked Not available.

8. Final quality check

Authority Score is a Semrush metric, not a Google score. No score or traffic estimate guarantees rankings.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Correct domain, scope, country, device and month.
  • Estimates labelled as estimates, not GA4 users or GSC clicks.
  • Preview rows opened in full before selection.
  • Competitors validated for product and market relevance.
  • Output: one baseline record plus a researched competitor shortlist.

2.7.C Semrush Rank

Step-by-step: compare domain-level organic visibility within one country database

1. Understand the score before using it

Semrush Rank orders domains by estimated organic search traffic in Semrush's regional database. A lower rank number indicates greater organic visibility.

This is not a keyword's Google position, Authority Score, a quality grade or an Alexa rank. Use Position Tracking for an agreed keyword set and specific target location.

2. Open Semrush Rank

Open SEO → Extras → Semrush Rank. Choose the regional database and reporting month/date offered in the interface.

For Client A and its competitors, use the agreed target market consistently. Record the actual displayed country and month before comparing domains.

3. Review the rank table

Capture Rank, Domain, SE Keywords, SE Traffic and Costs, plus Paid Keywords/Paid Traffic where shown. Hover tooltips when column labels are shortened.

SE Keywords counts observed organic keywords in the database's Google top 100. SE Traffic is estimated monthly organic visits. Costs is the estimated paid-search equivalent, not actual spend or revenue.

4. Find relevant domains

Use available search/filter controls or the current table to locate the target. If it is not listed, record Not found in this database/report rather than assigning a rank of zero.

For a useful comparison, collect the target plus validated competitors from the same country/month. A huge general marketplace may provide context but is not automatically a direct business competitor.

5. Inspect Winners & Losers

Open Winners & Losers where available. Review changes in organic/paid keywords and traffic compared with the previous month. Sort changes to inspect gains and declines.

Check current values as well as deltas; small sites can show large percentage changes. A gain is a research lead, not proof that a particular SEO tactic caused it.

6. Open the detailed domain report

Click a domain to open Domain Overview. Validate relevance, country distribution, non-branded keywords and top pages.

Real run on 19 Nov 2026 (US database, Nov 18 2026): the Semrush Rank table holds 41,385,763 ranked domains, led by youtube.com, facebook.com, wikipedia.org and reddit.com. In the same day's US Compare Domains run, Semrush Rank showed detroitdiamondtools.com at 1.3M, taitsales.com at 1.4M and client-a-store.com at 2.8M. A lower number means more estimated organic visibility, so both validated competitors currently outrank Client A in this database.

7. Save a reproducible comparison

Export CSV/Excel if available. Tracker: Research date | Country database | Report month | Domain | Semrush Rank | SE keywords | SE traffic estimate | Estimated Costs | Monthly change | Relevance note | Next investigation.

Keep the original export and settings. Compare future periods with the same database and method instead of mixing regional ranks.

8. Final quality check

Semrush estimates depend on its keyword coverage. A low-traffic or local site may have useful real customers even when this report has little data.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Lower-is-better interpretation used correctly.
  • Rank separated from keyword positions and Authority Score.
  • Same database/month for every domain.
  • Missing domain recorded without inventing a number.
  • Output: a domain visibility benchmark and any relevant trend leads.

2.7.D Expected Output

Output: ek baseline record (tracker row) per domain - client + top competitors. Aa numbers pachhi Position Tracking ane reporting ma compare thashe.
