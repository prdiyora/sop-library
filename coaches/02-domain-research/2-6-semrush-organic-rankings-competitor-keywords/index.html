🌐 2.8 — Semrush Organic Rankings - Competitor Keywords & URLs

Dabbo 02 · Domain Research & Selection · ✅ Done

2.6.A Aa stop su chhe

Competitor kaya keywords par rank kare chhe ane kayu URL rank thay chhe - e jovanu. Ahiya thi j khabar pade ke aapne kaya keywords par content banavvu haji baki chhe. Real run: Client A competitor analysis, 19 Nov 2026.

2.6.B Semrush Organic Rankings (step-by-step)

Goal: ranking keywords, improve thay chhe e pages, ane lost organic opportunities shodhvi.

Step 1: Kholo ane scope set karo

SEO → Competitive Analysis → Organic Rankings kholo. Target domain nakho ane jarur pramane Root Domain, Subdomain, Subfolder ke Exact URL select karo.

Country database, desktop/mobile ane date select karo. Working example: client-a-store.com, agreed US database. Rows collect karta PEHLA aa settings note karo.

Real run 19 Nov 2026 (US database, desktop, Nov 18 2026): 725 keywords (+5.2%), estimated Traffic 205 (-28.6%), Traffic Cost $73 (-28.4%), Branded Traffic 0. Top keywords: 'cutting granite' position 10 (volume 480, 14.1% of traffic), 'porcelain kitchen sinks' 3 (720), 'ready made kitchen sink with cabinet' 2 (590), 'leveling square' 1 (110), 'best polish for granite countertops' 1 (110). Intent mix: Informational 46.3% of keywords, Commercial 37.4%, Transactional 9.3%, Navigational 7%.

Step 2: Pehla Overview vacho

Note karo: keyword count, estimated traffic, traffic cost ane branded/non-branded split (dekhay to). Traffic ane keyword trends, position distribution ane SERP features pan juo.

Traffic Cost = organic visibility nu paid-search value estimate. E client ni advertising bill ke revenue NATHI.

Step 3: Positions kholo ane full row collect karo

Positions tab kholo. Note karo: Keyword, Intent, Position, Previous position, Volume, KD %, Traffic %, ranking URL, SERP features ane Last Update (available hoy to).

Jarur pade to Manage columns vapro. Darek keyword sathe je URL jodailo chhe E juo — fakt domain nu aggregate score nahi.

Step 4: Practical improvements shodho

Positions 11–20 filter karo — e relevant pages chhe je page 1 ni najik chhe. 4–10 alag check karo — e pages ne intent coverage ke internal links sudhre to faydo thai shake. Aa working filters chhe, quick wins ni guarantee nathi.

Keyword include/exclude ane intent filters vapro. Client A mate diamond blade ke polishing pad terms thi chalu karo ane irrelevant locations, brands ke products je business supply nathi kartu e kadhi nakhо.

Step 5: Position Changes ane Pages juo

Position Changes kholo ane New, Improved, Declined ane Lost groups juo. Same date ranges j sarkhavo ane affected URLs kholo.

Pages kholo — kaya URLs organic estimates laave chhe ane darek sathe ketla keywords map thay e juo. Aapna ghana URLs same intent target kare to cannibalization flag karo (pages delete karva nahi, review mate).

Step 6: Live SERP ane competitors check karo

Shortlisted keyword bagde nu SERP snapshot kholo. Eni collection date ane top relevant competing page types note karo. Snapshot aaje no live personalized result nathi pan hotu.

Example: commercial diamond-blade query ma mostly collections aave to, appropriate collection sudharo — blog article ne force kari ne rank na karavo. Page select karta pehla product relevance check karo.

Step 7: Findings ne work list banao

Tracker columns: Date | Database/device | Keyword | Intent | Volume | KD | Current/previous position | Our ranking URL | Competitor reference | Issue | Action | Owner | Status.

Action examples: useful specifications expand karo, mismatched title fix karo, relevant internal link umero, ke navi intent-matched page taiyar karo. Fakt approved kaam implement karo, pachhi Position Tracking ane site na first-party tools ma measure karo.

Step 8: Export ane verify

Filtered rows CSV/XLSX ma export karo, ke available hoy to PDF reporting vapro. Filters note karo jethi export reproduce thai shake.

Legacy Organic Rankings ane Rankings 2.0 alag models vapre chhe; banne ni traffic series ek chart ma JOIN na karo.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Akho report jovo, fakt top keywords nahi.
  • Darek selected keyword nu saachu ranking URL ane market chhe.
  • Tool estimates GSC/GA4 thi alag rakhela chhe.
  • Output: prioritized URL-keyword action tracker, evidence ane verification date sathe.

2.6.C Expected Output

Output: competitor na ranking keywords ni filtered list + URL mapping (kayu keyword kaya page par).
