🌐 2.10 — Semrush Compare Domains - Benchmarking

Dabbo 02 · Domain Research & Selection · ✅ Done

2.10.A Aa stop su chhe

Client vs competitors side-by-side compare karva - keywords overlap, common keywords, missing keywords. Ahiya thi j "te logo par chhe ane aapni paase nathi" eva keywords ni yadi bane. Real run: Skyline Stone vs competitors, 19 Nov 2026.

2.10.B Semrush Compare Domains (step-by-step)

Goal: aapni site ni search baseline validated competitors sathe compare karvi.

Step 1: Comparison set taiyar karo

Pehla FIND RIGHT COMPETITORS SOP thi relevant organic competitors select karo. Similar market ane intent vala businesses/pages j compare karo.

Working example, real run 19 Nov 2026 (US, desktop, Nov 18 2026, Root Domain for all three): skylinestoneproducts.com vs taitsales.com and detroitdiamondtools.com — Semrush ni own Organic Competitors list mathi top two domains for our site (36 and 34 common keywords). Results in order (Skyline / Tait / Detroit): Authority Score 8 / 16 / 11; Semrush Rank 2.8M / 1.4M / 1.3M; Organic Traffic 205 / 739 / 816; Organic Keywords 725 / 1.2K / 1.9K; Referring Domains 1.7K / 2.5K / 8.4K. Traffic share among the three: 11.6% / 42% / 46.4%. Non-branded share: 100% / 90% / 51%. Top missing-keyword opportunities shown for Skyline Stone: diamond cup wheel (volume 1,300), stain proof premium impregnating sealer (480), stain proof sealer (390), concrete cup wheel (260), cup diamond wheel (260).

Step 2: Compare Domains kholo

SEO → Competitive Analysis → Compare Domains kholo. Aapnu website PEHLA nakho, pachhi selected competitor domains umero. Tool total 5 targets sudhi support kare chhe.

Darek target mate Root Domain, Subdomain, Subfolder ke URL — je yogya hoy e select karo. Akha international domain ni ek nani product page sathe comparison na karo — scope difference explain karya vagar nahi.

Step 3: Same market select karo

Regional database, device ane date/month consistent rakho. Landing page initially Worldwide batave chhe; project ek country target kare to e badlo.

Compare click karo ane result header ma badha entered targets verify karo. Plan restrictions aave to restriction note karo — paid upgrade ma switch karvu nahi.

Step 4: Headline metrics compare karo

Note karo: organic/paid traffic, keywords, Authority Score ane backlink/referring-domain metrics. Change periods ane definitions mate tooltips vacho.

Numbers scale ane direction samjva mate chhe — revenue, quality ke actual ad spend no proof NATHI. Same metric darek domain par compare karo.

Step 5: Comparison widgets vacho

Organic/paid traffic ane backlink trends juo. Traffic Share, Branded/Non-branded ane Paid/Organic widgets pan juo (dekhay to).

Traffic Share ahiya = compared targets ni vachche no share, akha market no nathi. Motu branded traffic = brand demand, easy non-branded SEO opportunity nathi.

Step 6: Broad comparison thi keywords par jao

Aapna domain mate Keyword Overlap ane Top Opportunities juo. Keyword-level Missing, Weak ane Untapped research mate Keyword Gap kholo.

Example: relevant blade keywords ma aapnu domain validated competitor thi pachhat hoy to — action select karta pehla e keywords, competitor URLs ane SERP intent check karo. Darek competitor keyword aapni website par hovo j joie evu nathi.

Step 7: Short comparison conclusion lakho

Tracker: Domain/scope | Country/device/month | Organic estimate | Organic keywords | Referring domains | Branded mix | Trend | Relevant strength | Gap | Next report/action.

Factual conclusion lakho — example: aa database ma kayo domain vadhu relevant non-branded visibility dharave chhe. Darek recommendation ne observed widget ke detailed report sathe link karo. Supported hoy to dated export ke report capture save karo.

Step 8: Final quality check

Shared-access availability official subscription description thi alag hoi shake. Feature current account ma khule tya sudhi kyarey promise na karo ke e included chhe.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Aapnu domain pehla nakhelu ane darek competitor validated.
  • Comparable scope, market, device ane date.
  • Traffic share denominator ane estimates explain thayela.
  • Keyword opportunities detailed report ma check thayela.
  • Output: ek comparison matrix + prioritized next investigations.

2.10.C Expected Output

Output: benchmark table (client vs competitors) + missing/weak keywords ni shortlist.
