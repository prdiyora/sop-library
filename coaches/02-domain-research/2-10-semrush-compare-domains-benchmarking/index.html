2.10.B SEMrush Compare Domains

Step-by-step: compare our site's search baseline against validated competitors

1. Prepare the comparison set

Use the existing FIND RIGHT COMPETITORS SOP to select relevant organic competitors first. Compare businesses/pages addressing a similar market and intent.

Working example, real run on 19 Nov 2026 (US, desktop, Nov 18 2026, Root Domain for all three): client-a-store.com vs taitsales.com and detroitdiamondtools.com, the top two domains from Semrush's own Organic Competitors list for our site (36 and 34 common keywords). Results in order (Client A / Tait / Detroit): Authority Score 8 / 16 / 11; Semrush Rank 2.8M / 1.4M / 1.3M; Organic Traffic 205 / 739 / 816; Organic Keywords 725 / 1.2K / 1.9K; Referring Domains 1.7K / 2.5K / 8.4K. Traffic share among the three: 11.6% / 42% / 46.4%. Non-branded share: 100% / 90% / 51%. Top missing-keyword opportunities shown for Client A: diamond cup wheel (volume 1,300), stain proof premium impregnating sealer (480), stain proof sealer (390), concrete cup wheel (260), cup diamond wheel (260).

2. Open Compare Domains

Open SEO → Competitive Analysis → Compare Domains. Enter our website first and add the selected competitor domains. The tool supports up to five targets in total.

Choose Root Domain, Subdomain, Subfolder or URL for each target as appropriate. Do not compare a whole international domain against one tiny product page without explaining that scope difference.

3. Select the same market

Set the regional database, device and available date/month consistently. The checked landing page initially showed Worldwide; change it when the project targets a single country.

Click Compare and verify all entered targets in the result header. If plan restrictions appear, record the restriction rather than switching to a paid upgrade.

4. Compare the headline metrics

Record the displayed organic/paid traffic, keywords, Authority Score and backlink/referring-domain metrics. Read tooltips for change periods and definitions.

Use the numbers to understand scale and direction, not as proof of revenue, quality or actual advertising spend. Compare the same metric across every domain.

5. Read the comparison widgets

Review organic/paid traffic and backlink trends. Check Traffic Share, Branded/Non-branded and Paid/Organic widgets where present.

Traffic Share here is the share among the compared targets, not necessarily the whole market. Large branded traffic may reflect brand demand rather than an easy non-branded SEO opportunity.

6. Move from broad comparison to keywords

Review Keyword Overlap and Top Opportunities for our domain. Open Keyword Gap for keyword-level Missing, Weak and Untapped research.

Example: if our domain trails a validated competitor on relevant blade keywords, inspect those keywords, the competitor URLs and the SERP intent before choosing an action. Do not assume every competitor keyword belongs on our website.

7. Write a short comparison conclusion

Tracker: Domain/scope | Country/device/month | Organic estimate | Organic keywords | Referring domains | Branded mix | Trend | Relevant strength | Gap | Next report/action.

Write a factual conclusion, such as which domain has more relevant non-branded visibility in this database. Link each recommendation to an observed widget or detailed report. Save a dated export or report capture where supported.

8. Final quality check

Shared-access availability can differ from the official subscription description. Never promise a feature is included until it opens in the current account.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Our domain entered first and every competitor validated.
  • Comparable scope, market, device and date.
  • Traffic share denominator and estimates explained.
  • Keyword opportunities checked in the detailed report.
  • Output: one comparison matrix plus prioritized next investigations.
