2.1.D HOW TO FIND THE RIGHT COMPETITORS (Semrush SOP - added 19 Nov 2026)

COMPETITOR RESEARCH SOP

A practical, step-by-step guide to discovering, validating, and organizing organic search competitors

Document purposeScope
Build an accurate competitor list for the selected target country and industry.Five methods: Domain Overview, Keyword-Based SERP Research, Keyword Gap, Position Tracking, and Manual Google SERP Research.

Important scope note: Traffic & Market Analysis, Advertising Research, and Backlink Gap have been excluded as requested. The remaining methods are renumbered in a clean sequence.

1. Objective and Research Approach

The objective is to identify websites that compete with our website in organic search results for the selected target country. The research should produce a verified list of relevant competitor domains, supported by keyword and visibility data.

Use the methods in the order below. Start with Domain Overview to build the base list, then use keyword and SERP research to discover additional domains. Finally, validate relevance and remove duplicates.

Method 1 — Domain Overview: identify the main organic competitors and shortlist 50 domains.

Method 2 — Keyword-Based Research: find domains ranking for our high-volume keywords.

Method 3 — Keyword Gap: find competitors ranking for relevant keywords that our website is missing or ranking weakly for.

Method 4 — Position Tracking: discover domains repeatedly appearing for our tracked target keywords.

Method 5 — Manual Google SERP Research: validate which websites actually appear in organic results for important searches.

2. Before Starting — Prepare the Research

Step 1 — Confirm the website and target market

Write down our exact website domain (example used in the original notes: skylinestoneproducts.com).

Confirm the target country for this research. Use the same country consistently across country-specific reports.

List the main products, services, and business categories offered by our website.

Prepare a spreadsheet to store raw exports, keyword data, SERP results, and the final competitor list.

Step 2 — Create the research spreadsheet

Keep original exports and working data. Do not overwrite raw research when preparing the final shortlist.

Tab nameWhat to store
Domain Overview ExportOriginal CSV export: domain, country, competitor metrics, traffic, keywords, and cost.
Final CompetitorsUnique competitor domains, source method, target country, relevance, and final status.
Keyword ResearchKeyword, volume, country volumes, KD, intent, CPC, trend, and our position where available.
SERP ResultsKeyword, SERP position, domain, ranking URL, include/exclude decision, and reason.
Method NotesResearch date, selected database/country, filters used, and observations.

3. Method 1 — Domain Overview

Purpose: use our domain's organic competitor report to identify the main websites competing with us in the selected market. This method creates the base competitor list.

Step 1 — Open Domain Overview and enter our domain

Start with our own website, not a competitor domain.

Open SEMrush and go to the “Domain Overview” section.

Enter our complete website domain in the domain search field.

Set the country/database selector to “Worldwide” for the initial overview, as specified in the original workflow.

Run the report and wait for the overview dashboard to load.

Expected output: Our website's Domain Overview report is displayed.

Step 2 — Open Main Organic Competitors

Find the “Main Organic Competitors” card on the overview page.

Click “View Details” inside that card.

Open the “Competitors” tab in the detailed report.

Review the competitor domains shown in the report.

Expected output: A detailed list of domains identified as organic competitors.

Step 3 — Select the target country

In the Competitors report, find the country/database selector.

Choose the target country for which competitor research is being conducted.

Allow the report to refresh for the selected country.

Review the domains and metrics shown for that market.

Expected output: A country-specific competitor list. Use this country consistently in the final research.

Step 4 — Export the complete competitor data

Review the available pages or result rows in the competitor report.

Export all available pages/results rather than exporting only the first visible page.

Choose CSV as the export format.

Open the downloaded CSV file in Excel or Google Sheets.

Keep an untouched copy of the original CSV in the “Domain Overview Export” tab or source file.

Expected output: A complete raw export that can be filtered without losing the original data.

Step 5 — Apply the five competitor filters

Use the five filters from the original research process. Apply them to the exported competitor data and review the strongest matching rows.

Apply “Top 10 Com. Level” and shortlist the top-performing rows under this filter.

Apply “Top 10 Common Keywords” and shortlist domains with strong keyword overlap.

Apply “Top 10 SE Keywords” and shortlist domains with strong organic keyword coverage.

Apply “Top 10 Traffic” and shortlist domains with the highest traffic values.

Apply “Top 10 Costs” and shortlist domains with the highest traffic-cost values.

Compare the shortlisted rows across all five filters. Remove duplicate domains from the working shortlist.

Select 50 competitors in total for the target country. Save these domains in a separate tab named “Final Competitors”.

Expected output: A finalized base list of 50 main organic competitors for the selected country.

Filter nameHow to use it
Top 10 Com. LevelShortlist the top rows shown by the Com. Level filter/metric in the export.
Top 10 Common KeywordsShortlist domains sharing the most common organic keywords with our website.
Top 10 SE KeywordsShortlist domains with the strongest organic keyword coverage under this filter.
Top 10 TrafficShortlist domains with the highest estimated organic traffic.
Top 10 CostsShortlist domains with the highest estimated traffic cost.

Note: Keep the filter labels as they appear in the SEMrush report/export. The original workflow specifies these five filters; it does not define a separate weighting formula for combining them.

4. Method 2 — Keyword-Based Competitor Research

Purpose: discover competitors from the actual search results for our important keywords. This can reveal relevant domains that may not appear in the Domain Overview competitor list.

Step 1 — Open Organic Rankings and add our website

Open the “Organic Rankings” section in SEMrush.

Enter our own website domain. Example from the original notes: skylinestoneproducts.com.

Open the “Positions” tab.

Confirm that the report is showing our website's organic keyword positions.

Expected output: A list of keywords for which our website ranks.

Step 2 — Sort and shortlist high-volume keywords

Find the “Volume” column in the Positions report.

Sort the Volume column from High to Low.

Review the keywords from the highest volume downward.

Select relevant keywords with a search volume of 500 or more.

Prioritize keywords that relate to our actual products/services and business.

Record each selected keyword in the “Keyword Research” tab.

Expected output: A shortlist of relevant keywords with 500+ search volume.

Step 3 — Open SERP Analysis for each keyword

Click a shortlisted keyword, one at a time.

In the keyword row, click the small screen/preview-style icon beside the keyword.

Wait for the popup to open.

Find the “SERP Analysis” section inside the popup.

Review the websites/domains shown in the search results.

Check the Top 10 SERP results and note relevant business websites as potential competitors.

Expected output: A keyword-by-keyword list of domains ranking in the Top 10 search results.

Step 4 — Exclude irrelevant platforms

Do not treat every SERP result as a business competitor. Exclude social platforms and marketplaces from the final business competitor list.

Do not add YouTube.

Do not add Instagram.

Do not add Facebook.

Do not add Flipkart.

Do not add Amazon.

Also exclude other social media platforms and marketplace/e-commerce platforms when they are not actual competing business websites.

Include relevant brand/business websites that offer competing products or services.

Add eligible domains to the “Final Competitors” tab and record the keyword that revealed each domain.

Expected output: A filtered list of actual business websites, rather than social or marketplace platforms.

Step 5 — Save keyword popup metrics

For every keyword reviewed, preserve the keyword and its metrics in the spreadsheet.

Record Global Volume.

Record the country-wise search volumes shown in the popup.

Record KD (Keyword Difficulty), SF (SERP Features), Intent, CPC, and Trend.

Do not copy example values as actual research data. Record the values displayed in the live report.

Expected output: A keyword research record with the available popup metrics.

5. Method 3 — Keyword Gap

Purpose: identify relevant competitors that rank for keywords our website is missing or where our rankings are weaker. This method is useful after creating an initial competitor list.

Step 1 — Open Keyword Gap and enter domains

Open the “Keyword Gap” tool in SEMrush.

Enter our own website in the first domain field.

Enter known competitor domains in the remaining comparison fields.

The original workflow notes that up to four competitor domains can be compared alongside our website.

Select the target country/database for the project.

Choose the Organic keyword type.

Click “Compare” to generate the report.

Expected output: A keyword comparison report for our website and selected competitors.

Step 2 — Review the keyword opportunity tabs

Review the following tabs and understand what each one indicates:

Shared — keywords common to the compared websites.

Missing — keywords for which competitors rank but our website does not.

Weak — keywords for which our website ranks lower than competitors.

Untapped — keywords competitors rank for but our website does not currently rank for.

Strong — keywords where our website ranks better than competitors.

Unique — keywords unique to one of the selected domains.

Expected output: A set of keyword opportunities and competitor keyword patterns.

Step 3 — Identify potential competitors

Start with the “Missing” and “Untapped” reports.

Find domains that rank for multiple relevant keywords related to our products/services.

Shortlist domains that show repeated and meaningful keyword overlap.

Open each shortlisted domain's website.

Check its products, services, and target audience to confirm business relevance.

If it is a relevant business competitor, add it to “Final Competitors”.

Export the Keyword Gap report in CSV/Excel format and save the raw report.

Expected output: Additional relevant organic competitors supported by keyword gap data.

Step 4 — Save the Keyword Gap data

Save the competitor domain.

Save Common Keywords, Missing Keywords, and Untapped Keywords.

Record Organic Traffic where available.

Record keyword position, volume, and KD where available.

Keep the exported report for future reference.

Expected output: A saved Keyword Gap dataset linked to the domains shortlisted.

6. Method 4 — Position Tracking Competitor Discovery

Purpose: identify domains that repeatedly appear in search results for our selected target keywords.

Step 1 — Set up Position Tracking

Open “Position Tracking” in SEMrush.

Create a new project or select the existing project for our website.

Add our website/domain.

Select the target country and location for the tracking project.

Select the device setting required for the research.

Add our important target keywords to the tracking campaign.

Expected output: A Position Tracking project containing our website and selected target keywords.

Step 2 — Open Competitors Discovery

Open the Position Tracking dashboard.

Find and open the “Competitors Discovery” report.

Review the domains appearing in Google SERPs for our tracked keywords.

Identify domains that appear across multiple target keywords.

Shortlist domains with repeated visibility for relevant searches.

Expected output: A list of domains repeatedly visible for our tracked keywords.

Step 3 — Validate and save competitor data

Open each shortlisted domain's website.

Check whether its products/services and target audience are relevant to our business.

Record the competitor domain and its common keywords.

Record Visibility %, Estimated Traffic, and Keyword Positions where shown.

Record the target country used in the tracking project.

Add relevant domains to “Final Competitors” and note “Position Tracking” as the source method.

Expected output: A validated list of additional organic competitors discovered through tracked keywords.

FieldDetails to capture
Competitor DomainDomain shown in Competitors Discovery.
Common KeywordsRelevant tracked keywords shared with the domain.
Visibility %Visibility percentage shown in the report.
Estimated TrafficEstimated traffic shown, if available.
Keyword PositionsRelevant ranking positions shown.
Target CountryCountry/location configured in Position Tracking.

7. Method 5 — Manual Google SERP Competitor Research

Purpose: manually verify which websites appear in Google organic search results for important keywords in the target country.

Step 1 — Select important business keywords

List the main products/services offered by our website.

Create search keywords based on those products/services.

Prioritize high-volume and commercial-intent keywords.

Prepare the list according to the target country being researched.

Expected output: A focused list of business-relevant keywords to check in Google.

Example keywords from the original notes:

Best stone countertops

Quartz countertops near me

Natural stone suppliers

Best granite suppliers

Quartzite countertops

Step 2 — Review Google's organic results

Search each keyword in Google using the relevant target-country context.

Review the Top 10 organic results for that keyword.

Record the domains that appear in those results.

Notice which websites appear repeatedly across multiple keywords.

Open each website and verify its products, services, and target audience.

Do not automatically treat ads, social profiles, marketplaces, or irrelevant directories as direct business competitors.

Expected output: A manually reviewed list of websites that compete for the target searches.

Step 3 — Save manual SERP research

Record the search keyword.

Record the keyword volume if available from the keyword research data.

Record the Google organic position.

Record the competitor domain and ranking URL.

Record the target country.

Add a Business Relevance rating such as High, Medium, or Low.

Add relevant domains to the final competitor sheet and note “Manual Google SERP” as the source.

Expected output: A traceable manual SERP dataset showing which keyword surfaced each competitor.

8. Final Competitor Selection Rules

A domain discovered through a tool is only a potential competitor. Validate it before adding it to the final business competitor list.

The domain must be relevant to the selected target country and our industry.

Give priority to websites offering the same or closely related products/services.

Check organic keyword overlap and search visibility.

Open the website and manually verify its business relevance.

Exclude YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Flipkart, Amazon, and other social/marketplace platforms from the final business competitor list.

Remove duplicate domains. Keep the different keywords and discovery sources linked to the same domain in the research tabs.

Keep organic competitors and direct business competitors distinguishable in the spreadsheet.

Do not assume that every website ranking for one keyword is a direct business competitor.

10. Combined Research Workflow — Recommended Order

Start with Method 1 (Domain Overview) and create the base list of 50 organic competitors for the target country.

Use Method 2 (Keyword-Based Research) to inspect relevant keywords with 500+ search volume and review their Top 10 SERP results.

Use Method 3 (Keyword Gap) to discover domains ranking for Missing and Untapped keywords.

Use Method 4 (Position Tracking) to find domains repeatedly visible for the tracked target keywords.

Use Method 5 (Manual Google SERP Research) to independently verify important searches and competitor websites.

Combine all discovered domains in the working sheet and remove duplicates.

Visit each shortlisted website and verify its products/services and target audience.

Mark each domain Selected, Rejected, or Review and retain the discovery source and supporting data.
