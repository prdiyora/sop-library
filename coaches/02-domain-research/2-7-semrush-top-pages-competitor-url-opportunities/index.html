🌐 2.7 — Semrush Top Pages - Competitor URL Opportunities

Dabbo 02 · Domain Research & Selection · ✅ Done

📋 Sheet: Aa stop nu data Semrush Research sheet na "Top Pages" tab ma bharo. (Template ni copy banavvi hoy to link par click karo — "Make a copy" button aavse.)

2.7.A Aa stop su chhe

Competitor na sabthi vadhare traffic lavta pages kayo chhe - e joiye to khabar pade ke aapde kaya type na page banavvana. Traffic share, keywords count ane backlinks joi ne opportunity pages shortlist karo. Real run: Client A, 19 Nov 2026.

2.7.B Semrush Top Pages (step-by-step)

Goal: Google ane AI visibility laavta URLs shodhva.

Step 1: Saachu Top Pages kholo

SEO → Competitive Analysis → Top Pages kholo. Aa SEO Top Pages report chhe — Traffic & Market → Top Pages NATHI, e alag dataset ane traffic metrics vapre chhe.

Task pramane domain, subdomain ke subfolder nakho. Working example: client-a-store.com. Search pehla saachu country select karo — real run ma screen par default UK j rahyu hatu.

Real run 19 Nov 2026 (US, Nov 18 2026): Organic Traffic 205 (-28.6%), Organic Pages 268 (+0.75%), Cited Pages 36 (+24.1%), 296 URLs listed. Top URL: /collections/porcelain-sinks — 52 estimated visits (25.4% share), 45 keywords, 1 LLM prompt, 3 referring domains, top keyword 'porcelain kitchen sinks' (commercial intent). Pachhi: Distar granite diamond blade product page — 29 visits, 'cutting granite' (informational) thi; ane blog /kitchen-sink-cabinet-combo-buying-guide — 21 visits, "new" marked.

Step 2: Report settings consistent rakho

Note karo: domain/scope, selected database ane month. Competitor comparison mate identical settings vapro.

Table populate thay ke explicit no-data state aave tya sudhi wait karo. Blank ke loading area e puravо nathi ke website paase pages nathi.

Step 3: URL-level metrics capture karo

Darek relevant URL mate note karo: Traffic, Traffic difference, Traffic share, Keyword count, Main keyword, Referring domains, LLM prompts ane Search intent (dekhay to).

Traffic estimates ane prompt-related metrics ALAG columns ma rakho. Ghana prompts valo URL e proof nathi ke e sales aape chhe.

Step 4: Source ane page type thi filter karo

Sources vapro: Organic, AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT ane Gemini — alag alag ke combination ma juo.

URL search/Advanced filters thi relevant product, collection ke guide sections par focus karo. Top Keyword filters thi branded queries kadho ke commercial/transactional intent juo (je option available hoy).

Step 5: Winners ane declines juo

Traffic thi sort karo — mota pages; Traffic difference thi — changes; LLM prompts thi — AI-visible references. Organic pages, organic traffic ane cited pages no trend pan juo.

Decline dekhay to investigation karvi. Content quality ne dosh aapta PEHLA check karo: date range, redirects, indexing, seasonality ane page changes.

Step 6: Useful competitor pages kholo

Top relevant competitor URLs kholo. Note karo: page type, title, headings, useful information, product details, evidence, FAQs ane internal links. Fakt design nahi, ranking intent samjo.

Example: relevant diamond-blade collection lead kare to ena filters, specification guidance ane related guides note karo. Client A na verified products thi original plan banao — copied text ke unsupported claims nahi.

Step 7: Page opportunity tracker banao

Tracker: Research date | Country/month | Domain | Exact URL | Page type | Main keyword | Estimated traffic/change | Keywords | Referring domains | LLM prompts | Sources | Gap | Our existing/planned URL | Action/status.

Pehla existing page sathe match karo. Navi page fakt tyare j banavvi jyare intent genuinely alag hoy. Exports PDF, Excel ke CSV ma save karo (available hoy to).

Step 8: Final quality check

Competitor traffic ne actual analytics tarike report na karo. AI visibility = direct referral traffic evu assume na karo.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • SEO Top Pages select karyu, same-naam valo Traffic & Market dashboard nahi.
  • Darek shortlisted URL kholi ne relevance check kari.
  • Country, month, scope ane selected sources log thay gaya.
  • Aapnu content real products ane original explanations vapre chhe.
  • Output: ranked page opportunities + darek mate update/new-page decision.

2.7.C Expected Output

Output: competitor na top pages ni list, je page aapde banavvo/improve karvo e sathe mark.
