🌐 2.8 — Semrush Organic Rankings - Competitor Keywords & URLs

Dabbo 02 · Domain Research & Selection · ✅ Done

2.8.A Aa stop su chhe

Competitor kaya keywords par rank kare chhe ane kayu URL rank thay chhe - e jovanu. Ahiya thi j khabar pade ke aapne kaya keywords par content banavvu haji baki chhe. Real run: Skyline Stone competitor analysis, 19 Nov 2026.

2.8.B SEMrush Organic Rankings

Step-by-step: find ranking keywords, improving pages and lost organic opportunities

1. Open and set the scope

Open SEO → Competitive Analysis → Organic Rankings. Enter the target domain and select Root Domain, Subdomain, Subfolder or Exact URL as needed.

Choose the country database, desktop/mobile and date. Working example: skylinestoneproducts.com in the agreed US database. Record these settings before collecting rows.

Real run on 19 Nov 2026 (US database, desktop, Nov 18 2026): 725 keywords (+5.2%), estimated Traffic 205 (-28.6%), Traffic Cost $73 (-28.4%), Branded Traffic 0. Top keywords: 'cutting granite' position 10 (volume 480, 14.1% of traffic), 'porcelain kitchen sinks' 3 (720), 'ready made kitchen sink with cabinet' 2 (590), 'leveling square' 1 (110), 'best polish for granite countertops' 1 (110). Intent mix: Informational 46.3% of keywords, Commercial 37.4%, Transactional 9.3%, Navigational 7%.

2. Read Overview first

Record keyword count, estimated traffic, traffic cost and branded/non-branded split where shown. Review traffic and keyword trends, position distribution and SERP features.

Traffic Cost estimates the paid-search value of organic visibility. It is not the client's advertising bill or revenue.

3. Open Positions and collect the full row

Open Positions. Record Keyword, Intent, Position, Previous position, Volume, KD %, Traffic %, ranking URL, SERP features and Last Update where available.

Use Manage columns when necessary. Inspect the URL associated with each keyword, not just the domain's aggregate score.

4. Find practical improvements

Filter positions 11–20 to find relevant existing pages close to page one. Check 4–10 separately for pages that may benefit from better intent coverage or internal links. These are working filters, not promises of quick wins.

Use keyword include/exclude and intent filters. For Skyline Stone, start with diamond blade or polishing pad terms and remove irrelevant locations, brands or products the business does not supply.

5. Review Position Changes and Pages

Open Position Changes and inspect New, Improved, Declined and Lost groups where offered. Compare like-for-like dates and open the affected URLs.

Open Pages to identify which URLs drive organic estimates and how many keywords map to each. If several of our URLs target the same intent, flag possible cannibalization for review rather than deleting pages.

6. Check the live SERP and competitors

Open the SERP snapshot beside a shortlisted keyword. Record its collection date and the top relevant competing page types. A snapshot is not necessarily today's live personalized result.

Example: if a commercial diamond-blade query mostly returns collections, improve an appropriate collection rather than forcing a blog article to rank for it. Check product relevance before choosing a page.

7. Turn findings into a work list

Tracker columns: Date | Database/device | Keyword | Intent | Volume | KD | Current/previous position | Our ranking URL | Competitor reference | Issue | Action | Owner | Status.

Action examples: expand useful specifications, fix a mismatched title, add a relevant internal link, or prepare a new intent-matched page. Implement only approved work, then measure in Position Tracking and the site's first-party tools.

8. Export and verify

Export filtered rows to CSV/XLSX, or use PDF reporting if available. Record filters so the export can be reproduced.

Legacy Organic Rankings and Rankings 2.0 use different models; do not splice their traffic series into one chart.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Full report reviewed, not just top keywords.
  • Each selected keyword has the correct ranking URL and market.
  • Tool estimates kept separate from GSC/GA4.
  • Output: prioritized URL-keyword action tracker, with evidence and verification date.

2.8.C Expected Output

Output: competitor na ranking keywords ni filtered list + URL mapping (kayu keyword kaya page par).
