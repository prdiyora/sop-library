🌐 2.2 — Competitor Pool Analysis

Dabbo 02 · Domain Research & Selection · ✅ Done

📋 Sheet: Aa stop nu data Competitor Research sheet na "Competitor Pool" tab ma bharo. (Template ni copy banavvi hoy to link par click karo — "Make a copy" button aavse.)

Tamara top competitors juvo etle shortcut: te loko shu rank kare chhe, su lakhe chhe, kya thi backlinks le chhe - badhu free ma roadmap male.

2.2.A Steps

  1. Top 3-5 competitors shodho: main keyword Google ma search karo - first page par je consistency sathe dekhay te j real competitors.
  2. Temna top pages note karo: kaya pages sauthi vadhare traffic lave chhe (Ubersuggest/Semrush free trial).
  3. Temna keywords juvo: kaya keywords par rank kare chhe je tamara pase nathi - te gap list chhe.
  4. Content style juvo: ketlu lamba articles, images, videos, FAQ structure, schema.
  5. Backlink sources note karo: kya sites temne link kare chhe - je relevant sites (directories, associations, PR, suppliers) tamne pan naturally link aapi shake te target list ma lo; paid/spam placements skip.

2.2.B Pool mathi su levu

  • Je temna ma chhe pan tamara ma nathi (features, pages, topics)
  • Temni weakness (slow site, old content, no FAQ) - tya jeeto
  • Temna best content thi 10x better content banavvo

2.2.C Checklist

  • 3-5 competitors ni list + notes chhe
  • Keyword gap list banavi chhe
  • Temna 3 strengths ane 3 weaknesses lakhela chhe

2.2.D Right Competitors kevi rite shodhva (full SOP, step-by-step)

Goal: selected target country mate organic search ma aapni sathe compete karta websites ni verified list banavvi. 5 methods: Domain Overview, Keyword-Based SERP Research, Keyword Gap, Position Tracking, ane Manual Google SERP Research.

Order: pehla Method 1 thi base list (50 domains), pachhi Method 2-5 thi nava domains, chhelle validate kari ne duplicates kadho.

Step 0: Research taiyar karo

  1. Aapnu exact domain lakho (original notes ma example: client-a-store.com).
  2. Target country confirm karo — country-specific reports ma SAME country vapro.
  3. Aapna main products, services ane business categories ni list banao.
  4. Ek research spreadsheet taiyar karo — raw exports, keyword data, SERP results ane final competitor list mate. Raw research kyarey overwrite NA karo.

Method 1 — Domain Overview (base list)

  1. Semrush → Domain Overview kholo. AAPNU domain nakho (competitor nu nahi).
  2. Initial overview mate country selector "Worldwide" rakho (original workflow pramane) ane report run karo.
  3. "Main Organic Competitors" card shodho → "View Details" → "Competitors" tab.
  4. Have country selector ma TARGET country select karo ane report refresh thava do — aa country badhi research ma consistent rakho.
  5. Badha pages/results export karo (fakt pehla page nahi), CSV format ma. Original CSV nu untouched copy "Domain Overview Export" tab ma rakho.
  6. Exported data par aa 5 filters ek ek kari ne apply karo ane darek ma strongest rows shortlist karo: Top 10 Com. Level, Top 10 Common Keywords, Top 10 SE Keywords, Top 10 Traffic, Top 10 Costs. Pachhi badha filters ni shortlists compare karo, duplicates kadho, ane total 50 competitors select karo — ae "Final Competitors" tab ma save karo.

Output: target country mate ~50 domains ni base competitor list.

Method 2 — Keyword-Based Competitor Research

  1. Semrush → Organic Rankings kholo, aapnu domain nakho, "Positions" tab kholo.
  2. "Volume" column High → Low sort karo. 500+ volume vala relevant keywords shortlist karo (aapna actual products/services sathe related j). Darek "Keyword Research" tab ma note karo.
  3. Darek shortlisted keyword click karo (ek ek kari ne) → keyword row ma preview icon click karo → popup ma "SERP Analysis" → Top 10 SERP results ma dekhata relevant business websites note karo.
  4. Platform EXCLUDE karo: YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Flipkart, Amazon ane bija social/marketplace platforms — e business competitors nathi. Fakt competing products/services aapta brand/business websites j include karo.
  5. Eligible domains "Final Competitors" tab ma umero ane kayi keyword thi malya e note karo.
  6. Darek keyword na popup metrics save karo: Global Volume, country-wise volumes, KD, SF, Intent, CPC, Trend. Live report ma dekhata values j — example values copy NAHI.

Method 3 — Keyword Gap

  1. Semrush → Keyword Gap kholo. First field ma aapnu website, baki fields ma known competitor domains (4 sudhi).
  2. Target country/database select karo, Organic keyword type select karo, "Compare" click karo.
  3. Tabs samjo: Shared = common keywords; Missing = competitors rank kare, aapne nahi; Weak = aapne competitors karta niche; Untapped = competitors rank kare, aapne bilkul nahi; Strong = aapne vadhare saro; Unique = fakt ek domain na.
  4. "Missing" ane "Untapped" reports thi chalu karo. Aapna products/services sathe related ghana keywords par rank karta domains shodho. Repeated ane meaningful overlap vala shortlist karo.
  5. Darek shortlisted domain nu website kholo — products, services, target audience check karo. Relevant business competitor hoy to "Final Competitors" ma umero.
  6. Keyword Gap report CSV/Excel ma export kari ne raw save karo. Note karo: competitor domain, Common/Missing/Untapped keywords, Organic Traffic, position, volume, KD (available hoy to).

Method 4 — Position Tracking Competitor Discovery

  1. Semrush → Position Tracking ma project banao/select karo: aapnu domain, target country/location, device, ane important target keywords add karo.
  2. Dashboard ma "Competitors Discovery" report kholo — aapna tracked keywords na Google SERPs ma dekhata domains juo.
  3. Ghana target keywords par repeated visibility vala domains shortlist karo.
  4. Darek nu website kholi ne business relevance validate karo, pachhi "Final Competitors" ma umero, source = "Position Tracking".

Method 5 — Manual Google SERP Research

  1. Aapna main products/services mathi keywords banao — high-volume ane commercial-intent ne priority. Target country pramane list taiyar karo. Original notes na examples: Best stone countertops; Quartz countertops near me; Natural stone suppliers; Best granite suppliers; Quartzite countertops.
  2. Darek keyword Google ma search karo (relevant target-country context ma). Top 10 organic results na domains note karo. Ghana keywords par repeatedly dekhata websites mark karo.
  3. Darek website kholi ne products, services ane target audience verify karo. Ads, social profiles, marketplaces ke irrelevant directories ne direct business competitors NA gano.
  4. Save karo: search keyword, volume (available hoy to), Google organic position, competitor domain, ranking URL, target country, Business Relevance (High/Medium/Low). Source = "Manual Google SERP".

Final selection rules

  • Tool mathi avelo domain = fakt POTENTIAL competitor. Final list ma umerata pehla validate karo.
  • Domain target country ane aapni industry sathe relevant hovo joie; same ke closely related products/services vala websites ne priority.
  • Organic keyword overlap ane search visibility check karo; website kholi ne manually verify karo.
  • YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Flipkart, Amazon ane bija social/marketplace platforms exclude karo.
  • Duplicate domains kadho; same domain na alag keywords ane discovery sources research tabs ma linked rakho.
  • Spreadsheet ma organic competitors ane direct business competitors alag distinguishable rakho.
  • Ek keyword par rank karvu e direct business competitor nathi — evu assume na karo.

Combined workflow — recommended order

  1. Method 1 (Domain Overview) → target country mate 50 organic competitors ni base list.
  2. Method 2 (Keyword-Based) → 500+ volume keywords na Top 10 SERP results review.
  3. Method 3 (Keyword Gap) → Missing ane Untapped keywords par rank karta domains.
  4. Method 4 (Position Tracking) → tracked keywords par repeatedly dekhata domains.
  5. Method 5 (Manual Google SERP) → important searches independently verify.
  6. Badha domains combine karo, duplicates kadho, darek shortlisted website visit kari ne verify karo.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Base list (50 domains) Method 1 thi banavli, target country consistent rakhi.
  • Social/marketplace platforms (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Flipkart, Amazon) final list mathi kadhela.
  • Darek final competitor nu website manually open kari ne business relevance verify karyu.
  • Duplicates kadhela; raw exports ane method notes preserved.
  • Output: verified Final Competitors tab + keyword ane SERP evidence tabs.
