🎨 5.1 — UI/UX - AI-made na lage aevu design

Dabbo 05 · Design & Web Development · ✅ Done

2026 ma AI websites same-look thai gay chhe - same gradients, same layout, robotic text. User 3 second ma identify kari jay ane trust ghate. Human feel = easy to read + natural flow.

5.1.A Notebook na points

  1. Vanchvama easy: body text no font size 16px+, line height 1.5+, contrast strong rakho (white par light gray text = fail).
  2. Bad paragraphs avoid karo: lamba 10-line paragraphs nahi. 2-4 lines nu paragraph, vachche subheadings, bullet lists.
  3. AI text patterns avoid karo: "In today's fast-paced world", "delve into", "unlock the power" - aa words dekhata j user jaani jay. Simple, direct bhasha lakho.

5.1.B Human feel mate

  • Real photos (team, office, products) - stock images minimum rakho
  • Real customer na words (reviews, testimonials names sathe)
  • Brand nu consistent voice (formal/friendly - pan consistent)
  • Whitespaces
  • screen bharelu na dekhay

5.1.C Test

Page ne 5 second juvo ane pucho: "Aa page su kehve chhe?" Answer na male to redesign.

5.1.D Checklist

  • Font 16px+, contrast pass, 2-4 line na paragraphs
  • AI-typical words hataavela
  • Real photos/testimonials present chhe
  • 5-second clarity test pass thayelo

5.1.M Design build checklist & reusable template (Dabbo 11.8 thi merged)

Aa checklist na badha items client ni design sheet ma pan mukvana — koi item miss na thay.

Aa design ane colour ni detail have client ni baseline sheet ma j bharvani chhe (ek j sheet ma client info + design tab). Niche nu checklist vapari ne sheet ma badhu bharo, pachhi developer ke AI ne e j sheet aapo.
