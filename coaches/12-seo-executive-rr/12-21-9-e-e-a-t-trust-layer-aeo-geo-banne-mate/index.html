🎓 12.21 — 9. E-E-A-T — Trust layer (AEO + GEO banne mate)

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

E-E-A-T etle Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. Google ne ane AI — badhe joiye chhe ke content pachhal koi real, credible vyakti chhe. AEO ma answer par trust aavva mate ane GEO ma AI cite kare e mate aa layer j foundation chhe. (Yaad rakho: AEO formula ma PROVE ane GEO formula ma TRUST — aa e j vaat chhe.)

4 parts (simple):

  • Experience — topic no first-hand/real experience (tame kharekhar use karyu/joyu chhe?).
  • Expertise — content lakhnaar ni knowledge/skill (doctor, practitioner, specialist).
  • Authoritativeness — field ma brand/author ni reputation (biju kon tamne reference kare?).
  • Trustworthiness — accuracy, honesty, safety — sauthi important part.

Analogy: E-E-A-T etle resume + reference check. Interview ma tame gme te kaho, pan certificate, experience ane referral hoy to j employer trust kare. Google/AI pan evi j rite content trust kare.

Page par kem implement karvu:

  • Clear author info — name, photo, short bio, credentials.
  • High-stakes topics (health, legal, finance = YMYL) mate expert review/author batavo.
  • Credible sources cite karo (gov, research, official).
  • Original data / real experience / case studies add karo.
  • Transparent claims — limitations honestly kaho.
  • Update — facts badle tyare content update karo (updated date batavo).
  • Author/expert page banavo ane relevant articles sathe link karo.

Kadi na karo (red flags):

  • Fake expertise ke fake credentials.
  • Fake statistics / fake reviews / fake citations.
  • Unsupported medical / legal / financial claims.

Aa badhu fake hoy to short-term kadaach chale, pan trust ane long-term visibility — banne bagade.
