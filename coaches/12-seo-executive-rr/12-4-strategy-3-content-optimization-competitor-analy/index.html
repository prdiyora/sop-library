🎓 12.4 — Strategy 3: Content Optimization & Competitor Analysis

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

Timeline: Month 2–4 (Building Momentum)

Aa stage ma content improve karvanu ane competitor karta better information provide karvani strategy implement karvani.

12.4.A 1. Content Refresh Strategy

Objective

Traffic gumavi rahela pages ne fari rank karavva.

Action

Last 6–12 months ma traffic decline thayela pages identify karo.

Checklist

✅ Latest information add karo.

✅ New examples ane statistics include karo.

✅ Missing sub-topics mate H2/H3 headings add karo.

✅ Outdated screenshots ane images replace karo.

✅ Publish date update kari Google Search Console ma Request Indexing karo.

12.4.B 2. Competitor Keyword Gap Analysis

Objective

Competitors je keywords par rank kare chhe pan tame nathi karta e opportunities identify karvi.

Action

Semrush/Ahrefs Keyword Gap Tool no upyog kari Missing ane Weak keywords identify karo.

Checklist

✅ High Search Volume + Low Keyword Difficulty keywords select karo.

✅ Search Intent analyze karo.

✅ Better ane comprehensive content publish karo.

✅ Content Calendar create karo.

12.4.C 3. Keyword Cannibalization Fix

Objective

Same keyword mate multiple pages compete na kare ane ranking signals consolidate thay.

Action

Ek j keyword mate rank karta badha pages identify karo.

Checklist

✅ Best performing page identify karo.

✅ Remaining content merge karo.

✅ Weak pages par 301 Redirect implement karo.

✅ Internal links update karo.

✅ Canonical tags verify karo.
