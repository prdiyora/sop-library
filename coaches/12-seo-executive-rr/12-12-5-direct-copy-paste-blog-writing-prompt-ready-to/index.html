## 6. Monthly Execution & Measurement SOP

Week 1: Performance Audit └─ Export 3-month GSC data and identify striking-distance opportunities (positions 5-20).

Week 2: Mapping & De-duplication └─ Assign 1 primary keyword per URL and resolve any keyword cannibalization across existing pages.

Week 3: Content Production └─ Publish AEO-structured articles with contextual internal links directing traffic to core money pages.

Week 4: Review & Refinement └─ Track impression growth, CTR changes, and ranking shifts; adjust topics for the upcoming cycle.

### Implementation Checklist

  • Week 1 (Audit): Pull GSC metrics. Filter for keywords ranking in positions 5–20 with strong impressions. Identify underperforming pages with low CTR.
  • Week 2 (Keyword Mapping): Map exactly one primary keyword per target page. Check existing URLs to fix cannibalization before creating new content.
  • Week 3 (Publishing): Execute articles using the AEO framework. Build contextual internal links using proper descriptive anchors back to collection/money pages.
  • Week 4 (Measurement): Review search performance in GSC. Document movement in rank positions, impression spikes, and organic CTR to update next month's queue.
