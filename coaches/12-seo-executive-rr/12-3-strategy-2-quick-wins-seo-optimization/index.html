🎓 12.3 — Strategy 2: Quick Wins SEO Optimization

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

Timeline: Month 1–2 (Immediate Impact)

Aa strategies eva improvements par focus kare chhe je ochha effort ma relatively fast SEO results api shake. Primary objective existing pages ni visibility, CTR ane rankings improve karvano chhe.

12.3.A 1. Title & Meta Tag Optimization

Objective

Organic Click-Through Rate (CTR) improve karvi ane existing rankings mathi vadhu traffic melvo.

Action

Google Search Console mathi sauthi vadhu impressions vala Top 20 pages identify karo.

Checklist

✅ Title Tags ne attractive ane click-worthy banavo.

✅ Primary keyword ne possible hoy tya title ni sharuat ma mukvo.

✅ Meta Description ma keyword sathe clear value proposition add karo.

✅ Duplicate Title ane Meta Description remove karo.

✅ Title length (50–60 characters) ane Meta Description (140–160 characters) optimize karo.

12.3.B 2. Internal Linking Optimization

Objective

Website ni authority important pages sudhi pass karvi ane crawlability improve karvi.

Action

High-traffic ane high-authority pages identify kari target pages ne strategic internal links aapo.

Checklist

✅ High-authority pages mathi target pages ne internal links aapo.

✅ Relevant anchor text no upyog karo.

✅ Orphan pages identify kari internal links add karo.

✅ Related blog posts vachche contextual linking karo.

12.3.C 3. Low-Hanging Keywords Optimization

Objective

Already ranking pages ne Top 10 ma push kari vadhu organic traffic melvo.

Action

Google Search Console ma Position 11–20 par rank thata keywords identify karo.

Checklist

✅ Existing content update karo.

✅ Latest information ane visuals add karo.

✅ FAQ section add karo.

✅ 2–5 nava internal links add karo.

✅ Title ane Meta Description optimize karo.

✅ Updated page ne Google Search Console mathi re-index karavo.
