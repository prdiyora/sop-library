🎓 12.36 — 3. Off-Page SEO & Backlink Management

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

12.36.A Q: Backlink quality kevi rite check karo cho ane toxic links kevi rite handle karo?

  • Quality Evaluation Signals:
  • Domain Authority / Domain Rating metrics (DA/DR)
  • Niche Relevancy (most critical strategic factor)
  • Real organic traffic trend analysis (verification against PBNs)
  • Balanced Dofollow vs Nofollow distribution ratio
  • Spam Score threshold validation (< 5%)
  • Toxic Link Remediation Process:
  • Backlink audit tool ma toxic score check karvo. Pehla webmaster ne manual removal request karvi. Remove na thay ane penalty risk hoy to j Google Disavow tool thi disavow file upload karvi.
  • High-Impact Link Building Strategies:
  • Strategic Guest Posting & Niche Edits
  • Broken Link Building & Unlinked Brand Mentions
  • Skyscraper Technique with data-driven assets
  • Digital PR / HARO (Connectively) media outreach
  • Competitor Backlink Gap Analysis
  • Anchor Text Distribution Profile:
  • Exact match anchor text over-optimize na karvo. Natural mix rakhvo:
  • Branded: 40% – 50%
  • Partial Match: 20% – 25%
  • Naked URL: 15% – 20%
  • Generic / Phrase: 5% – 10%
