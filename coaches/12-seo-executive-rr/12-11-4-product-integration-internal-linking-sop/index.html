🎓 12.11 — 4. Product Integration & Internal Linking SOP

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

12.11.A Product Selection & Link Insertion

  • Relevance Limit: Feature a maximum of 5 closely related products from your store catalog per article.
  • Anchor Text Rule: Avoid using exact, full product names in running prose. Instead, use natural, 2–5 word lowercase descriptive anchor text (e.g., use "lightweight running shoes" instead of "Men's Apex VaporFly Pro 2024 Edition").

12.11.C Collection & Category Linking

  • Integrate the targeted parent category or collection URL exactly 2 times within the article body.
  • Use rich, descriptive anchor text. Never use generic anchors such as "click here", "link", or "buy now".
