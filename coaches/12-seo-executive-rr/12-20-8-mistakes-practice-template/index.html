🎓 12.20 — 8. Mistakes, practice & template

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

Common mistakes (na karo):

  • Answer 5 paragraph pachhi aapvo (answer hiding).
  • Keyword stuffing — same question 20 vaar.
  • Fake FAQ, fake reviews, fake stats, unsupported medical/legal claims.
  • Page par na hoy e schema markup (schema abuse).
  • Generic AI article copy-paste — original value 0.
  • Optimize kari ne measure na karvu.

10-minute practice: ek keyword lo → user no exact question → intent → 40–60 word direct answer → 5 follow-up questions → darek no format → 2 internal links → 1–2 proof → page structure → publish → GSC ne AI ma check.
