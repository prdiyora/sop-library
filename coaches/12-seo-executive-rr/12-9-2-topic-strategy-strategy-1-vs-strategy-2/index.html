🎓 12.9 — 2. Topic Strategy: Strategy 1 vs. Strategy 2

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

Keep discovery and optimization workflows strictly separated to prevent scope creep and keyword cannibalization.

12.9.A Strategy 1: New Topic Discovery

  1. Analyze Niche: Define exact target topics based on primary products, services, and core audience pain points.
  2. Map Existing Assets: Catalog all published blogs, URLs, and landed keywords across the domain.
  3. Build Exclusion Map: Cross-reference prospective topics against existing URLs to ensure no overlapping keyword intent.
  4. Identify Gaps: Select unaddressed user questions and informational queries that map directly back to business services or catalog products.

12.9.B Strategy 2: GSC Low-Click / High-Impression Optimization

  1. Export GSC Data: Filter Google Search Console performance data for the last 3 months.
  2. Isolate Striking Distance Queries: Filter for queries occupying positions 5–20 with high impressions but low click-through rates (CTR).
  3. Cluster & Map: Group related striking-distance queries around a single central topic.
  4. Create Supporting Assets: Write high-intent informational blogs targeting these query clusters.
  5. Funnel Page Equity: Link all new supporting articles directly back to primary commercial/money pages (never create competing commercial landing pages).
