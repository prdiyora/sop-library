🎓 12.39 — 6. HR & Behavioral Questions

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

12.39.A Q: Current company kem chodo cho / Kem change karva mango cho?

Answer: "Current company nu business model mostly in-house/personal projects par based chhe ane external client work diversify thai ne out-source thay chhe. Aa lidhe mane direct client-facing exposure ane diverse industry challenges par strategy test karvano scope limited male chhe. Mari learning curve faster rakhva ane broad client accounts handle karva hu navi opportunity sodhu chhu."

12.39.B Q: 8 hours daily current company ma su kaam karta hata?

  • 09:00 AM – 10:00 AM: Health Check & Performance Monitoring
  • Reviewing Google Search Console, GA4 analytics, rank tracking tools, and diagnosing sudden indexation or technical anomalies.
  • 10:00 AM – 12:30 PM: On-Page & Content Strategy Execution
  • Keyword mapping, content gap analysis, writing/optimizing title tags, meta descriptions, heading structures, and schema implementations.
  • 01:30 PM – 03:30 PM: Technical SEO & Engineering Coordination
  • Crawling sites via Screaming Frog, coordinating with developers on Core Web Vitals optimization, fixing 404 errors, and setting up redirect matrices.
  • 03:30 PM – 05:00 PM: Off-Page SEO & Competitive Intelligence
  • Analyzing competitor backlink profiles, managing high-quality outreach campaigns, and auditing link quality metrics.
  • 05:00 PM – 06:00 PM: Reporting, Analytics & Search Algorithm Research
  • Compiling client/executive performance dashboards, measuring conversion goals, and staying up to date with the latest Google algorithm updates.
