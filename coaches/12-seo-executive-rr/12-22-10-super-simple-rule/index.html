🎓 12.22 — 10. Super simple rule

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

Google ne trick karvano nahi — user + machine banne ne clear, useful, trustworthy answer aapvano. AI ne impress nahi karvano; AI ne confuse na thava devano.

  • SEO: page rank.
  • AEO: user no direct answer.
  • GEO: AI tamne samje ne cite kare.
  • AIO: AI Overview ma dekhavu.
  • SXO: site par saaru experience + conversion.

Flow: Structure → Retrieve → Cite → Trust → Convert.

Website Monitoring & SEO Analytics Complete Guide

  1. Monitoring Shu Chhe? (What is Monitoring?)

Monitoring etle tamari website nu performance regular basis par check karta rehvu. AA process thi tamne khabar pade chhe ke kaya pages sara perform kare chhe ane kaya pages ma traffic ghati rahyo chhe.

  • Performance Tracking: Continuous tracking thi website ni health jalvai rahe chhe.
  • Proactive Action: Issues ne vadhare mota thaya pehla j solve kari shakay chhe.
  1. Essential Monitoring & SEO Tools

Website health ane traffic grow karva mate niche aapeIa core tools khub j zaroori chhe:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Website par kon aave chhe, kaya source (Google, Social Media, Direct) thi aave chhe ane site par ketlo time spend kare chhe te check karva mate.
  • Google Search Console (GSC): Google search ma site nu ranking, search keywords, clicks, impressions ane indexing issues check karva mate.
  • SEMrush / Ahrefs: Competitor analysis, keyword rank tracking, and backlink audit mate best enterprise tools.
  • Google Looker Studio (Add-on): GA4 ane GSC no data visually interactive dashboard ma dekhadva mate free Google tool.
  1. Weekly Check-up Routine (Keviri Rite Check Karvu?)

Ek fixed routine follow karvathi data analysis saral bani jaay chhe:

  • Step 1: GA4 ane GSC accounts open karo.
  • Step 2: Last 7 days no data pichla 7 days sathe compare karo.
  • Step 3: Jo koi main page par traffic 10% thi vadhare drop thayo hoy, to turant dhyan aapo ane audit karo.
  • Step 4: New keywords, gainers, ane high-ranking queries ne sheet ma note karo.
  1. Traffic Ghati Jaay To Content Update Keviri Rite Karvu?

Traffic drop thava par aa practical action steps follow karo:

  • Fresh Content Add Karo: Nayi facts, recent case studies ke 2026 ni updated tips uméro.
  • Visuals Improve Karo: High-resolution images, original charts ke relevant video embeds add karo.
  • Search Intent Match Karo: User je query search kare chhe teno direct answer pehla 100 words ma aapo.
  • Internal Linking: Website na bija relevant, high-traffic pages mathi aa page par links aapo.
  • Core Web Vitals Check (Add-on): Page load speed ochhi nathi thay gayi ne te PageSpeed Insights ma check karo.

Organic traffic vadharvu ane tech articles na strategic keywords ne Top 5 results ma rank karva.

  • User Engagement: Average engagement time 1m 20s thi vadhi ne 2m 10s thayo.
