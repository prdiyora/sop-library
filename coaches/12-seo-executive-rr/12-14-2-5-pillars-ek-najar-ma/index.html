🎓 12.14 — 2. 5 Pillars ek najar ma

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

One line each:

  • SEO — traditional search (Google) ma rank karvu.
  • GEO — AI models (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude…) tamaru brand reference/cite kare.
  • AEO — search par direct answer banvu (snippet, voice).
  • AIO — AI Overview (Google AI Overview, Bing Copilot) ma dekhavu.
  • SXO — user site par aavya pachhi no experience optimize karvo (UX + conversion).
PillarShu meansKem kaam kare (signals)Real example
SEO Search Engine Opt.Google jeva engine ma high rankKeyword research, on-page, quality backlinks, technical SEO"best running shoes" → ranked blog "10 Best Running Shoes 2026"
GEO Generative Engine Opt.AI models brand ne reference kareAI-friendly content, structured data, expert authority, trusted sourcesAI ne "Best CRM software?" → HubSpot · Salesforce · Zoho (cited)
AEO Answer Engine Opt.Search par direct answer banvuQuestion-based content, clear concise answers, FAQ schema, zero-click"What is digital marketing?" → clear definition first dekhaay
AIO AI Overview Opt.AI-generated overview ma dekhavuTopical authority, multi-source credibility, semantic relevance, entity optimization"CRM software comparison" → AI Overview sources: HubSpot · Salesforce · Zoho
SXO Search Experience Opt.Landing pachi UX + conversionFast loading, mobile-friendly, clear CTA, trust signalsLanding page "Run Further" → Shop Now (Fast · Mobile · Trusted)

Note: AIO ne kayak jagyae "AI Integration Optimization" pan kahe chhe (content ne AI tools/APIs vaapri shake evi rite structure karvi) — concept same: AI-usable content.
