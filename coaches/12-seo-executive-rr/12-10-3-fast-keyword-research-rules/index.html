🎓 12.10 — 3. Fast Keyword Research Rules

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

RuleRequirementImplementation
1 Page = 1 Primary KeywordCore FocusInclude within the Page Title (first 50–60 characters), URL slug, H1 tag, and naturally within the first 100 words.
3–5 Secondary KeywordsContext & DepthDistribute across H2/H3 subheadings, body paragraphs, and dedicated FAQ sections.
Intent AlignmentMatch Searcher GoalInformational: Educational blogs/guides.Commercial: Product comparisons/reviews.Transactional: Service or product sales pages.
Winnability CheckFeasibility FirstFor new or growing domains, target low-difficulty keywords (KD 0–30) rather than broad high-volume terms.
