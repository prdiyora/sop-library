🎓 12.5 — Strategy 4: Content Marketing & Authority Building

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

Timeline: Month 3 Onwards (Long-Term Growth)

Aa strategy long-term organic growth ane industry authority build karva mate design karvama aavi chhe.

12.5.A 1. Keyword Research & Content Planning

Objective

Business goals sathe relevant keywords target kari sustainable organic traffic build karvu.

Action

Keyword Gap, Topic Research ane Search Intent analysis kari content opportunities identify karo.

Checklist

✅ Low KD + High Search Volume keywords select karo.

✅ Commercial, Informational ane Transactional intent balance karo.

✅ Topic clusters create karo.

✅ Monthly Content Calendar maintain karo.

12.5.B 2. High-Quality Content Creation

Objective

Helpful, comprehensive ane E-E-A-T compliant content publish karvu.

Checklist

✅ 1500+ words nu comprehensive content.

✅ Proper Heading Structure (H1–H3).

✅ Images sathe optimized ALT Text.

✅ FAQ Section add karo.

✅ 3–5 Internal Links.

✅ 2–3 High Authority External References.

✅ FAQ Schema ane Article Schema implement karo.

✅ GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) ane Semantic SEO principles follow karo.

12.5.C 3. Off-Page SEO & Content Promotion

Objective

Content ni authority ane visibility increase karvi.

Checklist

✅ High-quality Guest Posts publish karo.

✅ Relevant Niche Backlinks build karo.

✅ Social Bookmarking.

✅ Image Submission.

✅ Video Submission.

✅ Profile Creation.

✅ Business Listings.

✅ Content publish thaya pachi Google Search Console ma Request Indexing karo.

12.5.D 4. Topical Authority Strategy

Objective

Google mate ek specific niche ma website ne trusted expert tarike establish karvi.

Action

Ek primary topic select kari complete Topic Cluster build karo.

Checklist

✅ Ek detailed Pillar Page (4000–5000+ words) create karo.

✅ 10–15 supporting Cluster Articles publish karo.

✅ Pillar Page ↔︎ Cluster Pages vachche strong internal linking maintain karo.

✅ Related entities ane semantic keywords naturally use karo.

✅ Topic regularly update karto raho.

12.5.E 5. Competitor Backlink Replication Strategy

Objective

Competitor na proven backlink sources use kari backlink research no time bachavvo ane authority fast build karvi.

Action

Competitor websites ne Semrush/Ahrefs ma analyze kari tena backlink sources identify karo.

Checklist

✅ Competitor Backlink Report analyze karo.

✅ Relevant Guest Post websites identify karo.

✅ High-quality Directory, Profile ane Resource Pages shortlist karo.

✅ Spam websites avoid karo.

✅ DR/DA, Relevance ane Traffic filter apply karo.

✅ Master Backlink Database maintain karo.
