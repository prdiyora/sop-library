🎓 12.17 — 5. LLMs.txt — GEO nu naru tool (2026)

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

Shu chhe: LLMs.txt ek markdown file chhe je AI systems / LLMs ne samjave ke tamari website par kayu content sauthi important chhe. Robots.txt jem search crawlers ne guide karto, em LLMs.txt AI agents ne discover, interpret ane accurately cite karva ma help kare.

Kem important (benefits):

  • AI ne priority pages ane content structure samjaay.
  • AI-powered search / assistants ma discoverability vadhe.
  • AEO ne support kare.
  • GEO strategy ne strong kare.
  • Tamara valuable content ne structured access aape.

Kem kaam kare (3 step):

  1. Tame LLMs.txt banavo.
  2. AI agents ene read/understand kare.
  3. Tamaru content AI systems dwara cite ne recommend thaay.

Line yaad rakho: "Be the source AI chooses to cite." Content AI-friendly banavo. (More: llmstxt.org)
