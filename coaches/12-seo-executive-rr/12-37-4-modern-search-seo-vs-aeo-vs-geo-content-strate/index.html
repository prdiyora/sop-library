🎓 12.37 — 4. Modern Search: SEO vs AEO vs GEO & Content Strategy

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

12.37.A Q: SEO, AEO ane GEO no difference su chhe?

Optimization TypeCore ObjectivePrimary TargetStrategy Focus
SEO (Search Engine Optimization)Rank in top organic search listingsSearch Engine Results Pages (SERPs)Keywords, Technical SEO, Backlinks, On-Page
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)Capture Position 0 & direct answersFeatured Snippets, Voice Search (Siri/Alexa)Concisely structured answers, FAQ Schema
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)Secure citation in AI responsesGemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI OverviewsFirst-party data, E-E-A-T, Brand Entity authority
  • Optimization Workflow for GEO & Helpful Content:
  • BLUF Approach (Bottom Line Up Front): Provide the direct core answer within the first two sentences.
  • Structured Data: Comprehensive implementation of FAQPage and HowTo schemas.
  • E-E-A-T Signals: Detailed author credentials, original primary research, case studies, and proprietary data points.
  • Originality Verification: Pass strict AI and plagiarism checks while injecting authentic human expertise and lived experience.
