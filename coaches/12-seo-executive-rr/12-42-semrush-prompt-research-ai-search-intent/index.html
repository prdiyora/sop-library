12.42.B /ai-seo/prompt-research/

SEMrush Prompt Research

Step-by-step: find the topics and questions people ask AI tools, then decide which ones to target

1. Prepare the research

Decide one seed topic from the project brief, the target country and the AI platform view before opening the tool. Use a product or service topic, not a brand name.

Working example, real run on 19 Nov 2026: seed topic "diamond blades" for Skyline Stone, United States, All AI platforms, data as of Nov 18 2026. Run through Jupiter Semrush Server 2 (Server 1 showed an upgrade prompt for this report).

2. Open the correct report

Open Jupiter → Semrush (Server 2 if Server 1 asks to upgrade) → AI → AI Visibility → Prompt Research (/ai-seo/prompt-research/).

Type the seed topic into "Enter a topic to analyze" and click Analyze. If an upsell window opens, close it and click Analyze again. Check the country tab (US, UK, DE, FR) and the platform filter (All AI platforms) on the results page.

3. Read the summary cards

Record the Related Topics AI Volume, number of Topics, number of Prompts, the Intent split, Brands Mentioned and Source Domains.

Observed example: AI Volume 4.7M, Topics 1K, Prompts 32.3K. Intent: Informational 48%, Commercial 25%, Navigational 12%, Transactional 11%, Task 4%. Brands Mentioned 15.8K (top: gia, brilliant earth, reddit). Source Domains 19.8K (top: youtube.com, google.com, reddit.com).

These are estimates of AI activity, not Google search volume. Do not compare them with Keyword Overview volume as if they were the same unit.

4. Review the related topics table

Columns: Topic, Relevance, AI Volume, Intent, Actions. Sort by AI Volume and open the top rows. The table has 1K topics over 100 pages, so do not judge from page 1 only.

Observed example (page 1): Diamond Blades and Cutting Tools 25.6K; Diamond Knives and Blades 1.8K; Diamond and Granite Cutting Blades 1.6K; Diamond Blade Metal Cutting 1.1K; diamond blade types 588; diamond blade barbering 281; MK Diamond Products and Blades 148; diamond blade warehouse 135.

5. Filter out unrelated topics

Every row shows Relevance High, but relevance is word match, not business match. Mark each topic Keep or Reject against the product range.

For Skyline Stone: keep "Diamond and Granite Cutting Blades", "Diamond Blade Metal Cutting", "diamond blade types". Reject "diamond blade barbering" and "Diamond Knives and Blades" (hair and kitchen knives, not stone tools). Large AI Volume does not make a topic relevant.

6. Open the prompts of one topic

Click the topic row to open its prompts. Columns: Prompt, AI Response, Brands, Sources, Actions (Create Content, Monitor).

Observed example, topic "Diamond and Granite Cutting Blades": "What are the best diamond blades for cutting granite?" (Brands 7, Sources 3); "blade to cut granite" (AI Overview answer, Brands 6, Sources 10); "diamond blades for granite" (Brands 6, Sources 7). Use View full response to read which brands and sources the AI named.

7. Choose prompts and save the research

Pick prompts that match a product or collection page the site has, or a page it should create. Note the brands and sources AI already cites, because those are the competitors and the pages to beat.

Tracker columns: Research date | Seed topic | Country | Platform view | Topic | AI Volume | Intent | Keep/Reject | Prompt | Brands cited | Sources cited | Target page | Next action. Use Monitor only if the plan allows adding prompts to Prompt Tracking.

8. Final quality check

No prompt or topic guarantees that AI tools will mention the site.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Correct seed, country, platform view and date.
  • Unrelated topics rejected (barbering, kitchen knives).
  • AI Volume labelled as an AI estimate, not Google volume.
  • Prompts opened and responses read, not only titles.
  • Output: a kept-topic list with 5 to 10 target prompts and the competitors AI cites.
