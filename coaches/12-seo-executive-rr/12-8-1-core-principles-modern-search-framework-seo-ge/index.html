🎓 12.8 — 1. Core Principles: Modern Search Framework (SEO + GEO + AEO + SXO)

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

Traditional search optimization focused solely on keyword placement and backlinks to earn top positions in standard search engine results pages. Modern search requires a unified strategy across four foundational pillars:

PillarFocus AreaExecution Strategy
SEOTraditional SearchKeyword targeting, technical architecture, and internal linking to rank in Google blue links.
AEOAnswer EnginesPlacing a concise 40–60 word summary at the top of content to capture Featured Snippets and voice answers.
GEO / AIOGenerative AIOptimizing for brand mentions and entity citations inside ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.
SXOSearch ExperienceImproving core web vitals, mobile usability, fast load times, and conversion paths upon user arrival.

12.8.A E-E-A-T Trust Layer

  • Experience & Expertise: Content must feature verified author credentials, direct hands-on observations, and authentic domain knowledge.
  • Authoritativeness & Trust: Zero tolerance for fake stats or hallucinated data. Every claim must be backed by accurate source citation or primary research.
