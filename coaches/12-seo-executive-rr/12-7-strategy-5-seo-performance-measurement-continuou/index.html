🎓 12.7 — Strategy 5: SEO Performance Measurement & Continuous Improvement

Dabbo 12 · SEO Executive RR · ✅ Done

Timeline: Weekly & Monthly (Continuous Process)

SEO ma success measure karya vagar optimization possible nathi. Regular monitoring thi opportunities ane issues banne jaldi identify thai shake.

12.7.A 1. Weekly SEO Monitoring

Objective

SEO performance continuously monitor karvi.

Action

Google Search Console regularly review karo.

Checklist

✅ Keyword Rankings monitor karo.

✅ Clicks & Impressions compare karo.

✅ New Indexing Issues check karo.

✅ Coverage Errors solve karo.

✅ Core Web Vitals monitor karo.

12.7.B 2. Monthly SEO Performance Report

Objective

Overall SEO growth measure kari next month ni strategy plan karvi.

Action

Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console ane Semrush/Ahrefs no combined report prepare karo.

Checklist

✅ Organic Traffic Growth.

✅ Keyword Ranking Improvements.

✅ Top Performing Pages.

✅ New Backlinks Acquired.

✅ Indexed Pages Growth.

✅ Conversion & Goal Tracking.

✅ Content Performance Analysis.

✅ Next Month Action Plan.

Aa final document tamne step-by-step guide karshe. Remember, Consistency is the key to SEO success.
