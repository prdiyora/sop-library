🧭 3.9 — 09 Scalability — 1 Site thi 100 Sites (Any Niche System) — 11 Nov 2026

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Aaj 1 site, 6 mahina ma 10, pachhi 100 — SOPs same j rahe, faqt system replicate thay. Aa doc e batave chhe ke aa folder na SOPs ne multi-site machine kevi rite banavva.

3.9.A 0. Core idea

  • Niche badlay, process nahi. Niche-specific vaat fakta 3 chhe: keywords, competitors, target country. Baki akho flow (00 START HERE no order) universal chhe — etle j aa SOPs 100 sites par chale.
  • Effort scale nahi thay, system scale thay. 100 sites = 100x kaam nahi — templates, master sheets, automation ane team roles thi per-site kaam 60-70% ochhu thay.
  • Quality gate kyarey skip nahi. 100 thin sites karta 10 quality sites vadhare kamave. Google scaled content abuse policy badha sites par lagu.

3.9.C 2. Portfolio Master Sheet (1 row = 1 site) — aa banavo

  • Columns: Site name | URL | Niche | Type (ecom/leadgen/blog) | Owner | Priority tier | GSC link | GA4 link | Tracking Sheet link | Drive folder | Launch date | Status | Last report date | Next action
  • Priority tiers (P1/P2/P3): P1 = revenue/active clients (daily 10 min + weekly); P2 = growing (weekly); P3 = maintenance (monthly). Badha sites ne same time na aapo — 80/20.
  • Aa j sheet team nu command center chhe — koi pan puche "X site nu su chale" to ahiya jove.

3.9.D 3. Process replication — navi site aave to (same order, 00 START HERE pramane)

  2. 07 Setup & Tracking (GSC/GA4/Clarity/Bing)

3.9.E 4. Team workflow (1 person thi team sudhi)

  • Solo (1-5 sites): badhu pothe, SOP order follow karo, Portfolio Sheet ma track.
  • 2-3 loko (5-20 sites): Role split — Research & Strategy (senior) / Execution on-page+content (fresher, SOPs pramane) / Reporting & Off-page. Fresher pan SOPs vanchi ne chali shake — etle j docs Gujlish + checklist format ma chhe.
  • 20+ sites: Site pods — dar person ne 5-8 sites nu ownership; senior review gate: nava pages publish pehla keyword map + brief check (03 → 06 Step 7).
  • SEO Executive RR (Engine dabba ma) = role definition no base.

3.9.F 5. Automation layers (dhime dhime add karo)

  • Level 1 (day 1): Client reporting — badha sites na tracking sheets ane Super Report auto-refresh (11 → 11.6)
  • Level 2: GSC data API thi pull → central sheet (Report 2 na Vercel pattern j — 11 → 02; 10 sites rule)
  • Level 3: Alerts — traffic drop 20%+ = auto flag (10 → 02 diagnosis flow sathe link)
  • Level 4: Content production pipeline — Semrush export → brief template → writer → QA checklist → publish (03 → 06 na steps j batch ma)
  • Rule: automation reporting mate — decision human j le (golden rule: data pehla, opinion pachhi, pan final call manas nu).

3.9.G 6. Quality gates (100 sites par pan same)

  • Ek keyword-intent = ek URL (dar site, Master Keyword Map check)
  • Publish pehla on-page checklist pass (03 → 06 Step 8)
  • Thin/template-only pages across sites = na (doorway + scaled abuse banne risk)
  • Dar site par must-have pages + EEAT (05 → 01, 05 → 05)
  • Monthly: DA/backlink health (06 → 07) + maintenance routine (10 → 04) dar P1/P2 site par

3.9.H 7. Capacity math (planning mate)

  • Navi site launch: ~25-40 kalak (first month, SOPs follow karta)
  • Chalu P1 site: ~4-6 kalak/week; P2: ~2 kalak/week; P3: ~2 kalak/month
  • 1 full-time person ≈ 6-8 P1 sites KE 15-20 mixed sites. Aa thi vadhye = team ke tier drop.

3.9.I Common mistakes (aa bhool na karo)

  • Badha sites par same content/template wholesale copy (doorway/scaled abuse)
  • Tracking vagar sites launch karvi (kai kaam karyu e khbar j na pade)
  • Badha sites ne equal time (P1 suffer kare)
  • Site count vadharva mate quality gate skip karvu
  • Portfolio Master Sheet vagar 10+ sites manage karvi (bhul pakki)

3.9.J Checklist

  • Portfolio Master Sheet banaveli + badha sites ni rows
  • Per-site asset template set (folder, sheets, dashboard) ready
  • Dar site nu tier (P1/P2/P3) nakki
  • Navi site onboarding order print/linked (00 START HERE)
  • Quality gates team badha ne khabar
  • Capacity math pramane sites assign
