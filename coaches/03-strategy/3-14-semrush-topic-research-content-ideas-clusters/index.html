3.14.B SEMrush Topic Research — Step-by-Step

Goal: Find topic cards, competitor headlines, questions and related searches to plan useful SEO pages.

1. Prepare the main keyword

Take one important primary keyword from our high-volume keyword list. Prioritize terms relevant to Client A products: diamond blades, polishing pads, core bits, sinks and porcelain cutting tools.

Copy one keyword at a time. Open SEMrush → Topic Research, paste the keyword, select the target country, enter our website in “Search content on domain”, then run the search.

2. Review all four Topic Research views

Cards — collect every relevant topic/subtopic card.

Explorer — explore topic and subtopic details.

Overview — review topic summary and available headline, question and search information.

Mind Map — review how the main topic connects to related subtopics.

Review all four views for each primary keyword; save useful findings from each.

3. Process EVERY relevant card — repeat the full process

Open the first relevant card. Record its topic name and displayed Volume.

Click “Show more” to expand the card and reveal additional keyword/topic details.

Save all relevant keywords and displayed metrics, such as Volume, Difficulty and Topic Efficiency (where available).

Collect every relevant Headline and its source link.

Collect all Questions and Related Searches shown for the card.

For each relevant headline, click the arrow/link beside it and open the source page. Review the top 5 relevant competitor pages/results.

For each source, record URL, page title, headings/outline, key points, useful examples, product details and content gaps. Keep these research notes separate from our draft.

Repeat the same process for every relevant card. Do not stop after the first or highest-volume card.

4. Build our page from the research

Use the primary keyword as the main page topic and relevant card keywords as secondary/supporting terms.

Create a unique outline from the research. Cover important subtopics, answer relevant Questions and naturally address useful Related Searches.

Use competitor pages for research only. Do NOT copy paragraphs, wording, images or full content. Write original content, verify facts/specifications and use Client A’s actual products and expertise.

Add genuine FAQs where useful. Include related searches naturally in headings/body; avoid keyword stuffing.

Add relevant internal links to Client A collections, products and supporting blogs. Match page intent to products actually available.

5. Save every card and page in one tracking sheet

Sheet columnWhat to record
Primary keyword / CountrySeed keyword and selected target country
Card / Subtopic / VolumeEvery relevant card name and displayed volume
Keyword metricsKeyword, volume, difficulty, Topic Efficiency (if shown)
Headlines + source linksEach headline and its source URL
Questions / Related SearchesAll relevant questions and related searches
Top 5 competitor researchCompetitor title, URL, outline/key points and content gaps
Planned pageProposed title, URL/slug, page type and target keyword
StatusTo research / Brief ready / Drafting / Published / Updated

6. Final quality check

Important: This workflow supports broader, intent-matched content. It cannot guarantee 80% of a keyword’s traffic; rankings depend on competition, content quality, authority, search intent, technical SEO and ongoing performance.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • All relevant cards for the seed keyword have been processed.
  • Show more details, headlines and source links have been saved.
  • Questions and Related Searches have been captured.
  • Top 5 competitor sources have been reviewed for each selected headline.
  • Every planned page is logged in the same sheet to prevent duplicate topics and keyword cannibalization.

Output: topic cluster list - main topic + subtopics + questions, content calendar ma mukva layak.
