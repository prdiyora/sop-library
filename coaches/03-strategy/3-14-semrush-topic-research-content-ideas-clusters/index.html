🧭 3.14 — Semrush Topic Research - Content Ideas & Clusters

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

3.14.A Aa stop su chhe

Ek seed topic nakho etle Semrush related subtopics, questions ane headlines batave - content ideas ane topic clusters banavva mate. Headlines, questions ane related searches thi content calendar ready thay. Real run: Skyline Stone, 19 Nov 2026.

3.14.B Semrush Topic Research — step-by-step

Goal: topic cards, competitor headlines, questions ane related searches melavva — jethi useful SEO pages plan thai shake.

Step 1: Main keyword ready karo

Aapni high-volume keyword list mathi ek important primary keyword lo. Skyline Stone products sathe relevant terms ne priority aapo: diamond blades, polishing pads, core bits, sinks ane porcelain cutting tools.

Ek vaar ma ek j keyword copy karo. Semrush → Topic Research kholo, keyword paste karo, target country select karo, "Search content on domain" ma aapnu website nakho, pachhi search run karo.

Step 2: Topic Research na 4 views badha juo

  • Cards — badha relevant topic/subtopic cards collect karo.
  • Explorer — topic ane subtopic ni detail juo.
  • Overview — topic summary ane headline, question, search info juo.
  • Mind Map — main topic related subtopics sathe kevi rite connect thay chhe e juo.

Darek primary keyword mate 4e views juo; darek mathi useful findings save karo.

Step 3: DAREK relevant card process karo — akhu process repeat karo

  1. Pehlu relevant card kholo. Enu topic naam ane dekhato Volume note karo.
  2. "Show more" click karo jethi card expand thay ane vadhara na keyword/topic details dekhay.
  3. Badha relevant keywords ane metrics save karo: Volume, Difficulty, Topic Efficiency (je dekhay e).
  4. Badha relevant Headlines ane ena source links collect karo.
  5. Card ma dekhata badha Questions ane Related Searches collect karo.
  6. Darek relevant headline bagde nu arrow/link click kari ne source page kholo. Top 5 relevant competitor pages/results review karo.
  7. Darek source mate note karo: URL, page title, headings/outline, key points, useful examples, product details ane content gaps. Aa research notes aapna draft thi ALAG rakho.
  8. Same process darek relevant card mate repeat karo. Pehla ke highest-volume card pachhi ATAKVO NAHI.

Step 4: Research mathi aapnu page banao

  • Primary keyword ne main page topic banavo ane card na relevant keywords ne secondary/supporting terms banavo.
  • Research mathi unique outline banao. Important subtopics cover karo, relevant Questions na jawab aapo, ane useful Related Searches ne naturally address karo.
  • Competitor pages FAKT research mate. Paragraphs, wording, images ke content COPY NAHI. Original content lakho, facts/specifications verify karo, ane Skyline na actual products ane expertise vapro.
  • Useful hoy tya genuine FAQs umero. Related searches ne headings/body ma naturally vaporo; keyword stuffing nahi.
  • Skyline collections, products ane supporting blogs par relevant internal links umero. Page nu intent e products sathe match karvu joie je actually available chhe.

Step 5: Badha cards ane pages ek j tracking sheet ma save karo

Headlines + source linksDarek headline ane eno source URL
Questions / Related SearchesBadha relevant questions ane related searches
Top 5 competitor researchCompetitor title, URL, outline/key points ane content gaps
Planned pageProposed title, URL/slug, page type ane target keyword
StatusTo research / Brief ready / Drafting / Published / Updated

Step 6: Final quality check

Important: Aa workflow intent-matched content banavva ma madad kare chhe. E keyword na traffic no 80% hisso garantee nathi — ranking competition, content quality, authority, search intent, technical SEO ane ongoing performance par depend kare chhe.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Seed keyword na badha relevant cards process thay gaya.
  • Show more details, headlines ane source links save thay gaya.
  • Questions ane Related Searches capture thay gaya.
  • Darek selected headline mate top 5 competitor sources review thay gaya.
  • Darek planned page same sheet ma log thay gayu — duplicate topics ane keyword cannibalization rokva.

3.14.C Expected Output

Output: topic cluster list - main topic + subtopics + questions, content calendar ma mukva layak.
