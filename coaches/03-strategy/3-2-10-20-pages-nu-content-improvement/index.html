🧭 3.2 — 10-20 Pages nu Content Improvement

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Position 10-20 (second page) vala pages ne top 10 ma lavva mate title patse nahi

  • content j improve karvu padshe. Second page = Google page ne relevant mane chhe pan enough quality/depth nathi.

3.2.A Steps (pagla)

  1. GSC ma position 10-20 vala pages shodho.
  2. Top 3 competitor pages vanchho same keyword mate - temna ma su chhe je tamara ma nathi?
  3. Content depth vadharo: missing subtopics add karo ane navu value (data, examples, experience) - word count target nahi.
  4. Structure improve karo: H2/H3 proper hierarchy, FAQ section add karo, internal links add karo (3-5).
  5. Freshness signal: date update, new examples, latest data add karo.
  6. Media add karo: images, screenshots, tables - text-only pages harai jay chhe.

3.2.B Kayu pan check karvu

  • Page target keyword nu intent satisfy kare chhe? (Informational query par product page hoy to flip karo)
  • Core Web Vitals pass chhe? (On-Page point 4 juvo)
  • Page ne internal links male chhe? Na hoy to 2-3 relevant internal links aapo; trusted sources ne 1-2 outbound citations; backlinks mate Off-Page SOP juo

3.2.C Checklist

  • 10-20 position vala pages ni list taiyar
  • Competitor comparison complete (shu missing chhe te list karo)
  • Depth
  • structure + freshness improves karela
  • 1 month ma position re-check scheduled
