🧭 3.4 — Blog Strategy (Category thi Blog sudhi)

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Blog topic shodhvathi publish ane Google indexing sudhi - full Semrush-first SOP
Updated 19 Nov 2026 - actual Semrush run par based (example: Skyline Stone Products, diamond blades, US market).

3.4.A Blog shu mate?

  • Blog = long-tail keywords, internal linking ane EEAT nu engine.
  • Random blogs nahi - category/product sathe linked blogs j sales lave.
  • Har blog ek problem ke question solve kare (search intent clear).

3.4.B Step 1 - Keyword Magic Tool ma blog topic shodho

Tool path: SEO → Keyword Research → Keyword Magic Tool

  • Semrush ma Keyword Magic Tool kholo.
  • Seed keyword enter karo - tamari category ke product sathe linked. Example: "diamond blade".
  • Country select karo (example: United States).
  • "Questions" filter lagao - loko je questions search kare chhe e j best blog topics chhe.
  • List ma Volume ane KD % jovo. Pick rule: KD 0-15 (easy) ane Volume 100+ hoy to perfect first blog.

Real run - 19 Nov 2026, US database, seed "diamond blade" (11,487 keywords madadthi Questions view):

  • Question keyword | Volume | KD %
  • how to dress a diamond blade | 260 | 2
  • can a diamond blade cut metal | 210 | 3
  • how to sharpen a diamond blade | 210 | 13
  • what is a diamond blade | 210 | 8
  • what are diamond blades | 210 | 24

Pick: "how to dress a diamond blade" - KD 2 (easiest) ane Volume 260. Low KD pehla, high KD pachhi.

3.4.C Step 2 - Keyword Overview thi topic validate karo

Tool path: SEO → Keyword Research → Keyword Overview

  • Picked keyword enter karo, country select karo.
  • 3 things check karo: Volume (260 chhe), KD % (2% = "Very easy"), Intent (Informational = blog mate perfect; Transactional hoy to e product/category page mate chhe, blog mate nahi).
  • Nichhe SERP Analysis section ma "Update metrics" click karo - 2-5 min lage, wait karo.
  • SERP jovo: top 10 ma blog articles chhe ke forums/videos?

Real run - "how to dress a diamond blade" SERP: fakt 189 results. #1 defusco.com no blog (Page AS 4), pachhi forum thread, Facebook post, Reddit thread, video page ane tiletools.com no blog. Weak SERP = navu blog easily rank kari shake.

  • Rule: top 10 ma forums, Reddit, Facebook ke videos hoy = content gap, green signal.
  • Badha strong blogs hoy ane KD high hoy = pacho Keyword Magic Tool ma jai ne bijo topic pick karo.

3.4.D Step 3 - Topic Finder thi next blogs nu cluster banao

Tool path: Content → Topic Finder

  • Seed keyword enter karo (diamond blade), Audience location select karo (United States).
  • "Find topics" click karo - topics collect thava ma 5 min sudhi lai shake.
  • "Low-hanging fruit" label vala topics pehla pick karo - low KD + high volume.

Real run - 19 Nov 2026, US:

  • Topic | Type | Keywords | Total Volume | Avg KD
  • concrete saw blades | Low-hanging fruit | 22 | 5.4K | 8%
  • tile blade for grinder | Low-hanging fruit | 19 | 4.6K | 8%
  • Balanced growth topics: grinder blade, tile saw blade, diamond blade, 14 diamond blade, diamond saw blades, concrete blade for grinder

Har topic niche ready title ideas made chhe (example: "Choosing the Right Concrete Saw Blade", "How to Use a Grinder for Tiles"). Aa topics Content Calendar (3.5) ma add karo - ek topic = ek blog.

3.4.E Step 4 - Competitor blog analysis + blog spec nakki karo

  • SERP na top 2-3 blog articles kholo ane darek nu word count check karo (page select all kari ne wordcounter.net ma paste karo).
  • Darek blog ma su cover karyu chhe te note karo - headings, steps, images, FAQ.

Real run - "how to dress a diamond blade" SERP na competitor blogs:

  • Competitor blog | Word count
  • defusco.com - Diamond Blade Sharpening (#1) | ~1,800
  • toolsliving.com - How to Sharpen a Diamond Blade | ~1,300
  • tiletools.com - Blade maintenance blog | ~900

Rules:

  • Length rule: tamaro blog top competitor ne match ke beat karvo joiye - content ma, fakt words ma nahi. Example: #1 competitor 1,800 words chhe to target 1,500-2,000 words + tena thi vadhare useful points.
  • Image spec (fixed): 1 banner image (featured image, blog title sathe) + 3 photos content ni andar (steps, products, real examples).
  • Real photos vapro - stock photos ochha ma ochha.
  • Headings competitor karta vadhare clear ane step-by-step rakho.

3.4.F Step 5 - Blog lakho (rules)

  • Primary keyword: title ma, first paragraph ma ane 1-2 headings ma.
  • 3-5 internal links (related category / products / blogs). Blog → category page link jaruri (sales flow).
  • End ma FAQ section (AEO/GEO mate).
  • Word count ranking factor nathi - topic fully cover karo, navu data ke real examples add karo.

3.4.G Step 6 (last) - Publish + Google Search Console ma Request Indexing

  • Blog publish karo - correct category ma, banner image + 3 photos sathe.
  • Publish thayu etle live URL copy karo.
  • Google Search Console kholo → upar URL Inspection bar ma live URL paste karo → Enter.
  • "Request Indexing" click karo - 1-2 min ma confirm thay.
  • Aa step kyarey skip na karo - indexing request vagar navo blog Google ma weeks sudhi na dekhay.

3.4.H Publishing frequency

  • Navi site: week ma 2-4 blogs consistent (quality over quantity).
  • Juna blogs dar 6 mahine review karo - outdated hoy ke traffic ghatyu hoy to refresh (ranking ni guarantee nahi).

3.4.I Checklist

  • Blog topic category/products sathe linked chhe.
  • Keyword Magic Tool ma Questions filter thi topic pick karyo (low KD + volume).
  • Keyword Overview ma Volume, KD %, Intent ane SERP validate karya.
  • Topic Finder ma next topics ane title ideas notdhya (Content Calendar ma add).
  • Top 2-3 competitor blogs na word count check karya ane target length nakki kari.
  • Spec ready: 1 banner image + 3 photos.
  • Blog ma 3-5 internal links + FAQ section chhe.
  • Publish pachhi Google Search Console ma Request Indexing karyu.
