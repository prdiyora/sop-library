🧭 3.4 — Blog Strategy (Category thi Blog sudhi)

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Blog = long-tail keywords, internal linking ane EEAT nu engine. Pan random blogs nahi - category/product sathe linked blogs j sales lave. (Aa point ma Priyank na juna "Ecommerce Blog SEO Workflow" doc nu merge karel chhe.)

3.4.A 3-Step Blog Workflow (puri detail + rules + example: 03 Strategy → 07 Ecommerce Blog Workflow)

  1. Step 1 - Title: Category page, tena products, GSC queries ane People Also Ask joi ne 8-10 blog titles jate shortlist karo, pachhi client/manager pase review karavo. Pehla title nakki thay to j sacha products ane keywords male.
  2. Step 2 - Products + Keywords: Final title par website mathi j Top 5 products ane target keywords levo - sacha internal links sales lave.
  3. Step 3 - Final Blog: Title
  • products + keywords + rules saathe blog lakhavo.

3.4.B Blog rules (koi pan website type mate)

  • Har blog ek problem/question solve kare (search intent clear)
  • Length: topic fully cover karo - word count ranking factor nathi; competitors thi vadhare useful banavo (navu data, examples, experience)
  • Har blog ma 3-5 internal links (related products/pages/blogs)
  • FAQ section end ma (AEO/GEO mate)
  • Real images/examples vapro - stock photos ochha ma ochha vapro

3.4.C Publishing ni frequency

  • Navi site: week ma 2-4 blogs consistent (quality over quantity)
  • Juna blogs dar 6 mahine review karo - outdated hoy ke traffic ghatyu hoy to refresh (ranking ni guarantee nahi)
  • Blog → category page link jaruri (sales flow)

3.4.D Checklist

  • Blog topics category/products sathe linked chhe
  • 3-step workflow follow thay chhe (title → products/keywords → final)
  • Har blog ma internal links + FAQ chhe
  • Cadence fixed ane calendar ma chhe
