🧭 3.4 — Blog Strategy (Category thi Blog sudhi)

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Blog topic shodhvathi lai ne blog publish karva ane Google ma indexing karava sudhi ni full SOP.

Updated: 19 Nov 2026 Based on real Semrush data. Example: Client A Products, diamond blades, United States market.

3.4.A Blog shu mate banavvo?

Blog no main goal chhe loko na questions na jawab aapva ane website par sacha visitors lavva.

Blog thi 3 fayda thay:

  • Long-tail keywords: Loko je specific questions Google ma search kare chhe, tena par rank thavani tak male.

  • Internal linking: Blog mathi category ane product pages par links api shakay.

  • E-E-A-T: Real experience ane useful information thi website par trust vadhe.

Main rule: Random topic par blog na lakho. Blog tamari category ke product sathe related hovo joiye.

Darek blog ek problem solve kare ke ek question no clear jawab aape. Aathi visitor ne help male ane te tamara product sudhi pan pahochi shake.

3.4.B Step 1: Keyword Magic Tool ma blog topic shodho

Tool path: Semrush → SEO → Keyword Research → Keyword Magic Tool

  1. Semrush kholo ane Keyword Magic Tool ma jao.

  2. Seed keyword lakho. Aa tamari category ke product nu main keyword hovu joiye. Example: diamond blade.

  3. Country select karo. Example: United States.

  4. Questions filter par click karo. Ahiya loko Google ma puchhta questions malse.

  5. Darek keyword nu Volume ane KD % check karo.

Blog topic pick karvano rule:

  • KD 0–15 = relatively easy keyword.

  • Volume 100+ = first blog mate saro target.

  • Low KD ane saru Volume hoy te keyword pehla lo.

Country: United States Seed keyword: diamond blade Keyword Magic Tool ma 11,487 keywords na data mathi Questions view check karyo.

Yaad rakho: Pehla low KD keywords par blog lakho. Pachhi high KD topics par kaam karo.

3.4.C Step 2: Keyword Overview thi topic check karo

Tool path: Semrush → SEO → Keyword Research → Keyword Overview

Step 1 ma pick karelo keyword ahiya check karvano chhe.

  1. Keyword Overview kholo.

  2. Keyword how to dress a diamond blade enter karo.

  3. Country United States select karo.

  4. Volume, KD % ane Search Intent check karo.

Aa 3 vastu samjo

MetricExampleShu samajvu?
Volume260Mahine ketli vaar keyword search thay chhe teno estimate
KD %2% — Very easyAa keyword par rank karvu relatively easy hoi shake
IntentInformationalLoko jawab ke mahiti shodhe chhe; blog mate saro intent

Intent rule:

  • Informational = blog mate saru.

  • Transactional = usually product ke category page mate vadhu suitable.

SERP Analysis check karo

Keyword Overview ma niche SERP Analysis section sudhi jao.

  1. Update metrics click karo.

  2. Data update thava 2–5 minutes lagi shake.

  3. Update pachhi Google na top 10 results check karo.

  4. Juo ke tya blogs, forums, Reddit, Facebook ke videos chhe.

Real SERP example

Keyword: how to dress a diamond blade

Semrush ma aa run darmiyan fakt 189 results dekhaya.

Top results ma aa pages hata:

  • #1 defusco.com no blog — Page AS 4.

  • Forum thread.

  • Facebook post.

  • Reddit thread.

  • Video page.

  • tiletools.com no blog.

Aa SERP ma ghana strong blogs na hata. Etle nava ane useful blog mate opportunity dekhay chhe.

Decision rule:

  • Top 10 ma forums, Reddit, Facebook ke videos vadhu hoy to content gap hoi shake. Aa green signal chhe, pan ranking ni guarantee nathi.

  • Top 10 ma badha strong blogs hoy ane KD pan high hoy to Keyword Magic Tool ma pacha jao ane bijo topic shodho.

Tool path: Semrush → Content → Topic Finder

Ek blog topic malya pachhi tena related bija topics pan shodhvana chhe.

  1. Topic Finder kholo.

  2. Seed keyword diamond blade lakho.

  3. Audience location United States select karo.

  4. Find topics click karo.

  5. Results aavta 5 minutes sudhi lagi shake.

Results ma Low-hanging fruit label vala topics check karo. Aa topics ma generally low KD ane saru search volume hoy chhe.

Aa rite related blogs nu cluster banshe. Pachhi aa blogs mathi category ane product pages ne internal links api shakasho.

3.4.E Step 4: Competitor blogs check karo ane blog spec banao

Topic final thay pachhi Google na top blogs check karvana chhe.

  1. SERP mathi top 2–3 blog articles kholo.

  2. Darek blog no text select karo ane wordcounter.net ma paste karo.

  3. Word count note karo.

  4. Blog ma kya headings, steps, images ane FAQ chhe te juo.

  5. Competitor karta tamaro blog kya vadhu useful bani shake te nakki karo.

Real competitor example

Blog length rule

Tamaro blog top competitor jetlo ke tena karta vadhu useful hovo joiye. Fakt vadhare words lakhvathi ranking nathi malti.

Example: #1 competitor na 1,800 words chhe.

Tamaro target 1,500–2,000 words rakhi shako. Pan tema competitor karta vadhu clear steps, examples ane useful information hovi joiye.

Real photos vadhu vapro. Stock photos bane etla ochha vapro.

Headings simple rakho. Reader ne pehla shu karvu ane pachhi shu karvu te step-by-step samjay evu lakho.

3.4.F Step 5: Blog content lakho

Have keyword, competitor research ane image spec ready chhe. Etle blog lakhvano chhe.

1. H1 ane title

  • H1 = blog nu main title.

  • H1 ma primary keyword rakho.

  • Akha blog ma ek j H1 hovu joiye.

2. Introduction

  • Pehla 100 words ma reader na question no direct answer aapo.

  • Primary keyword naturally lakho.

  • Lambi ane unnecessary introduction na lakho.

3. Headings

Competitor analysis ma malela important topics ne H2 ane H3 ma cover karo.

  • H2 = main section.

  • H3 = H2 ni andar no subtopic.

  • Steps ne sacha order ma samjavo.

4. Writing style

  • Simple words vapro.

  • Paragraph 2–4 lines na rakho.

  • Jarur hoy tya bullet lists vapro.

  • Mobile par pan easily vanchi shakay evu lakho.

5. Keywords

Primary keyword ahiya naturally aavvo joiye:

  • Blog title ma.

  • First paragraph ma.

  • 1–2 headings ma.

Secondary keywords body content ma naturally vapro.

Keyword stuffing na karo. Ek j keyword vaaramvaar lakhvani jarur nathi.

6. Internal links

Darek blog ma 3–5 internal links add karo.

Links ahiya api shako:

  • Related category page.

  • Related product page.

  • Related blog.

Blog → Category page link jaruri chhe. Aa link thi reader tamara products sudhi jai shake.

7. Images

Step 4 ni spec pramane:

  • 1 banner image.

  • 3 content photos.

  • Darek image mate proper alt text.

8. Useful content

Word count Google no direct ranking factor nathi.

Etle topic ne pura samjavo. Jya possible hoy tya real experience, navu data ane examples add karo.

Goal chhe: Reader ne competitor blog karta vadhu saral ane useful jawab male.

3.4.G Step 6: E-E-A-T pramane blog banao

E-E-A-T = Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness.

Aa Google na Quality Rater Guidelines ma vaprato concept chhe. E-E-A-T direct ranking factor nathi, pan aa principles thi helpful ane trustworthy content banavva ma madad male chhe.

E-E-A-TSimple meaningBlog ma shu add karvu?
ExperienceTame pote kaam karyu chheReal photos, potana examples ane results
ExpertiseTame topic sari rite samjo chhoCorrect facts, technical terms ane author bio
AuthoritativenessTamari brand ke author ni reputationAuthor page, relevant mentions ane consistent brand information
TrustworthinessTamari mahiti par bharoso kari shakaySacha facts, source links, real contact details; fake claims nahi

Experience

Fakt book jevi definition na lakho.

Example: diamond blade dress karta hoy to teni real photo ane tamaro experience aapo.

"Ame aa method try kari" jevi vaat fakt kharekhar test karyu hoy to j lakho.

Expertise

Technical information sachi hovi joiye.

Example: Dressing ane Sharpening vachche shu difference chhe te sachi rite samjavo.

Author nu naam ane short bio add karo.

Authoritativeness

Website ane author ni reputation build karo.

Author page, bija relevant sites par mentions ane badhi jagyae same brand information rakho.

Trustworthiness

Koi claim karo to tena mate source link aapo.

Website par real contact details rakho. Fake results, fake reviews ke khota claims kyarey na vapro.

Blog E-E-A-T checklist:

  • Author name dekhay.

  • Published/Updated date dekhay.

  • Real photos add karela hoy.

  • Important facts mate source links hoy.

  • Potanu real experience ke data add karelu hoy.

Blog ready thay etle direct publish na karo. Pehla badhu check karo.

3.4.I Step 8: Meta title ane description lakho

Blog mate alag SEO title ane meta description set karvana chhe.

Meta title rules

  • Primary keyword beginning ma rakho.

  • Target length 50–60 characters.

  • Darek blog mate unique title lakho.

Formula:

Primary Keyword - Faydo/Modifier | Brand

Meta description rules

  • Target length 150–160 characters.

  • Primary keyword add karo.

  • Reader ne shu faydo malshe te samjavo.

  • CTA (action karva mate nu short message) add karo.

  • Darek blog mate unique description lakho. Copy-paste na karo.

Meta description direct ranking factor nathi. Pan saru description search result ma dekhay to reader click kare tevi chance vadhi shake.

Real example

Primary keyword: how to dress a diamond blade

Meta title:

How to Dress a Diamond Blade: 5-Min Fix | Client A

Meta description:

Blade slow ke glazed cut kare chhe? Dressing stone ane wet saw tricks thi diamond blade ne 5 minutes ma sharp karo - step-by-step guide.

Aa title ane description example chhe. Live mukta pehla character count ane claim sacho chhe ke nahi te check karo.

3.4.J Step 9 (Last): Blog publish karo ane GSC ma Request Indexing karo

Content ane quality testing complete thay pachhi blog live karvano chhe.

Part 1 — Blog publish

  1. Blog ne sachi category ma add karo.

  2. Banner image ane 3 content photos check karo.

  3. Meta title ane description set karo.

  4. Badha internal links check karo.

  5. Publish click karo.

  6. Live thayela blog no URL copy karo.

Part 2 — Google Search Console (GSC)

  1. Google Search Console kholo.

  2. Uparna URL Inspection bar ma live blog URL paste karo.

  3. Enter dabavo.

  4. Request Indexing par click karo.

  5. Confirmation aave tya sudhi juo. Aa process ma lagbhag 1–2 minutes lagi shake.

Important: Aa step SOP mathi skip na karo.

Request Indexing Google ne nava URL ni jaan karava mate chhe. Pan aa request thi immediate indexing ke ranking ni guarantee nathi. Request vagar pan Google page shodhi shake chhe; kyarek nava page discover thava ane index thava ma weeks lagi shake.

3.4.K Publishing frequency

Navi website mate

Week ma 2–4 quality blogs publish karvano target rakho.

Pan quality ochhi kari ne fakt vadhu blogs na mukva.

Regular ane useful content vadhu important chhe.

Juna blogs mate

Darek 6 mahine old blogs review karo.

Aa vastu check karo:

  • Information outdated thai gai chhe?

  • Traffic ghatyo chhe?

  • Nava examples, photos ke data add karvani jarur chhe?

Jarur hoy to blog update/refresh karo. Refresh karvathi ranking improve thashe evi guarantee nathi.
