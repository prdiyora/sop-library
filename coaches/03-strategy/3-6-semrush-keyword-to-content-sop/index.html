🧭 3.6 — Semrush Keyword to Content SOP

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Version 1.1 | Nov 2026 | Blog, Category, Collection, Service — badha page type mate

Aa SOP no goal: koi pan team member (fresher hoy to pan) ek seed keyword thi sharu kari ne — keyword research, clustering, mapping, SERP analysis, content brief, internal linking ane measurement — same proper process thi kari sake.

Pehla 3 imandar vaat (vanchya vagar SOP use na karvi) 1. Koi pan SOP 'super rank guaranteed' nathi aapti. Ranking ma content quality, site authority (backlinks), technical health, intent match ane competition — badhu kaam kare chhe. Aa SOP tamaru chance khub vadhare chhe, guarantee nahi. 2. Original notes ma "competitor no content 90% same rakho, slug 10% change karo" lakhyu hatu — aa step SOP mathi kaadhi nakhyu chhe. Google na spam policies ma 'scraped content' ane 'scaled content abuse' clearly che: biji site no content thoda badlav sathe republish karvo, ke 2-3 sites nu content jodi ne kai navu add na karvu = spam. Teni jagya e 'SERP study → better page banavo' step mukyu chhe (Step 6). [CONFIRMED — Google Search Central spam policies] 3. KD% ane Volume Semrush na estimate chhe, Google na number nahi. Decision mate useful chhe, pan GSC no real data aave pachhi ene j priority aapvi.

3.6.C Step 1 — Project ni taiyari (10 min)

Keyword shodhta pehla aa vaat sheet ma lakhi do. Aa vagar keyword research disha vagar nu thay.

  1. Target country: Client kya vechhe chhe? (US / UK / India). Semrush ma database aaj country no select karvo — Global volume par decision na levo.
  2. Business goal: Sale joie chhe (product/category) ke traffic + trust (blog)?
  3. Site type: Shopify ecommerce / service / local / blog.
  4. Existing pages: Site par pehla thi kaya pages chhe — sitemap ke Shopify admin mathi list kaadho. (Step 5 ma cannibalization check mate joie.)
  5. [v1.1 navu] Seasonality: Google Trends ma seed keyword nu 5-year trend (target country) juo. Keyword seasonal hoy (Christmas gifts, back to school, Diwali) to page season thi 2-3 mahina pehla publish karo — Semrush nu volume 12 mahina nu average chhe, peak mahina ma demand ghanu vadhare hoi shake. [CONFIRMED — Semrush volume = monthly average]

3.6.D Step 2 — Seed keyword research

[v1.1 navu] 2A. Existing site hoy to pehla GSC (free ane sauthi accurate)

  1. GSC → Performance → Search results → last 3 months → Queries tab.
  2. Filter: Position 8 thi 30 + impressions vadhare. Aa keywords par Google tamari site ne already relevant maane chhe — sauthi jaldi result aa j aape.
  3. Query par click → Pages tab: kayo page rank thay chhe te note karo (Step 5 ma Update decision mate).
  4. Aa list 'GSC Opportunities' tab ma muko. Semrush data sathe match karo — GSC real chhe, Semrush estimate.

2B. Keyword Overview (Semrush)

  1. Semrush → Keyword Overview → seed keyword nakho (example: meal planner template) → database = target country (example: US).
  2. Jovu: Volume (target country nu), KD%, Intent, CPC, SERP features (images, PAA, AI Overview, shopping).
  3. Niche 'Questions' list joi lo — aa FAQ / H2 ideas mate kaam aavse. Export kari lo.

2C. Keyword Magic Tool (Keyword ideas)

  1. 'Keyword ideas' → View all → Keyword Magic Tool khulse. Database = target country.
  2. Broad match tab rakho (sauthi vadhare ideas). Example result: 748 keywords, total volume 18,720, average KD 26%.
  3. Filters lagavo: Volume ≥ 50 (niche ma 20 pan chale), KD% ≤ tamari site ni capacity (navi/low authority site mate 0-30 thi sharu karo), Language = English.
  4. Left side na groups (example: printable, free, weekly, family, google sheets) joi lo — aa j pachhi cluster ideas bane chhe.
  5. Export → sheet ma 'Raw Keywords' tab ma paste karo.
Senior tip KD ni limit fix number nathi. Strong DA/backlinks vali site 40-50 KD par pan rank kari shake; navi site mate 0-25 thi start karvu safe chhe. Pehla tamari site kaya KD na keywords par already page 1 par chhe e GSC + Semrush Organic Research ma juo — e tamari 'safe KD range' chhe.
[v1.1 navu] Semrush na hoy to — free alternatives • Google Search Console: existing site mate sauthi saro source (real queries). • Google Keyword Planner: volume ranges (exact number ad spend vagar nathi male), country select thay. • Google autocomplete, People Also Ask, Related searches: long-tail ane question keywords. • Google Trends: seasonality ane rising topics. • KD nu manual check: top 10 ma kon chhe juo — moti brands/marketplaces j hoy to agharu; forums, nana blogs, juna pages hoy to chance chhe. Process same rahe — fakt data sources badlay.

3.6.E Step 3 — Clean + priority (sauthi important filter)

Original notes ma 'highest volume + lowest KD' lakhyu hatu — e sachu chhe, pan adhuru. Aa filter lagavo:

CheckShu jovuExample
RelevanceBusiness ne kaam nu chhe? Je vechta nathi teno keyword kaadhoMeal planner app na hoy to 'meal planner app download' kaadho
IntentKeyword nu intent tamara page type sathe match thay chhe?'buy meal planner notebook' = product/collection; 'how to meal plan' = blog
Business valueAa traffic sale/lead aape? (CPC pan signal chhe — vadhu CPC = advertisers ne aa traffic thi kamai)'free meal planner template' traffic aape, sale ochhu; 'meal planner notebook' sale nu
VolumeTarget country volumeUS: 2,400
KD %Tamari safe range ma?26% → OK

Simple priority score (sheet ma column banavo):

Priority = Business Value (1-3) × Intent match (1-3) × Opportunity (1-3, volume vadhu + KD ochhu = 3)

Score 18-27 = pehla banavo, 8-17 = pachhi, 1-7 = skip. Aa formula andha thai ne na vaparvu — client e koi product push karvu hoy to te upar aave. [v1.1 navu] GSC ma position 8-30 vala keywords ne +1 Opportunity aapo — tya page already chhe.

3.6.F Step 4 — Keyword Strategy Builder: Topic, Pillar, Cluster

  1. Semrush → Keyword Strategy Builder → seed keyword(s) nakho → country select → 2-3 min ma SERP analyze thase.
  2. All Pages / Topics tab: dar topic ma ek pillar page + ena cluster pages dekhase. Example: Topic: printable meal planner templates — Total volume 97,590, Avg KD 32.52%.
  3. Dar topic nu naam + volume + KD sheet ma 'Topic Map' tab ma save karo.
  4. Topic par click → pillar page keyword (example: meal planner template) par click → tena related keywords + KD + volume malse. Aa pillar page na secondary keywords chhe.
  5. 'Potential SERP competitors and content references' na 10 URLs copy karo — Step 6 ma vaparvana chhe.
  6. Puro topic (pillar + clusters + keywords) ek sathe sheet format ma export kari lo — data accurate rahe.
Semrush na grouping par blind trust na karvo — SERP overlap test Be keyword ek page ma jase ke alag? Google ma bane search karo (same country, incognito). Top 10 ma 3-4 ke vadhu same URLs hoy → Google ene same topic maane chhe → ek j page. Overlap 0-2 hoy → alag page. Tool khota grouping kare tyare aa test j final decision chhe.

5D. Slug (URL) na rules

  • Pillar no slug = primary keyword nu short version: /meal-planner-template/ (notes ma kahyu tem highest-volume relevant keyword — sachu, pan intent match pehla).
  • Cluster slug = eno potano primary keyword: /printable-weekly-meal-planner/
  • Short, lowercase, hyphen, no dates/years (refresh vakhte URL badalvo na pade).
  • Competitor no slug copy karvani jarur nathi — slug ranking ma nano role chhe; tamaro keyword clear hoy e j poorto chhe. [CONFIRMED that URL words are a minor factor per Google]
  • Juna page nu slug badalvu hoy to 301 redirect farjiyat.

Information gain ideas (ahiya thi j rank aave chhe):

  • Potanu original template / printable / calculator (example: free downloadable meal planner PDF)
  • Real photos, real use example, client/product no first-hand experience
  • Comparison table, step-by-step, common mistakes je competitors e cover nathi karya
  • Ecommerce: size guide, material info, kaya age/use mate — real buyer questions

Teva keyword par: (1) H2 pachhi pehla 2-3 line ma sidho jawab, (2) clear definitions ane lists, (3) original data/experience je AI ne cite karvo pade. 3.6.M Step 11 — Keyword Gap + Competitor na pages

11A. Keyword Gap

  1. Semrush → Keyword Gap → tamari domain + 2-4 competitors (je tamara main keyword par top par chhe). Country = tamari fixed country.
  2. Tabs: Missing (competitors rank, tame nahi), Weak (tame pachhal), Untapped (ek competitor j rank kare).
  3. Step 3 nu priority filter lagavo → Step 4-9 nu same process.
  4. Sauthi pehla 'Weak' juo — tamaro page chhe j, fakt sudharvanu chhe.

11B. Competitor top pages (Organic Research)

  1. Semrush → Organic Research → competitor domain → country → Positions tab (example: 7,393 positions).
  2. Traffic column thi sort (high → low). Je pages vadhu traffic laave chhe e list karo.
  3. Pages tab pan juo — kaya page type (blog/collection/tool) competitor mate kaam kare chhe.
  4. Aa topics par tamaru potanu, vadhu saru page Step 5-9 thi banavo. Content ke slug copy karvani jarur nathi ane karvu pan nahi.
Kem copy nahi? (simple) Google ek j vaat 10 jagya e batavva nathi maangtu. Je page 90% competitor jevu hoy te Google mate 'duplicate jevu' chhe — kem rank kare? Ulti site par spam action nu risk. 3.6.U Keyword Magic Tool (juno tool) — step-by-step

Kam: seed keywords mathi clean, intent-matched keyword shortlist banavvi.

1. Legacy Magic Tool kholo

  1. SEO → Keyword Research → Keyword Magic Tool kholo (2.0 label VADU — e alg tool che, section V ma che).
  2. Ek seed keyword nakho ane target country select karo. Click Search.
  3. Report header ma seed ane country banne confirm karo.

Real run (19 Nov 2026, diamond blades, United States): All keywords 11,487. Sauthi mota groups: saw 2,862, cut 1,635, inch 790, tile 686, grinder 595, concrete 517. Sample rows: 'raimondi diamond blade resin dressing stone' — informational/commercial intent, volume 2,900, KD 0%, CPC $1.65; 'diamond blade' — informational, 1,900, KD 9%, CPC $2.05.

2. Pehla broad, pachhi narrow

  1. All/Broad Match, Phrase Match, Exact Match ane Related tabs review karo. Tooltips vancho — aa match labels research filters che, Google Ads campaign settings nahi.
  2. Broad ma variations shodho, Related ma adjacent meanings shodho. Lists compare karo — darek suggestion ma exact seed hoy evu assume na karo.

3. Groups ane subgroups process karo

  1. Left-hand groups juo, keyword count ke volume thi sort karo, relevant subgroups expand karo.
  2. Actual product range sathe relevant DAREK group process karo. Example: porcelain, granite, wet cutting, polishing ke size-related ideas fakt tyare j alag karo jyare live suggestions ane catalog support kare. Unrelated saws, brands ane materials kadho.

4. Questions ane intent

  1. Questions tab ma switch karo — explanatory needs shodho. Informational topics ne buying terms thi ALAG record karo.
  2. Example training pair: "diamond blades" = category page candidate; "how to choose a blade for porcelain" = guide candidate. Alag pages joie ke nahi e SERP check karya pachhi j decide karo.

5. Filters apply kari ne record karo

  1. Volume, KD, Intent, Include/Exclude ane advanced filters (word count, SERP features) vapro.
  2. Nano initial KD/volume filter review focus kare, pan ek threshold ne badha sites mate rule NA banavo.
  3. Domain-specific PKD fakt tyare vapro jyare correct domain entered hoy ane account supply kare. Paid Competitive Density ne organic KD na badle NA vapro.

6. Shortlist validate karo

  1. Final candidates mate Keyword Overview ane SERP results kholo. Actual country-specific metrics ane competing page types record karo.
  2. Same-intent variants ne ek page brief ma group karo. Spelling ke word-order variation dida page NA banavo. Duplicate topics avoid karva existing site ane mapping sheet check karo.

7. Save, export ane map

  1. Relevant rows select karo. Export ma All / This group / Selected — yogya scope choose karo ane row scope verify karo. XLSX/CSV export karo; grouping handoff mate helpful hoy to preserve karo.
  2. Tracker columns: Seed | Group/subgroup | Keyword | Country/date | Intent | Volume | KD/PKD | SERP features | Existing/proposed URL | Status | Rejection reason.
  3. Keywords save karva navu clearly named list vapro; bija nu list dekhay etle modify NA karo.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Badha relevant groups review karya, fakt first-page suggestions nahi.
  • Questions, buying intent ane irrelevant terms alag karya.
  • Final metrics ane SERPs Keyword Overview ma check karya.
  • Ek intent = ek suitable page.
  • Output: clean keyword groups Keyword Strategy Builder ane content briefs mate taiyar.

3.6.V Keyword Magic Tool 2.0 — step-by-step

Kam: semantic topics, Google keywords ane AI prompts sathe research karvi.

1. Exact 2.0 tool kholo

  1. SEO → Keyword Research → Keyword Magic Tool 2.0 kholo. Ek seed nakho, target country select karo, "Explore ideas" click karo.
  2. Results page par country fari confirm karo. Tool nu full name "Keyword Magic Tool 2.0" j che.

2. Seed ane subtopic metrics alag samjo

Seed na metrics (Volume, KD %, Global Volume, Intent) ane adjacent subtopic na totals (Total Volume, SEO Volume, Keywords, AI Volume, Prompts) ALG che — mix na karo.

3. Topics ane subtopics expand karo

  1. Left topic list vapro. Relevant topics expand karo, pachhi subtopic select karo — table filter thase. Relevance ke Keywords count thi sort karo.
  2. Checked search ma construction & power tools aayu, subtopics: diamond blade tools, tile cutting blades, quartz cutting blades. Darek ne real catalog sathe validate karya pachhi j rakho. Semantic grouping ma seed na words vagar na terms pan hoy shake.

4. Keywords review karo

  1. Keywords view ma collect karo: Keyword, Relevance, Volume, Intent, KD %, SERP Features. All/Questions tabs, Filter box, Volume/KD filters ane Add filter vapro.
  2. Seed ke subtopic na details kholi ne Top SEO Competitors juo. Topic nu combined volume ane ek page jeeti shake evu demand — e saman NAHI.

5. Prompts alag review karo

  1. Prompts tab ma switch karo ane matching relevant topic/subtopic shodho. Prompt, AI platform, response, brands ane sources record karo. Context mate "View full response" juo.
  2. Platforms filter karo (ChatGPT, AI Overview, AI Mode, Gemini — available hoy tya). AI Volume = modeled prompt demand, Google search volume NAHI. Factual answer vaparta pehla reliable product sources sathe check karo.

6. Ek intent = ek useful page

  1. Subtopic na keywords ane prompts banne review karo, pachhi decide karo kayu existing ke navu page need answer kare.
  2. Example: porcelain blade selection queries + AI questions ek original buyer guide ma fit thay shake; product-size query ne product/collection page joie. Darek variation mate thin pages NA banavo, ane unrelated intents ek page ma promise NA karo.

7. Save ane export — right scope

  1. Keyword rows select karo → Save keywords → Save selected keywords (list joie to). Exports ma relevant topic/subtopic ke selected rows choose karo → Export.
  2. Keyword ane prompt exports ALAG rakho. Tracker: Seed | Topic/subtopic | Country/date | Keyword or prompt | Platform | Metrics with units | Reference source | Page mapping | Action/status.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Seed figures ane subtopic totals alag rakhela.
  • Google keyword demand ane AI prompt demand alag rakhela.
  • Relevant topics banne views ma check karya.
  • Responses ane source pages claims vaparta pehla review karya.
  • Output: semantic topic plan je keywords ane prompts ne useful pages sathe link kare.

3.6.W Keyword Strategy Builder — step-by-step

Kam: Seed keywords → Topics → Pillar pages → Subpages → Final verification.

1. 5 sudhi seed keywords taiyar karo

  1. SEMrush → Keyword Strategy Builder kholo.
  2. Ek batch ma 5 sudhi RELATED seed keywords taiyar karo — same product/category ke search-intent group ma. Example: "miss nella", "non toxic kids makeup", "non toxic makeup for kids", "kids non toxic makeup".
  3. Seeds paste karo, target country/database select karo, "Create" click karo.
  4. Limit note: workflow ma ~30 keyword creations per day nu limit che. Usage track karo. Limit puri thay to reset ni wait karo ke authorized access mangvo.

2. Preparation ni wait karo, pachhi ek seed select karo

  2. Results aave tyare strategy name, database/country, currency ane seed keywords verify karo.

5. Potential SERP Competitors ane Content References research karo

  1. Topic/page mate "Potential SERP competitors and content references" kholo.
  2. Listed URLs ane Domain Authority Score (AS) juo. Ocho AS = easy competition SHAKE, pan fakt ek signal j.
  3. Top 5 relevant URLs kholo. Darek page keyword ane search intent match kare e confirm karo.
  4. Record karo: competitor title, URL, page type, headings, subheadings, questions answered, examples ane content gaps.
  5. Competitor pages fakt RESEARCH mate. Pages, wording, images ke content copy NA karo. Client na real products, expertise ane services thi original, accurate content lakho. Client sathe unrelated topics na pages NA banavo.

6. Pillar page banao

  1. Pillar page nu main keyword = primary target.
  2. Relevant associated keywords ne suitable sections ma naturally map karo; irrelevant phrases force NA karo, keyword-stuffing NA karo.
  3. Structure: clear title/H1, introduction, logical H2/H3 sections, helpful explanations, product/service info, useful FAQs ane relevant internal links.
  4. Genuine People Also Ask questions answer karo ane relevant Related Searches naturally cover karo. Images/video fakt tyare j umero jyare reader ne help kare.
  5. Competitor research mathi missing information shodho ane vadhare useful original page banao.
  6. Client na relevant collections, products ane supporting pages ne link karo. Content brief, draft URL ane final page URL tracker ma save karo.

7. Darek subpage same workflow thi process karo

  1. Current topic hetal subpage dropdown/list kholo.
  2. Pehli subpage kholi ne capture karo: page keyword, supporting keywords, Intent, KD %, Volume, SERP features.
  3. Ae subpage na Potential SERP Competitors review karo; top 5 relevant reference URLs kholi ne structure ane content gaps record karo.
  4. Original subpage banao je potani search intent satisfy kare ane relevant keywords naturally cover kare.
  5. Subpage ne parent pillar page sathe link karo; sibling links fakt useful hoy tya.
  6. Topic ni DAREK subpage mate repeat karo, pachhi next topic: pehla pillar page, pachhi eni badhi subpages.

11. Important rules

  • Ek seed keyword PURO complete karya pachhi j bija par jao.
  • All Pages hetal DAREK topic mate pillar + subpage workflow repeat karo.
  • Badha relevant associated keywords naturally map karo; irrelevant keywords page copy ma stuff NA karo.
  • Tool nu page count planning structure che — thin ke duplicate pages publish karvano requirement NAHI. Same search intent hoy to pages combine karo.
  • Client na actual products, services ane audience sathe relevant pages j banao. Tire-repair examples illustrative che.
  • Competitor content copy NA karo — structure ane coverage research karo, pachhi original verified content lakho.
  • Koi pan process 80% search traffic nu guarantee nathi aapti. Rankings intent, competition, content quality, authority, technical SEO, internal links ane ongoing measurement par depend kare.
  • Research date record karo — volumes, KD, SERP features ane competitor results badlay shake.
