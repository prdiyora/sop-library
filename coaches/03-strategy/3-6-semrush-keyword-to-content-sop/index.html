Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Aa SOP no goal: koi pan team member (fresher hoy to pan) ek seed keyword thi sharu kari ne — keyword research, clustering, mapping, SERP analysis, content brief, internal linking ane measurement — same proper process thi kari sake.

Pehla 3 imandar vaat (vanchya vagar SOP use na karvi) 1. Koi pan SOP 'super rank guaranteed' nathi aapti. Ranking ma content quality, site authority (backlinks), technical health, intent match ane competition — badhu kaam kare chhe. Aa SOP tamaru chance khub vadhare chhe, guarantee nahi. 2. Original notes ma "competitor no content 90% same rakho, slug 10% change karo" lakhyu hatu — aa step SOP mathi kaadhi nakhyu chhe. Google na spam policies ma 'scraped content' ane 'scaled content abuse' clearly che: biji site no content thoda badlav sathe republish karvo, ke 2-3 sites nu content jodi ne kai navu add na karvu = spam. Teni jagya e 'SERP study → better page banavo' step mukyu chhe (Step 6). [CONFIRMED — Google Search Central spam policies] 3. KD% ane Volume Semrush na estimate chhe, Google na number nahi. Decision mate useful chhe, pan GSC no real data aave pachhi ene j priority aapvi.

3.6.C Step 1 — Project ni taiyari (10 min)

Keyword shodhta pehla aa vaat sheet ma lakhi do. Aa vagar keyword research disha vagar nu thay.

  1. Target country: Client kya vechhe chhe? (US / UK / India). Semrush ma database aaj country no select karvo — Global volume par decision na levo.
  2. Business goal: Sale joie chhe (product/category) ke traffic + trust (blog)?
  3. Site type: Shopify ecommerce / service / local / blog.
  4. Existing pages: Site par pehla thi kaya pages chhe — sitemap ke Shopify admin mathi list kaadho. (Step 5 ma cannibalization check mate joie.)
  5. [v1.1 navu] Seasonality: Google Trends ma seed keyword nu 5-year trend (target country) juo. Keyword seasonal hoy (Christmas gifts, back to school, Diwali) to page season thi 2-3 mahina pehla publish karo — Semrush nu volume 12 mahina nu average chhe, peak mahina ma demand ghanu vadhare hoi shake. [CONFIRMED — Semrush volume = monthly average]

3.6.D Step 2 — Seed keyword research

[v1.1 navu] 2A. Existing site hoy to pehla GSC (free ane sauthi accurate)

  1. GSC → Performance → Search results → last 3 months → Queries tab.
  2. Filter: Position 8 thi 30 + impressions vadhare. Aa keywords par Google tamari site ne already relevant maane chhe — sauthi jaldi result aa j aape.
  3. Query par click → Pages tab: kayo page rank thay chhe te note karo (Step 5 ma Update decision mate).
  4. Aa list 'GSC Opportunities' tab ma muko. Semrush data sathe match karo — GSC real chhe, Semrush estimate.

2B. Keyword Overview (Semrush)

  1. Semrush → Keyword Overview → seed keyword nakho (example: meal planner template) → database = target country (example: US).
  2. Jovu: Volume (target country nu), KD%, Intent, CPC, SERP features (images, PAA, AI Overview, shopping).
  3. Niche 'Questions' list joi lo — aa FAQ / H2 ideas mate kaam aavse. Export kari lo.

2C. Keyword Magic Tool (Keyword ideas)

  1. 'Keyword ideas' → View all → Keyword Magic Tool khulse. Database = target country.
  2. Broad match tab rakho (sauthi vadhare ideas). Example result: 748 keywords, total volume 18,720, average KD 26%.
  3. Filters lagavo: Volume ≥ 50 (niche ma 20 pan chale), KD% ≤ tamari site ni capacity (navi/low authority site mate 0-30 thi sharu karo), Language = English.
  4. Left side na groups (example: printable, free, weekly, family, google sheets) joi lo — aa j pachhi cluster ideas bane chhe.
  5. Export → sheet ma 'Raw Keywords' tab ma paste karo.
Senior tip KD ni limit fix number nathi. Strong DA/backlinks vali site 40-50 KD par pan rank kari shake; navi site mate 0-25 thi start karvu safe chhe. Pehla tamari site kaya KD na keywords par already page 1 par chhe e GSC + Semrush Organic Research ma juo — e tamari 'safe KD range' chhe. [LIKELY — industry practice]
[v1.1 navu] Semrush na hoy to — free alternatives • Google Search Console: existing site mate sauthi saro source (real queries). • Google Keyword Planner: volume ranges (exact number ad spend vagar nathi male), country select thay. • Google autocomplete, People Also Ask, Related searches: long-tail ane question keywords. • Google Trends: seasonality ane rising topics. • KD nu manual check: top 10 ma kon chhe juo — moti brands/marketplaces j hoy to agharu; forums, nana blogs, juna pages hoy to chance chhe. Process same rahe — fakt data sources badlay.

3.6.E Step 3 — Clean + priority (sauthi important filter)

Original notes ma 'highest volume + lowest KD' lakhyu hatu — e sachu chhe, pan adhuru. Aa filter lagavo:

CheckShu jovuExample
RelevanceBusiness ne kaam nu chhe? Je vechta nathi teno keyword kaadhoMeal planner app na hoy to 'meal planner app download' kaadho
IntentKeyword nu intent tamara page type sathe match thay chhe?'buy meal planner notebook' = product/collection; 'how to meal plan' = blog
Business valueAa traffic sale/lead aape? (CPC pan signal chhe — vadhu CPC = advertisers ne aa traffic thi kamai)'free meal planner template' traffic aape, sale ochhu; 'meal planner notebook' sale nu
VolumeTarget country volumeUS: 2,400
KD %Tamari safe range ma?26% → OK

Simple priority score (sheet ma column banavo):

Priority = Business Value (1-3) × Intent match (1-3) × Opportunity (1-3, volume vadhu + KD ochhu = 3)

Score 18-27 = pehla banavo, 8-17 = pachhi, 1-7 = skip. Aa formula andha thai ne na vaparvu — client e koi product push karvu hoy to te upar aave. [v1.1 navu] GSC ma position 8-30 vala keywords ne +1 Opportunity aapo — tya page already chhe.

3.6.F Step 4 — Keyword Strategy Builder: Topic, Pillar, Cluster

  1. Semrush → Keyword Strategy Builder → seed keyword(s) nakho → country select → 2-3 min ma SERP analyze thase.
  2. All Pages / Topics tab: dar topic ma ek pillar page + ena cluster pages dekhase. Example: Topic: printable meal planner templates — Total volume 97,590, Avg KD 32.52%.
  3. Dar topic nu naam + volume + KD sheet ma 'Topic Map' tab ma save karo.
  4. Topic par click → pillar page keyword (example: meal planner template) par click → tena related keywords + KD + volume malse. Aa pillar page na secondary keywords chhe.
  5. 'Potential SERP competitors and content references' na 10 URLs copy karo — Step 6 ma vaparvana chhe.
  6. Puro topic (pillar + clusters + keywords) ek sathe sheet format ma export kari lo — data accurate rahe.
Semrush na grouping par blind trust na karvo — SERP overlap test Be keyword ek page ma jase ke alag? Google ma bane search karo (same country, incognito). Top 10 ma 3-4 ke vadhu same URLs hoy → Google ene same topic maane chhe → ek j page. Overlap 0-2 hoy → alag page. Tool khota grouping kare tyare aa test j final decision chhe. [LIKELY — widely used industry method]

5D. Slug (URL) na rules

  • Pillar no slug = primary keyword nu short version: /meal-planner-template/ (notes ma kahyu tem highest-volume relevant keyword — sachu, pan intent match pehla).
  • Cluster slug = eno potano primary keyword: /printable-weekly-meal-planner/
  • Short, lowercase, hyphen, no dates/years (refresh vakhte URL badalvo na pade).
  • Competitor no slug copy karvani jarur nathi — slug ranking ma nano role chhe; tamaro keyword clear hoy e j poorto chhe. [CONFIRMED that URL words are a minor factor per Google]
  • Juna page nu slug badalvu hoy to 301 redirect farjiyat.

3.6.U SEMrush Keyword Magic Tool (Semrush SOP - added 19 Nov 2026)

Step-by-step: expand seeds into a clean, intent-matched keyword shortlist

1. Open the legacy Magic Tool

Open SEO → Keyword Research → Keyword Magic Tool, without the 2.0 label. Enter one seed and select the target country.

Working example: diamond blades, United States. Click Search and confirm both the seed and country in the report header.

Real run on 19 Nov 2026 (diamond blades, United States): All keywords 11,487. Largest groups: saw 2,862, cut 1,635, inch 790, tile 686, grinder 595, concrete 517. Sample table rows: 'raimondi diamond blade resin dressing stone', informational/commercial intent, volume 2,900, KD 0%, CPC $1.65; 'diamond blade', informational, 1,900, KD 9%, CPC $2.05.

2. Start broad, then narrow

Review All/Broad Match, Phrase Match, Exact Match and Related where offered. Read the tooltips because match labels here are research filters, not Google Ads campaign settings.

Use Broad to explore variations and Related to discover adjacent meanings. Compare the lists rather than assuming every suggestion contains the exact seed.

3. Process groups and subgroups

Review the left-hand groups, sort by keyword count or volume, and expand relevant subgroups. Process every group relevant to the actual product range.

For Client A, separate porcelain, granite, wet cutting, polishing or size-related ideas only if the live suggestions and catalog support them. Remove unrelated saws, brands and materials.

4. Use Questions and intent

Switch to Questions to find useful explanatory needs. Record informational topics separately from buying terms.

Example training pair: diamond blades as a category candidate; how to choose a blade for porcelain as a guide candidate. Check the actual SERP before deciding whether they need separate pages.

5. Apply and record filters

Use Volume, KD, Intent, Include/Exclude and available advanced filters such as word count or SERP features. A small initial KD/volume filter can focus review, but do not make one threshold a rule for every site.

Use domain-specific PKD only when the correct domain is entered and the current account supplies it. Do not substitute paid Competitive Density for organic KD.

6. Validate the shortlist

Open Keyword Overview and SERP results for final candidates. Record actual country-specific metrics and competing page types.

Group same-intent variants into one page brief. Do not make a page per spelling or word-order variation. Check the existing site and mapping sheet to avoid duplicate topics.

7. Save, export and map

Select relevant rows. Use Export for All, This group or Selected as appropriate, and verify the row scope. Export XLSX/CSV; preserve grouping if it helps the research handoff.

Tracker: Seed | Group/subgroup | Keyword | Country/date | Intent | Volume | KD/PKD | SERP features | Existing/proposed URL | Status | Rejection reason.

Use a new clearly named list when saving keywords; never modify another person's list simply because it is visible.

8. Final quality check

The legacy tool and Magic Tool 2.0 group ideas differently. Use the next tab for semantic topics and AI prompts.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • All relevant groups reviewed, not only first-page suggestions.
  • Questions, buying intent and irrelevant terms separated.
  • Final metrics and SERPs checked in Keyword Overview.
  • One intent mapped to one suitable page.
  • Output: clean keyword groups ready for Keyword Strategy Builder and content briefs.

3.6.V SEMrush Keyword Magic Tool 2.0 (Semrush SOP - added 19 Nov 2026)

Step-by-step: research semantic topics, Google keywords and AI prompts together

1. Open the exact 2.0 tool

Open SEO → Keyword Research → Keyword Magic Tool 2.0. Enter one seed, select the target country and click Explore ideas.

Working example checked on 19 Nov 2026: diamond blades, United States. Confirm the country again on the results page. The original tab name is retained; the current tool's full name is Keyword Magic Tool 2.0.

2. Separate seed and subtopic metrics

Review the seed's Volume, KD %, Global Volume and Intent, then the adjacent subtopic's Total Volume, SEO Volume, Keywords, AI Volume and Prompts.

Observed example in the checked Jupiter session: diamond blades displayed US Volume 1K, KD 32% and Global Volume 6.4K. These are time-sensitive tool estimates, not a traffic forecast. Intent displayed n/a, which must not be invented.

The adjacent diamond blade tools subtopic displayed Total Volume 76.1K, SEO Volume 60.6K, AI Volume 15.5K, 2.3K keywords and 249 prompts. Those are subtopic totals, not the seed's volume.

3. Expand topics and subtopics

Use the left topic list. Expand relevant topics, then select a subtopic to filter the table. Sort by Relevance or Keywords count where offered.

The checked search included construction & power tools, with subtopics such as diamond blade tools, tile cutting blades and quartz cutting blades. Validate each against the real catalog before keeping it. Semantic grouping can include terms that do not share the seed's words.

4. Review Keywords

In Keywords view, collect Keyword, Relevance, Volume, Intent, KD % and SERP Features. Use All/Questions, the Filter box, Volume/KD filters and Add filter.

Open details for the seed or subtopic and inspect Top SEO Competitors. Do not confuse a topic's combined volume with the demand one page can win.

5. Review Prompts separately

Switch to Prompts and find the matching relevant topic/subtopic. Record the prompt, AI platform, response, brands and sources where shown. Use View full response to inspect context.

Filter platforms such as ChatGPT, AI Overview, AI Mode or Gemini where available. AI Volume is modeled prompt demand, not Google search volume. Check any factual answer against reliable product sources before using it.

6. Plan one useful page per intent

Review both keywords and prompts for a subtopic, then decide which existing or new page can answer the need.

Example: related porcelain blade selection queries and AI questions may fit one original buyer guide. A product-size query may need a product/collection page instead. Do not make separate thin pages for every variation or promise that one page must cover unrelated intents.

7. Save and export the right scope

Select keyword rows, then Save keywords → Save selected keywords if a list is needed. For exports choose the relevant topic/subtopic or selected rows, then Export and the displayed format.

Keep keyword and prompt exports separate. Tracker: Seed | Topic/subtopic | Country/date | Keyword or prompt | Platform | Metrics with units | Reference source | Page mapping | Action/status.

3.6.W SEMrush Keyword Strategy Builder (Semrush SOP - added 19 Nov 2026)

Step-by-step: Seed keywords → Topics → Pillar pages → Subpages → Final verification

1. Prepare up to five seed keywords

Open SEMrush → Keyword Strategy Builder.

Prepare up to five related seed keywords in one batch. Example: “Client D”, “non toxic kids makeup”, “non toxic makeup for kids”, “kids non toxic makeup”. Keep them within the same product/category or search-intent group.

Paste the seed keywords, select the target country/database, then click “Create”.

The workflow notes a working limit of up to 30 keyword creations per day. Track usage. If the limit is reached, use Toolzbuy only if it is an authorized account with permitted access under SEMrush terms; otherwise wait for reset or request authorized access.

2. Wait for preparation and select one seed keyword

After clicking Create, wait while SEMrush shows “Preparing keywords”. Allow approximately 5–10 minutes as indicated in this workflow; actual time may vary.

When results appear, verify the strategy name, database/country, currency and seed keywords.

Use the dropdown to select one seed keyword. Complete the full workflow for this keyword before selecting the next one.

Review listed URLs and Domain Authority Score (AS). Lower AS may indicate easier competition, but use it only as one signal.

Open the top five relevant URLs. Confirm that each page matches the keyword and search intent.

Record each competitor title, URL, page type, headings, subheadings, questions answered, examples and content gaps.

Use competitor pages for research only. Do not copy their paragraphs, wording, images or full content.

Write original, accurate content using Client A’s real products, expertise and services. Do not create pages for topics unrelated to Client A.

6. Build the pillar page

Use the pillar page’s main keyword as the primary target.

Map all relevant associated keywords to suitable sections. Include them naturally; do not force irrelevant phrases or keyword-stuff.

Create a clear title/H1, introduction, logical H2/H3 sections, helpful explanations, product/service information, FAQs where useful and relevant internal links.

Answer genuine People Also Ask questions and naturally address relevant Related Searches. Add images/video only when they help the reader.

Use competitor research to find missing information, then create a more useful original page.

Link to relevant Client A collections, products and supporting pages. Save the content brief, draft URL and final page URL in the tracker.

7. Process every subpage using the same workflow

Open the subpage dropdown/list under the current topic.

Open the first subpage and capture its page keyword, supporting keywords, Intent, KD %, Volume and SERP features.

Review Potential SERP Competitors and Content References for that subpage.

Open and review the top five relevant reference URLs. Record page structure and content gaps.

Create an original subpage that satisfies its own search intent and naturally covers the relevant associated keywords.

Link the subpage to its parent pillar page and add sibling links only where useful.

Repeat for every subpage under the topic. Then move to the next topic: pillar page first, followed by all its subpages.

11. Important rules

Complete one seed keyword fully before moving to the next.

Repeat the pillar + subpage workflow for every topic under All Pages.

All relevant associated keywords must be considered and mapped naturally; do not stuff irrelevant keywords into page copy.

The tool’s page count is a planning structure, not a requirement to publish thin or duplicate pages. Combine pages when their search intent is the same.

Only create pages relevant to Client A’s actual products, services and audience. The tire-repair examples are illustrative, not automatically relevant to Client A.

Do not copy competitor content. Research their structure and coverage, then write original, verified content.

No process can guarantee 80% of search traffic. Rankings depend on intent, competition, content quality, authority, technical SEO, internal links and ongoing measurement.

Record the research date because volumes, KD, SERP features and competitor results can change.
