🧭 3.12 — 11 Lead Gen SEO (Service Business) — 11 Nov 2026

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Plumber, agency, clinic, consultant, contractor — koi pan service business mate je traffic nahi, booked calls/leads joiye. Ecommerce SOPs sathe aa differences apply karo. Source: 2026 service-business playbooks (niche sources niche).

3.12.B 2. Page architecture (03 → 15 NAVI architecture sathe match kare)

  • Ek page per service — buyer intent pramane, internal jargon pramane nahi. Customer "drain cleaning" search kare, "hydro-jetting solutions" nahi.
  • Ek page per city/service-area — fakta jya sach ma service aapo. Same template 50 cities ma faqt name badalvu = doorway pages (CONFIRMED: Google spam policy). Dar city page par unique content: local proof, local reviews, area specifics.
  • Proof pages — case studies per vertical (residential/commercial), reviews hub, before/after gallery.
  • Homepage → Services hub → Service pages → City pages. Blog cluster service pages ne support kare.

3.12.C 3. Service page must-haves (rank + convert banne mate)

  • Headline: service + city + USP (3 second ma khabar pade)
  • Scope: su include chhe ane su nahi — vagueness conversion mare
  • Price range ke "starting at" ke quote process (khotu price kyarey nahi — audit PDF rule same)
  • Timeline expectation (kitla divas/kalak)
  • Service area clarity + payment/financing info
  • Ek direct CTA — call, form, WhatsApp (above the fold + end ma)
  • Schema: Service + LocalBusiness + FAQ (08 → 04)
  • Reviews/proof same page par

3.12.D 4. Keyword strategy — P&L jevu vicharo

  • High-margin services pehla — vanity traffic nahi (volume karta job value juvo)
  • Buyer-stage terms: "cost", "quote", "installer", "contractor near me", "emergency + service + city"
  • Location-modified: "city + service" ane "service + near me"
  • Low-margin emergency terms = alag page/messaging (qualification steps sathe)
  • Keyword research process same: 02 → 07 + 03 → 06 (Semrush SOP)

3.12.E 5. Local SEO (lead gen nu 50% kaam ahi chhe)

  • GBP complete: categories, services, photos, posts, Q&A (06 → 04 Local SEO)
  • Reviews system: dar job pachhi review request; badha reviews ne reply
  • NAP consistency badha citations ma
  • Local keywords service + city pages ma map

3.12.F 6. Conversion path (traffic aave to lead bane)

  • Ek page = ek primary CTA. 3 alag CTA = confusion = no lead.
  • Phone number clickable, top-right + sticky mobile
  • Form short (name, phone, service, message) — lambo form = drop
  • Response time mention ("2 kalak ma call pacho") — ane follow karo
  • Call tracking / form tracking GA4 key events ma (07 → 02) — nahi to SEO ROI prove na thay

3.12.G 7. Blog nu role (lead gen ma)

  • Blog service pages ne support kare — "how much does X cost in [city]", "X vs Y", "signs you need X"
  • Dar blog → related service page par 1-2 contextual links (03 → 07 workflow same)
  • AEO format (05 → 07) — service questions AI Overviews ma pan aave

3.12.H Common mistakes

  • Ek j "Services" page par 10 services list karvi (ek pan rank nahi thay)
  • 50 city pages same content sathe (doorway risk)
  • Traffic report karvu pan leads track na karvu (client ne ROI na dekhay = client jashe)
  • GBP ignore karvu
  • Pricing ekdum hide karvu (conversion + trust banne ghate)

3.12.I Checklist

  • Dar high-margin service nu dedicated page chhe
  • Dar real service-area nu city page (unique content sathe)
  • Dar service page par 7 must-haves chhe
  • GBP optimized + review process chalu
  • Call
  • form tracking GA4 ma set
  • Keyword map buyer-intent pramane (02 → 07 ma entry)
