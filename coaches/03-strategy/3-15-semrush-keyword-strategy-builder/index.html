🧭 3.15 — Semrush Keyword Strategy Builder — Topic thi Pillar + Subpage sudhi

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

3.15.A Aa stop su chhe

Semrush no Keyword Strategy Builder seed keyword mathi aakhu topic plan aape chhe: Topics → Pillar pages → Subpages. Aa SOP e tool nu full working guide chhe — seed keywords nakvathi lai ne final verification sudhi, kai pan miss na thay evi rite. Flow: Seed keywords → Topics → Pillar pages → Subpages → Final verification.

Kyare use karvo: nava site ke nava section nu content plan banavvu hoy tyare — jethi Google ne full topical authority dekhay, fakt 2-3 pages nahi.

3.15.B Keyword Strategy Builder — step-by-step

Step 1: 5 sudhi na seed keywords taiyar karo

  1. Semrush → Keyword Strategy Builder kholo.
  2. Ek j batch ma 5 sudhi na related seed keywords ready rakho. Example: "miss nella", "non toxic kids makeup", "non toxic makeup for kids", "kids non toxic makeup". Badha same product/category ke same search-intent group ma j rakho.
  3. Seed keywords paste karo, target country/database select karo, pachhi "Create" click karo.
  4. Note: divas ni ~30 keyword creations ni working limit chhe. Usage track karo. Limit puri thay to reset ni raah juo ke authorized access mango — be-authorized account no use nahi.

Step 2: Preparation puri thava do, pachhi ek seed keyword select karo

  1. Create click karya pachhi Semrush "Preparing keywords" batave — ~5-10 minute raah juo (actual time different hoy sake).
  2. Results aave etle verify karo: strategy name, database/country, currency ane seed keywords saacha chhe ke nahi.
  3. Dropdown mathi ek seed keyword select karo. E keyword nu full workflow puru karo, pachhi j bija par javo.
  4. Example result header: Keyword Strategy Builder: Tire Repair Services | Database: United States | Currency: USD | Seed keywords: Tire Repair Services.

Step 3: "All pages" kholo ane darek topic list karo

  1. Strategy na tabs juo: "Best for strategy", "Quick conversions", "More potential traffic" ane "All pages".
  2. "All pages" kholo — ahiji full topic/page structure nu working view chhe.
  3. Darek topic nu naam, Total Volume ane Average KD record karo. Fakt pehlo topic process kari ne ATAKVO NAHI.

Step 4: Darek topic nu pillar page process karo

  1. All Pages mathi pehlo topic kholo.
  2. Pillar Page shodho ane enu proposed page title/main keyword note karo.
  3. Pillar page sathe connected badha keywords capture karo — Intent, KD %, Volume ane biji dekhati metrics sathe.
  4. Dekhata SERP Features note karo: Local pack, Sitelinks, Video, Image, People also ask, Related searches, Discussions and forums.
  5. Pillar page na supporting keywords review karo ane relevant terms ne pillar content na sections ma map karo.
  6. Badha topics mate same repeat karo.

Step 5: Potential SERP Competitors ane Content References research karo

  1. Topic/page mate "Potential SERP competitors and content references" kholo.
  2. List thayeli URLs ane Domain Authority Score (AS) juo. Ocho AS = easy competition hoy sake, pan e fakt ek signal chhe.
  3. Top 5 relevant URLs kholo. Confirm karo ke darek page keyword ane search intent sathe match kare chhe.
  4. Darek competitor mate note karo: title, URL, page type, headings, subheadings, je questions na javab ape chhe, examples ane content gaps.
  5. Competitor pages fakt research mate — tenu copy NAHI (paragraphs, wording, images ke full content koi pan).
  6. Content hamesha original lakho, aapna real products, expertise ane services par based. Aapna business sathe related na hoy eva topics na pages BANAVO NAHI.

Step 6: Pillar page banao

  1. Pillar page nu main keyword primary target rakhо.
  2. Badha relevant associated keywords suitable sections ma map karo — naturally; irrelevant phrases force nahi, keyword stuffing nahi.
  3. Clear title/H1, introduction, logical H2/H3 sections, helpful explanations, product/service info, jarur hoy to FAQs ane relevant internal links banavo.
  4. People Also Ask na real questions na javab aapo ane relevant Related Searches naturally cover karo. Images/video fakt tyare j umero jyare reader ne madad kare.
  5. Competitor research mathi missing information shodho, pachhi e kartha vadhare useful original page banavo.
  6. Relevant collections, products ane supporting pages ne link karo. Content brief, draft URL ane final page URL tracker ma save karo.

Step 7: Darek subpage same workflow thi process karo

  1. Current topic niche subpage dropdown/list kholo.
  2. Pehlo subpage kholo ane enu page keyword, supporting keywords, Intent, KD %, Volume ane SERP features capture karo.
  3. E subpage mate pan Potential SERP Competitors and Content References review karo.
  4. Top 5 relevant reference URLs kholo ane review karo — page structure ane content gaps note karo.
  5. Original subpage banao je potana search intent satisfy kare ane relevant associated keywords naturally cover kare.
  6. Subpage ne ena parent pillar page sathe link karo; sibling links fakt jya useful hoy tya.
  7. Topic na badha subpages mate repeat karo. Pachhi next topic: pehla pillar page, pachhi ena badha subpages.

Step 9: Final verification — Topical Overview

  1. Ek seed keyword na badha pillar pages ane subpages process thay pachhi "Topical Overview" kholo.
  2. Dekhata total topics ane pages note karo. Example: "8 topics, 135 pages".
  3. E totals ne tracking sheet sathe compare karo.
  4. Verify karo: darek topic recorded chhe, darek pillar page accounted chhe, darek subpage ne status chhe.
  5. Missing pages, duplicates ane same search intent vala overlapping pages shodho. Overlap hoy to pages combine karo ke publish pehla intent alag karo.
  6. Seed keyword ne complete tyare j mark karo jyare badha topic groups ane badha pillar/subpage rows check thay gaya hoy.

Step 11: Important rules

  • Ek seed keyword fully puru karo, pachhi j bija par javo.
  • All Pages na darek topic mate pillar + subpage workflow repeat karo.
  • Badha relevant associated keywords naturally map karo; irrelevant keywords page copy ma stuff nahi.
  • Tool no page count planning structure chhe, thin ke duplicate pages publish karvani requirement nahi. Search intent same hoy to pages combine karo.
  • Fakt aapna actual products, services ane audience sathe relevant pages j banavo. Tire-repair examples illustrative chhe.
  • Competitor content copy nahi — tenu structure ane coverage research karo, pachhi original, verified content lakho.
  • Koi pan process search traffic no 80% hisso guarantee nathi aapti. Rankings intent, competition, content quality, authority, technical SEO, internal links ane ongoing measurement par depend kare chhe.
  • Research date record karo — volumes, KD, SERP features ane competitor results badlai shake chhe.

3.15.C Expected Output

Output: ek seed keyword mate full topic plan — badha topics, pillar pages ane subpages tracking sheet ma status sathe, Topical Overview thi verify thayelu. Pachhi aa plan stop 3.13 (Pillar + cluster) ane 3.6 (Keyword to Content) sathe execute thay.
