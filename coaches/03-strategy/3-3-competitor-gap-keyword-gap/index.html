🧭 3.3 — Competitor Gap + Keyword Gap

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Competitors je keywords par rank kare chhe ane tame nahi - te list j tamari next 3-6 mahina ni content strategy chhe. Gap analysis = ready-made roadmap.

3.3.A Steps (pagla)

  1. Tool select karo: Semrush (Keyword Gap tool), Ahrefs (Content Gap), ke free option - Ubersuggest + manual Google search.
  2. Tamaru domain vs 3-4 competitors enter karo.
  3. Filter: competitors top 20 ma, tame 50+ ke absent - te keywords export karo.
  4. Cluster karo: similar keywords ne group karo - ek group = ek page/blog (keyword cannibalization avoid karva).
  5. Priority set karo: Volume x (1/KD) x business relevance - je highest te pehla.

3.3.B Manual method (tool vagar)

  • Main keyword search karo, competitor na top pages open karo, temna H2/H3 topics note karo - tame cover karel nathi te gap chhe
  • Google "People Also Ask" ane "Related Searches" manually note karo (ke Semrush Questions / AlsoAsked jeva tools) - automated scraping nahi

3.3.C Output nu format

  • Sheet: Keyword | Volume | KD | Competitor ranking | Tamari status | Priority | Target page

3.3.D Checklist

  • Gap keyword sheet taiyar chhe (50+ keywords)
  • Clusters banavela (ek cluster = ek page)
  • Next content batch mate Top 10 priorities select karela chhe

3.3.E SEMrush Keyword Overview (Semrush SOP - added 19 Nov 2026)

Step-by-step: validate a keyword before adding it to the content plan

1. Prepare one keyword

Take a candidate from Keyword Gap, Magic Tool or the client's product list. Begin with one exact phrase so its intent can be checked carefully.

Working example, real run on 19 Nov 2026: diamond blades, United States, desktop. Observed results: Volume 1.0K, KD 32% (Possible), Global Volume 6.4K (India 1.3K, US 1.0K, Philippines 480, Australia 390, UK 320, South Africa 320), Intent n/a, CPC $1.92, Competitive Density 1.00. Keyword Variations: 11.5K ideas, 125.1K total volume; top ones: raimondi diamond blade resin dressing stone (2.9K, KD 0%), diamond blade (1.9K, KD 9%), diamond saw blades (1.9K, KD 26%). Questions: 491 ideas, 4.7K total volume; top ones: how to dress a diamond blade (260), can a diamond blade cut metal (210), how to sharpen a diamond blade (210).

2. Open Keyword Overview

Open SEO → Keyword Research → Keyword Overview. Enter the phrase, choose the target country, and click Search.

Optionally enter our domain for personalized metrics if available. Do not confuse the domain box with the keyword box. Local location settings apply only when a local analysis is intended.

3. Record keyword metrics

Capture research date, database/location, device/date where shown, Volume, Global Volume, KD %, Intent, Trend, CPC and Competitive Density.

Keep PKD, Potential Traffic and Topical Authority separate when domain-specific fields are available. KD describes general competition; PKD is tailored to the entered domain. Missing data stays Not available, not zero.

4. Confirm intent in the SERP

Review the SERP Analysis, its update date, top relevant URLs and SERP features. Decide whether the query mainly needs a collection, product page, guide, comparison or local service page.

Example: do not map a buyer-focused blade query to a generic company About page. Open relevant competitors to understand the task the searcher is trying to finish.

5. Review variations and questions

Open Keyword Variations, Questions and Keyword Strategy/cluster links where available. Save only relevant terms that fit the proposed page.

A question about safe material use needs verified manufacturer guidance. Do not invent tool compatibility, blade size, RPM limits or safety instructions from a keyword suggestion.

6. Use bulk analysis after validation

Use Bulk Analysis for a shortlist of up to 100 keywords. Check the count and country before running. Review Intent, Volume, KD, CPC and other selected columns.

Bulk analysis does not supply local metrics or a historical snapshot for every row in the same way as a single-keyword report. Do not mix national and city-level values in one comparison.

7. Save the decision and evidence

Tracker: Keyword | Database/location | Research date | Intent | Volume | KD | PKD/domain if shown | Trend | SERP date/features | Top reference URLs | Target page | Keep/reject/review reason.

Save selected terms to a properly named list only when needed, or export CSV/XLSX. Save a PDF for a single-keyword report if the plan permits it. Do not overwrite another user's shared list.

8. Final quality check

Volume estimates are not guaranteed visitors. Global Volume is not the demand in the client's target country.

Final quality gate (tick karo)

  • Exact phrase and country verified.
  • Keyword intent checked against ranking pages.
  • KD, paid competition and domain-specific PKD distinguished.
  • Values include collection date and location.
  • Output: a validated keyword record with one intended target page.
