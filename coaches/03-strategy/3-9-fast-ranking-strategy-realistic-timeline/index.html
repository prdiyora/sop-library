🧭 3.9 — Fast Ranking Strategy — Realistic Timeline sathe

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Navi ke juni, koi pan website ne Google ma jaldi rank karavva mate sachi ane practical strategy. Khoti guarantee nahi. Google na official docs na sources niche aapela chhe.

3.9.A 0. Pehla sach samjo — "1-2 divas ma rank" ni reality

Google pote kahe chhe: "No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google."

Etle koi kahe ke 1-2 divas ma Google par #1 rank karavi daish, to e khoti guarantee aape chhe athva indexing ane ranking vachche no farak samajto nathi.

Google SEO Starter Guide pramane:

  • Ketlak changes nu result thoda kalak ma dekhay.

  • Ketlak changes nu result aavta ghana mahina lage.

  • Result judge karta pehla thoda week wait karvu.

Indexing ≠ Ranking

  • Indexing: Google tamaru page potana database ma add kare. Aa thoda kalak ma pan thai shake.

  • Ranking: Koi keyword search kare tyare tamaru page Google na results ma upar aave. Aa mate vadhare time lage.

Google na John Mueller pramane, navi website ma su khas value chhe e samajva Google ne time lage chhe. Etle navi site ne trust banavva samay aapvo.

1-2 divas ma su thai shake?

  • Main pages Google ma index thai shake.

  • Potana brand name par website dekhai shake.

  • Ekdum ochhi competition vala long-tail keyword par ranking mali shake.

Aa fast result chhe. Pan je keyword mate ghani websites compete kare chhe, tema 1-2 divas ma ranking ni guarantee nathi.

3.9.C 2. Speed levers — je sach ma kaam kare (Priority order ma)

1. Crawl blockers kadho

Pehla check karo ke Google tamaru page access kari shake chhe ke nahi.

  • noindex lagelu hoy to check karo.

  • robots.txt Googlebot ne block kartu hoy to sudharo.

  • canonical khota URL par hoy to fix karo.

  • GSC → URL Inspection ma badhu check karo.

Aa problem hoy to biji SEO mehnat no faydo ochho male.

2. Sitemap clean rakho ane submit karo

  • Sitemap ma sacha canonical URLs rakho.

  • lastmod ma page sachche update thayu hoy e date rakho.

  • Sitemap GSC ma submit karo.

3. Homepage ane hub mathi internal links aapo

  • Homepage thi important pages ne link karo.

  • Category/hub pages thi relevant pages ne link karo.

  • Google je pages varamvar crawl kare chhe, tya thi links aapo.

Aa rite Google nava pages jaldi shodhi shake. Ketlik vaar manual indexing request karta pan vadhare useful thay.

4. Request Indexing karo

GSC → URL Inspection → Request Indexing.

  • Faqt important pages mate use karo.

  • Request ni limit hoy chhe.

  • Aa Google ne crawl request mokle chhe.

  • Aa ranking jaldi aavvani guarantee nathi.

5. IndexNow on karo

IndexNow thi Bing, Yandex, Seznam ane Naver jeva search engines ne page update ni jaldi jaan kari shakay.

  • Google IndexNow support kartu nathi.

  • Google e 2022 ma test karyu hatu, pan adopt karyu nathi.

  • Cloudflare, Shopify, Wix ke RankMath ma setup available hoy shake.

  • Supported setup ma free ma enable kari shakay.

6. Google Indexing API no sacho use karo

Google Indexing API faqt aa pages mate chhe:

  • JobPosting vala pages.

  • BroadcastEvent markup vala livestream pages.

Normal blog, product ke service pages mate use na karo. Aa API na rules ni bahar chhe ane quota revoke thavanu risk chhe.

7. Server fast ane error-free rakho

  • Website jaldi load thay e check karo.

  • Server errors fix karo.

  • Googlebot ne pages kholvama problem na aavvi joie.

Slow server ke errors hoy to Googlebot ochha pages crawl kari shake.

Source: Google Crawl Budget docs.

8. Position 4-20 vala pages improve karo

Aa sauthi fast ranking gains aapti opportunities mathi ek chhe.

  • GSC ma position 4 thi 20 vala pages shodho.

  • Title improve karo.

  • CTR vadhe evu title/meta description lakho.

  • Content ma missing information add karo.

  • Relevant internal links aapo.

Aa pages Google ma pehla thi dekhay chhe, etle improvement thi jaldi faydo mali shake.

Related SOP: 03 → 01 ane 03 → 02.

9. Zero-KD / low-competition long-tail target karo

Long-tail etle lamba ane specific search keywords.

Example: "blue cotton kurti under 500"

  • Keyword specific hovo joie.

  • Competition ochhi hoy.

  • Search volume ochhu hoy shake.

  • Jaldi rank thavani chance vadhe, pan guarantee nathi.

10. AI surfaces (AIO/GEO) mate content taiyar karo

  • Simple ane clear answers lakho.

  • Questions na direct answers aapo.

  • Headings ane structured content rakho.

  • AEO format follow karo (05 → 07).

Aa rite navi website nu content pan ketlik long-tail queries mate AI Overviews ma jaldi dekhai shake. Pan guarantee nathi.

3.9.D 3. Fast playbook — navi site (Day-by-day)

Day 1-2: Technical foundation

  • 07 Setup folder na badha technical steps complete karo.

  • Sitemap banavi submit karo.

  • IndexNow on karo.

  • 5-10 quality core pages taiyar karo.

  • Pages ma useful ane complete information rakho. Thin content na mukvo.

Day 3-7: Indexing ane content

  • GSC ma important pages mate Request Indexing karo.

  • Homepage → hub → important pages internal links set karo.

  • 3-5 zero/low-KD long-tail blogs publish karo.

  • Blogs AEO format ma lakho, jethi question no clear answer male.

Week 2-4: Trust ane monitoring

  • Bing ane bija search engines ma site submit karo.

  • 2-3 quality backlinks ke business citations melavo (06 folder).

  • GSC ma indexing status daily check karo.

  • Local business hoy to GBP (Google Business Profile) setup/optimize karo.

Month 2-3: Growth

  • Related topics par vadhare content lakho (cluster expansion).

  • Position 4-20 vala pages improve karo.

  • Google Discover mate strong featured images rakho.

  • Pehlo SEO report taiyar karo (11 folder).

3.9.E 4. Su na karvu (Shortcuts je pachhi nuksan kare)

ShortcutKem na karvu?
Ek sathe ghani links banavvi ke links kharidviLink spam thi Google manual action lai shake. Recovery ma mahinao lagi shake.
Ek sathe 100 thin pages publish karvaScaled content abuse nu risk chhe. Site ni overall quality ane performance par asar padi shake.
Fake freshnessFaqt date badalvi ane content same rakhvu useful nathi.
Request Indexing varamvar karvuGoogle priority vadhartu nathi. Request quota waste thay.
Expired domain hijack / PBNThoda samay ranking vadhi shake, pan Google spam policy thi pachhi moto nuksan thai shake.
Dar 2-3 divase page edit karvuKai change thi result malyu e samajvu mushkel bane. Google ne recrawl ane reassess karva time aapo.

Source: Google Spam Policies ane Helpful Content Guidance.

3.9.F 5. Golden rule

Fast SEO = Crawl blockers kadho + low-competition long-tail keywords pasand karo + internal links aapo + patience rakho.

Google trust website ni quality ane evidence joi ne banave chhe. Ene force kari shakatu nathi.

Client ke team ne "1-2 divas ma ranking guarantee" kyarey na aapo.
