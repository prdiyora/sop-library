🧭 3.10 — 10 Fast Ranking Strategy — Realistic Timeline sathe (11 Nov 2026)

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Koi pan site — navi ke juni — ne sauthi jadpi rank karavvani sachi (honest) strategy. Sources niche. Claim labels: CONFIRMED (Google official) / LIKELY (industry data).

3.10.A 0. Pehla sach samjo — "1-2 divas ma rank" nu reality

  • CONFIRMED: Google potee kahe chhe — "No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google" (Google: Do you need an SEO?). Koi pan person/tool je 1-2 divas ni guarantee aape, e khotu bole chhe ke indexing ne ranking samje chhe.
  • CONFIRMED: Google Starter Guide: "Some changes might take effect in a few hours, others could take several months. Wait a few weeks before judging."
  • Indexing ≠ Ranking. Page Google ma index thay e kala ko ma thay shake — pan koi keyword par page 1 par aavvu e alag vaat chhe.
  • CONFIRMED (John Mueller, Google): Navi site ne Google "unique value" samajva ma time le chhe — navi site par trust build thava do.
  • 1-2 divas ma su shakya chhe: indexing (key pages), brand name keywords, ane ekdum low-competition long-tail je koi target j nathi kartu. Aa "fast" chhe — pan contested keyword mate nahi.

3.10.C 2. Speed levers — je sach ma kaam kare (priority order ma)

  1. Crawl blockers kadho — noindex, robots.txt block, khotu canonical. URL Inspection thi check karo. Aa hoay to baki badhu zero. (CONFIRMED)
  2. Sitemap clean ane submitted — badha canonical URLs, saacho lastmod. (CONFIRMED)
  3. Homepage/hub thi internal link — Google je pages varrecrawl kare tya thi link — manual request karta jadpi. (CONFIRMED/LIKELY)
  4. Request Indexing — GSC URL Inspection → key pages only. Quota limited; ranking ne speed nahi aape, faqt crawl. (CONFIRMED)
  5. IndexNow on — Bing/Yandex/Seznam/Naver mate instant notify. Google IndexNow vanche j nahi (2022 ma test kari ne reject karyu). Free, ek toggle (Cloudflare/Shopify/Wix/RankMath). (CONFIRMED)
  6. Google Indexing API — faqt JobPosting ke livestream (BroadcastEvent) pages mate. General pages mate scope violation = quota revoke risk. (CONFIRMED)
  7. Server fast + error-free — slow server par Googlebot ochho crawl kare. (CONFIRMED — crawl budget docs)
  8. Position 4-20 pages improve karo — e pages already Google ni nazar ma chhe; title/CTR/content improve karvo = sauthi fast ranking gain (03 → 01, 03 → 02).
  9. Zero-KD / long-tail target karo — specific questions ("blue cotton kurti under 500"), e jaldi rank thay. Volume ochho pan jeet guaranteed nazde. (LIKELY)
  10. AI surfaces (AIO/GEO) — well-structured answer content (AEO format: 05 → 07) AI Overviews ma new sites ne pan jaldi show thay shake long-tail queries ma. (LIKELY)

3.10.D 3. Fast playbook — navi site (day-by-day)

  • Day 1-2: Technical foundation complete (07 Setup folder badhu) + sitemap + IndexNow on
  • 5-10 quality core pages (thin nahi)
  • Day 3-7: Key pages par Request Indexing; homepage → hub internal links set; 3-5 zero-KD long-tail blogs publish (AEO format ma)
  • Week 2-4: Bing/others submit; 2-3 quality backlinks/citations (06 folder); GSC ma indexing status daily check; GBP (local hoy to)
  • Month 2-3: Cluster expansion; position 4-20 pages optimize; Discover mate strong featured images; first report (11 folder)

3.10.E 4. Su na karvu (shortcuts je pacho marse)

  • Link bursts / links kharidva — link spam manual action, recovery mahinao (CONFIRMED)
  • 100 thin pages ek sathe — scaled content abuse, akhi site slow thay (CONFIRMED)
  • Fake freshness — faqt date badalvi, content same (CONFIRMED — helpful content guidance)
  • Request Indexing var-var — priority vadhto nathi, quota burn thay (CONFIRMED)
  • Expired domain hijack / PBN — short spike, pachhi site jay (CONFIRMED spam policy)
  • Dar 2-3 divase page edit — Google reassess j na kari shake su kaam karyu

3.10.F 5. Golden rule

Fast = blockers kadvo + long-tail pasand karvo + internal links + patience. Trust Google evidence thi aape — ene force na kari shakay. Client ke team ne kyarey 1-2 divas nu promise nahi; aa timeline table batavo.

3.10.G Checklist

  • URL Inspection ma blockers check karela
  • Sitemap submitted + IndexNow on (Bing mate)
  • Key pages ne homepage/hub thi link
  • Pehla 10 keywords zero/low-KD long-tail chhe
  • 4-6 week review date set kareli (judge karta pehla wait)
  • Koi "guaranteed fast ranking" promise kyarey nahi
