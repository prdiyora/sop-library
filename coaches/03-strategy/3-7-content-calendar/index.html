🧭 3.7 — Content Calendar

Dabbo 03 · Strategy · ✅ Done

Random kabhi pan publish karva thi Google confuse nahi thay, pan consistent cadence thi topical authority speed ma bane. Content calendar = discipline + planning.

3.7.A Simple calendar nu structure

  1. Week 1-2: Money pages pehla (service/product/category pages)
  2. Week 3-4: Supporting blogs je money pages ne link kare
  3. Ongoing: Week ma 2-4 pieces, same days (da. Mon + Thu)

3.7.B Calendar columns (Sheet ma)

  • Date | Topic | Target keyword | Intent | Type (page/blog) | Internal links | Status | Published URL

3.7.C Topic sources (kadi topic khatam nahi thay)

  • Keyword gap sheet (Strategy point 3)
  • People Also Ask + Related Searches
  • Customer na questions (Client Ask point 4)
  • Seasonal events (festival, sale seasons - 1 month pehla publish)
  • Competitor na new posts

3.7.D Checklist

  • 1 mahina nu calendar pehla tyaar
  • Money pages pehla, supporting blogs pachhi
  • Seasonal content 1 month advance ma
