🛒 8.5 — Merchant Center

Dabbo 08 · Ecommerce SEO · ✅ Done

8.5.A Steps

  1. merchants.google.com → account banavo, website claim + verify.
  2. Policy pages ready: shipping, returns, contact, privacy (6.5 Must-Have Pages).
  3. Product feed: Shopify/WooCommerce app ke Google Sheet feed. Fields: id, title, description, link, image_link, price, availability, brand, gtin/mpn (jo hoy), condition.
  4. Product page par Product schema (price + availability) feed sathe match thavu joiye (6.14 Schema).
  5. Diagnostics ma errors ane warnings fix karo (Merchant Center ma "Needs attention").
  6. Free listings (Surfaces across Google) ON.

8.5.B Rules

  • Price ane stock site par ane feed ma same (8.4 Out-of-Stock).
  • Image clean, white background, product clear, koi watermark ke promo text nahi.
  • Title ma brand + product + key attribute (color, size).

8.5.C Monthly check

  • Disapproved products 0 ke ochha. GSC Merchant listings report joyu.
