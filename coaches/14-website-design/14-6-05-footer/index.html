🖌️ 14.6 — 05 Footer

Dabbo 14 · Website Design Setup · ✅ Done

Footer = trust ane help. Visitor ahin 'shipping', 'return', 'contact' shodhe chhe. Pan footer ma bahu bharvu nahi - clean.

14.6.A A. Footer layout (desktop: 4 columns + bottom bar)

ColumnContent
1 BrandLogo, 1-2 line real brand description, social icons (IG, YT, Pinterest, WhatsApp), real address/city ya support email
2 ShopTop 5-6 categories + New + Best Sellers + Gifts
3 HelpShipping & Delivery, Returns & Refund, Track Order, FAQ, Size/Care guide, Contact
4 CompanyAbout, Blog, Reviews, Wholesale/Collab (ho to), Careers (ho to)
Newsletter (optional)Ek field + button; incentive sachu j. Column 4 ni niche ya alag strip
Bottom barContent
Left© year Brand name. All rights reserved.
Center/RightPrivacy Policy · Terms · Cookie ya Refund policy · Sitemap link
Payment iconsJe accept karo e j (UPI, cards, COD ho to)

14.6.B B. Rules

  • Mobile: columns accordion (tap to open), social + contact upar.
  • Legal pages compulsory: Privacy, Terms, Refund/Return, Shipping. GST/business detail jya lagu (invoice/legal pramane).
  • Footer links crawlable; important pages (categories, about, contact) internal links tarike.
  • Background: brand dark colour ya warm neutral; contrast pass.
  • 'Made with AI/template' credit ke generic 'Powered by' nahi.
  • Fake trust badges (Norton etc.) nahi - je sachu hoy e j.
  • Contact: ek real email + WhatsApp. Phone hoy to click-to-call.
Footer checklist: 4 columns ☐ Legal pages ☐ Contact real ☐ Payment icons sachu ☐ Mobile accordion ☐ Links work ☐ Copyright year auto ☐
