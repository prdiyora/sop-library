🔗 6.2 — Social Media Platforms (Off-Page)

Dabbo 06 · Off-Page SEO · ✅ Done

Notebook point 2: "Social media platform". Social signals directly ranking factor nathi, pan indirect boost powerful chhe: brand searches vadhe, content distribute thay, backlinks organic aave, ane EEAT mate brand presence prove thay.

6.2.A Platform pasandgi

  1. Instagram/Facebook: B2C products, visuals ane local business mate
  2. LinkedIn: B2B, services ane professional content mate
  3. YouTube: tutorials, demos - videos search ma pan rank kare
  4. Pinterest: ecommerce, recipes, fashion, decor (ochhu dhyan melavto traffic source)
  5. X/Twitter: tech, news ane quick updates mate

6.2.B SEO sathe connection

  • Darek nava blog/page → social post (traffic + index speed)
  • Profiles complete karo: website link, bio keywords, consistent NAP
  • YouTube videos ma website links (description ma)
  • Content repurposing: 1 blog mathi → 5 social posts → 1 video script

6.2.C Consistency perfection karta vadhu mahatvani

3 posts/week consistent > 15 posts ek week ma pachhi 1 mahino silence. Calendar banavo ane follow karo.

6.2.D Checklist

  • 2-3 platforms selected (badha ma thin presence na banavo)
  • Profiles links sathe fully optimized
  • Blog-to-social workflow active chhe
  • Posting calendar chalu chhe
