🔗 6.8 — Brand & entity SEO

Dabbo 06 · Off-Page SEO · ✅ Done

6.8.A Steps

  1. Same brand identity badhe: name, logo, address/email, short description - website, social, Etsy, Google Business Profile, directories ma ek j.
  2. About + Contact + Author pages clear (05 On-Page / 05 Must-Have Pages, 01 EEAT).
  3. Organization schema with sameAs links (social profiles) - 08 Technical / 04 Schema.
  4. Brand SERP check: Google ma brand name search karo. Pehla page ma own website, social, profiles dekhay chhe? Negative ke wrong result hoy to note karo.
  5. Mentions: relevant sites, bloggers, directories ma brand name sathe mention. Unlinked mention male to link request (link reclamation).
  6. Reviews: real customer reviews (Google, Trustpilot, Etsy). Fake/incentivised nahi.
  7. Knowledge Panel: guarantee nathi. Verified profiles + schema + press/mentions thi kyarek bane. Wikidata/Wikipedia fakat jo notability sachi hoy, spam nahi.
  8. Track: GSC ma branded queries (brand name vala) trend dar mahine; GA4 ma direct
  • AI Assistants channel.

6.8.B Monthly 20 min

  • Brand SERP screenshot, branded clicks note, 3 nava mentions/links note karo.
