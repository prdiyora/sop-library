🔗 6.4 — Local SEO & Listings (Extra - Instinct added)

Dabbo 06 · Off-Page SEO · ✅ Done

Local business mate aa sauthi important off-page point chhe. (Juna SOP docx mathi merged: "NAP and GMB Maintaining, Listing on a third-party website like Justdial".)

6.4.A Google Business Profile (GMB)

  1. Profile 100% complete chhe: categories, services, photos, hours, attributes
  2. Weekly posts/updates (profile active ane useful dekhay; direct ranking factor confirm nathi)
  3. Reviews collect karo - target 30+ reviews, real clients j (fake/incentivised nahi) - ane har review nu reply karo
  4. Q&A feature Google e Dec 2025 thi band karyu (AI "Ask" button aavyu) - GBP description, services, attributes ane reviews complete rakho, AI tyathi j answer banave

6.4.B NAP Consistency

Name, Address, Phone - exact same everywhere: website, GBP, Justdial, socials, directories. Ek digit different = Google confuse = local ranking drop.

6.4.C Local citations (listings)

  • Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART (India)
  • Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps
  • Industry pramane na directories

6.4.D Local keywords

  • "[service] in [city]", "[service] near me" optimize karo
  • Multi-location businesses mate service-area pages

6.4.E Checklist

  • GBP 100% complete + weekly posts karo
  • Badha platforms par NAP consistent
  • 10+ quality citations live chhe
  • Review generation system active chhe (30+ reviews target) + GBP ma photos regular add karo (real office/team/kaam photos)
