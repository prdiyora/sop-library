🔗 6.5 — Global Business Submission (All Countries)

Dabbo 06 · Off-Page SEO · ✅ Done

Source: Divy nu SOP (Nov 6, 2026). New website ane Existing website banne mate. Keyword research fakt Semrush thi.

Senior ni note Business submission directly ranking nathi vadhartu. Eno sacho fayado che brand trust, entity signals (Google ne samjay ke business real che), referral traffic ane AI search ma brand mentions. Etle quantity nahi, quality ane consistency par focus rakhvo.

6.5.A Step 0: Master Business Sheet banavo

Ek Google Sheet ma badhi details ek j format ma lakho, pachhi badhe copy-paste karo.

  • Business Name (exact same spelling, badhe)
  • Address (jo physical office hoy to), Phone with country code, Email
  • Website URL (https, ek j version, e.g. https://example.com/)
  • Short description (150 chars) ane Long description (500–750 chars)
  • Category: primary + 2–3 secondary
  • Logo (square), Cover image, 5–10 real photos
  • Opening hours, Social profile links, Founded year

Why: NAP (Name, Address, Phone) badhe same hovu joie. Alag-alag hoy to Google confuse thay ane trust ghate.

6.5.B Step 1: Semrush thi keyword research (description mate)

  1. Semrush ma Keyword Magic Tool kholo.
  2. Database country select karo (US, UK, AU, CA, IN — je country ma submit karo cho e j).
  3. Seed keyword nakho (e.g. kids skincare, diesel repair).
  4. Filter: Intent = Commercial / Transactional, KD% low–medium.
  5. 3–5 main keywords choose karo ane description ma naturally vaparo, stuffing nahi.
  6. Same country ma competitor domain Organic Research ma nakhi jovo ke e kaya keywords par rank kare che.

Full keyword process: 3.6 Semrush Keyword to Content SOP.

Google ma "site:directory.com" thi pages index che? Aa check fail thay to skip karo. Link nofollow hoy to pan chale — aa listings no fayado trust ane NAP consistency che, link juice nahi.

6.5.E Step 4: Navi website vs pehlethi ni website

Navi website:

  • Pehla week: top 5 global platforms (GBP, Bing, Apple, LinkedIn, Facebook)
  • Pachhi dar week 5–10 quality listings, dhime dhime, jethi spam pattern na dekhay
  • Sitemap GSC ma submit che e confirm karo

Pehlethi ni website:

  • Pehla audit: Google ma "Business Name" + phone search kari juna listings sodho
  • Khoti/juni details (juno address, phone) update karo, duplicate listings remove karo
  • Semrush Backlink Analytics ma jovo kaya directories mathi already link che
  • Semrush Backlink Gap thi competitor pase che pan aapne nathi evi directories sodho

6.5.G Step 6: Monthly measure karo

  • GSC Performance ma brand name queries vadhi ke nahi

6.5.H Common mistakes (aa bhool na karo)

  • Spammy 500-directory packages levi
  • Alag-alag NAP lakhvu
  • Khoti category select karvi
  • Badhe same copy-paste description (thodu unique rakho)

6.5.I Practical task

Ek client (e.g. lav-kids.com, UK) mate:

  • Semrush UK database ma 5 commercial keywords sodho business description mate
  • 5 UK directories select karo ane kaaran lakho ke kem
