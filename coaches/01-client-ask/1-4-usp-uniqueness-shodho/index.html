🎯 1.4 — USP - Uniqueness shodho

Dabbo 01 · Client Ask (R&D) · 🔴 REQUIRED — client pasethi lena mandatory ✅ Done

USP etle Unique Selling Point - tamara client ma aevu su chhe je competitor ma nathi? USP clear hoy to title, content ane ads badha sharp bane. USP na hoy to website "beeja jevi j" lage ane Google pan ene special rank nahi aape.

1.4.A USP kevi rite shodho

  1. Top 5 competitors ni website kholo ane temna homepage na main claims note karo.
  2. Su offer kare chhe je bija nahi karta? Free delivery, same-day service, 10 varas experience, lowest price, handmade, warranty?
  3. Customers sauthi vadhare su vakhan kare chhe? Reviews vanchi ne pattern shodho.
  4. Client ne seedho puchho: "Customer tamne j kema pasand kare chhe?"

1.4.B USP ne kya vaparvo

  • Homepage na H1 ane title ma
  • Meta description ma (CTR vadharva)
  • Product/service pages na first paragraph ma
  • Schema ma (Offer, priceRange)

1.4.C Example

Bija badha jewellers "best designs" lakhe chhe. Jo client 100% BIS hallmark ane lifetime exchange aape chhe - te j USP chhe. Homepage par "BIS Hallmark Gold | Lifetime Exchange" bolu joiye, "Welcome to our website" nahi.

1.4.D Checklist

  • 1 main USP + 2 supporting USP lakhela chhe
  • USP homepage na H1/title ma dekhay chhe
  • USP competitor na claims thi different chhe (copy nathi)
