🎯 1.2 — Business Goal samajvu

Dabbo 01 · Client Ask (R&D) · 🔴 REQUIRED — client pasethi lena mandatory ✅ Done

Client Ask (R&D) no pehlo point. Koi pan website par kaam chalu karta pehla client no main goal clear hovo joiye - bija badha decisions (keywords, design, content) aa goal par j depend kare chhe.

1.2.A Kema important?

Goal vagar SEO karo to traffic aave pan business ne faydo na thay. Example: client ne leads joiye ane tame blog traffic vadharo - traffic aavyu, inquiry nahi. Etle pehla goal, pachhi strategy.

1.2.B Client ne aa questions puchho

  1. Main goal su chhe? Sales (ecommerce), Leads/inquiries (service), ya Branding/awareness?
  2. Business model su chhe? B2B (business ne vechay) ke B2C (direct customer ne)?
  3. Revenue kya thi aave chhe? Kaya product/service thi sauthi vadhare kamai thay chhe?
  4. 6-12 mahina ma su achieve karvu chhe? Number ma - da. 100 leads/month, 2x sales.
  5. Atyare marketing kya thi chale chhe? Instagram, ads, referral, offline?
  6. Success kevi rite mapse? Calls, form fills, orders, store visits?

1.2.C Checklist

  • Main goal ek vakya ma lakhelu chhe
  • B2B ke B2C clear chhe
  • Success na numbers fix chhe
  • Goal na adhare j aagal nu planning thay chhe

1.2.D Khas Tip

Client bole "badhu j joiye" to priority puchho: "Jo next 3 mahina ma fakt ek j vastu improve thay, te kayi?" - te j real goal chhe.
