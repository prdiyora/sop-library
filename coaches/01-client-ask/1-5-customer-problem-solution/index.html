🎯 1.5 — Customer Problem & Solution

Dabbo 01 · Client Ask (R&D) · 🔴 REQUIRED — client pasethi lena mandatory ✅ Done

Customer koi product nahi, potani problem nu solution search kare chhe. Jo website problem first address kare ane pachhi solution aape, to Google ma pan rank saru male ane conversion pan.

1.5.A Problem kevi rite shodho

  1. Customer reviews vanchho - client na ane competitor na be. Loko su complain kare chhe?
  2. Google "People Also Ask" ma keyword lakhine juvo - te real problems chhe.
  3. Quora/Reddit/YouTube comments ma niche na prashno juvo.
  4. Client ne puchho: "Customer phone par sauthi vadhare su puche chhe?"

1.5.B Problem thi Solution nu mapping

Har problem mate ek answer:

  • Problem = blog topic / FAQ question
  • Solution = service/product page content
  • Example: "cake delivery late thay chhe" problem → "Same-day cake delivery in Surat - order before 4 PM" page

1.5.C Aa data kya vaparvu

  • FAQ section (AEO/GEO mate pan best)
  • Blog topics - problem keywords long-tail hoy, rank karva easy
  • Service page headings ma customer nu j word vapru

1.5.D Checklist

  • Top 10 customer problems ni list chhe
  • Har problem nu solution page/blog ma mapped chhe
  • FAQ ma real customer questions add karela chhe
