🎯 1.3 — Target Customer & Location

Dabbo 01 · Client Ask (R&D) · 🔴 REQUIRED — client pasethi lena mandatory ✅ Done

Customer kon chhe ane kya chhe - be vastu clear hoy to j sacha keywords, content ane design bane. Generic website badha mate, pan sales targeted website thi j aave.

1.3.A Customer Persona banavo

  1. Kon chhe? Umar group, gender, job, aavak nu level.
  2. Online shu search kare chhe? Customer Google ma su lakhe te exact words note karo.
  3. Temni samasya (problem) su chhe? Je solution mate te search kare chhe.
  4. Kya decision le chhe? Mobile par ke laptop? WhatsApp ke email?

1.3.B Location decide karo

  • Local dhando (salon, repair, classes): city + area keywords, Google Business Profile jaruri chhe.
  • India-level (India ecom/services): India-first keywords, Hindi/Gujarati/English mix content kaam kare chhe.
  • International (US/UK clients): English content, tya ni spelling (color vs colour), tya na competitors check karo.

1.3.C Udaharan

Surat ni bakery: target = Surat na 20-45 varas na loko, birthday/eggless cake search karta. Keywords: "eggless cake surat", "birthday cake delivery surat". USA client nu software: target = US small business owners, keywords in English, competitor US-based.

1.3.D Checklist

  • 1 clear customer persona lakhyo chhe
  • Location: local / India / international decide karel chhe
  • Customer na exact search words collect karela chhe
