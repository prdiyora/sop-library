Badha SOP par lagu

Senior golden rules

Ten rules that sit above every SOP in this library. When a doc and these rules disagree, the rules win.

  1. 01Data pehla, opinion pachhi. Decide from GSC/GA4; Semrush/Moz are estimates.
  2. 02Intent > volume. A keyword's intent and business value come first, volume second.
  3. 03Ek keyword-intent = ek URL. Check the Master Keyword Map before making any new page.
  4. 04White-hat only. No paid links, PBNs, fake reviews, scraped content or doorway pages. Kyarey nahi.
  5. 05Rules of thumb = guideline, law nahi. 50–60 char titles, 3–5 internal links, KD 0–30 — adjust to the situation.
  6. 06Speed = Core Web Vitals (real users). PSI score is a goal, not a ranking rule.
  7. 07Label every claim: CONFIRMED (Google official) / LIKELY (industry) / SPECULATION. Never promise a client a result.
  8. 08Kyarey number banavvo nahi. Every report carries its period and its source.
  9. 09Security. Passwords and tokens never go in a sheet, doc or chat — password manager only.
  10. 10Measure everything. Log the date of every change in the Page Audit & Fix log; judge it after 4–6 weeks.
Docs vachche conflict lage to: Google Search Central official docs → aa golden rules → specific SOP. SOP ma kai khotu dekhay to comment kari ne team lead ne janavo.
