Step 30 / 111

EEAT Follow on All Pages

EEAT = Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. E-E-A-T Google na Quality Rater Guidelines no concept chhe (direct ranking factor nathi), pan Google na systems trustworthy ane helpful content ne reward kare chhe - khas karine jo site health, finance, ya shopping related hoy (YMYL - Your Money Your Life).

4 pillars practical ma

  1. Experience: content lakhelu aeva vyakti pase thi jeney real experience chhe. Real photos, personal examples, "ame aa try karyu" style proof.
  2. Expertise: author bio with credentials, technical depth content ma, accurate information.
  3. Authoritativeness: industry ma recognition - backlinks, mentions, reviews, certifications.
  4. Trustworthiness: HTTPS, clear contact info, privacy policy, return/refund policy, real address/phone, ane secure payment.

Har page par implementation

  • Author name + bio har blog par
  • Facts mate sources cite karo
  • Updated date show karo
  • About Us ane Contact pages complete ane linked
  • Reviews/testimonials real names sathe

Jadpi audit

Ek visitor tamari site par aave ane puche "saalo, aa site par bharoso kemu karu?" - je answer page par visible chhe te j EEAT chhe.

Checklist

  • Badha content pages par author bios
  • Trust pages complete chhe (About, Contact, Privacy, Refund)
  • HTTPS active chhe, real contact details visible chhe
  • Facts/claims hoy tya sources cite karo

Step 31 / 111

Robots.txt & Sitemap Properly

Robots.txt Google ne kahe chhe su crawl karvu ane su nahi. Sitemap.xml Google ne site na badha pages nu map aape chhe. Banne technical SEO nu base chhe - kharab robots.txt important pages ne crawl thi roki shake; sitemap index ni guarantee nathi aaptu, pan discovery fast kare.

Robots.txt na niyamo

  1. Location: domain.com/robots.txt ma accessible hovu joiye.
  2. Crawl block karo: admin pages, cart, checkout, internal search result pages. Duplicate/filter URLs ane thank-you pages mate pehla canonical/noindex vaparo — robots.txt block fakt crawl waste motu hoy tyare (ane index mathi nikli gaya pachhi).
  3. Kyarey block na karo: CSS/JS files (Google render karvu joiye), main content pages.
  4. Sitemap line add karo: Sitemap: https://domain.com/sitemap.xml

Sitemap na niyamo

  1. Generate karo: WordPress ma Yoast/RankMath auto-banave; custom site ma generator tool.
  2. Fakt 200-status, canonical URLs sitemap ma hova joiye - no 404s, no redirects, no noindex pages.
  3. Submit karo: Google Search Console → Sitemaps → submit. Bing Webmaster Tools ma pan.
  4. Update automatic hovu joiye new page add thay tyare.

Common mistakes (aa bhool na karo)

  • "Disallow: /" staging ma hoy ane live site par j reh jay - site deindex!
  • Dead URLs sathe nu old sitemap
  • Sitemap submit karel pan GSC ma errors check na karela

Checklist

  • robots.txt accessible ane sitemap line sathe
  • Admin/cart/search pages block karela, content pages open
  • Sitemap GSC + Bing ma submit karel chhe, koi errors nathi
  • New pages sitemap ma auto-add thay

Step 32 / 111

Internal Linking (3-5 Per Page)

Internal links = tamari site na pages vachche na connection. Google aena thi site structure samje, authority flow thay, ane users vadhare pages jove. Notebook rule: har page par 3-5 internal links minimum.

Rules (niyamo)

  1. Darek page par 3-5 contextual links: content ma naturally fit thay aeva - footer/sidebar na links count ma nathi.
  2. Varietybharyu anchor text: "click here" nahi - "eggless cake delivery in Surat" jevu keyword-rich text.
  3. Hierarchy no flow: Blog → Category → Product/Service (money pages taraf authority flow).
  4. Deep pages ne pan link: fakt homepage/popular pages ne j nahi - nava ane deep pages ne pan.
  5. Anchor text ma variety: same page ne link karva different-different natural anchors use karo.

Structure nu example

  • Homepage → Category pages → Product pages (parent-child sambandh)
  • Blog "10 cake designs" mathi "chocolate truffle cake" product page ne link karo (contextual)
  • Related blogs ek bija ne link kare (topic cluster)

Checklist

  • Har page par 3-5 contextual internal links
  • Descriptive anchor text use karelu chhe
  • Money pages ne most internal links male chhe
  • Koi broken internal links nathi (dar mahine crawl check)

Step 33 / 111

Page Speed & Core Web Vitals (PageSpeed Insights)

Notebook rule: "PageSpeed Insight no use karine 90 ni upar hoy joiye". Slow site = users pacha jay ane sales ghate; Core Web Vitals Google na page experience signals no bhaag chhe (ranking ma nano factor, content pehla).

Kevi rite check karvu

  1. pagespeed.web.dev ma URL nakho
  2. Mobile score juvo pehla (desktop score misleading hoy)
  3. Main target: Core Web Vitals "Good" — real users nu data (GSC → Core Web Vitals report / PSI field data): LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1. PSI score 90+ = saro goal, ranking rule nahi (Shopify par mobile 60–80 hoy pan CWV pass hoy to chale).

Common fixes (sauthi vadhare impact vala pehla)

  1. Images optimize karo: WebP format, compressed (TinyPNG), lazy loading (fold niche ni images j — main/LCP image lazy nahi), yogya dimensions
  2. Caching on rakho: browser
  • server caching (WP ma WP Rocket/LiteSpeed)
  1. JS/CSS minimize karo: unused plugins remove karo, minify karo, non-critical JS defer karo
  2. Hosting upgrade karo: cheap shared hosting thi server response slow thay (TTFB < 600ms target)
  3. CDN use karo: Cloudflare free tier pan chale chhe
  4. Fonts limit: 2 fonts max, font-display: swap

Lighthouse 4 categories (juna SOP doc mathi)

  • Performance (speed ane loading)
  • Accessibility
  • Best Practices (shreshth paddhatio)
  • SEO
  • charma 90+ target rakho

Checklist

  • Key pages par Core Web Vitals pass (field data); Mobile PSI 90+ jo shakya hoy
  • Core Web Vitals green (GSC ma pan check)
  • Images WebP + lazy load karo
  • Monthly re-test schedule karo

Step 35 / 111

No Orphan Pages

Notebook (Gujarati): "Koi pan new page banavo to orphan page na rahe - jarur bija blog ma te te link kari do." Orphan page = jene site na koi pan page mathi link nahi miltu. Google ene mushkel thi find kare (fakt sitemap/backlink thi) ane internal authority nathi malti, users pan nahi pahochta - dead page.

Rule (niyam)

  1. Darek new page → tarat minimum 2-3 internal links: related blog mathi, category page mathi, ke related service page mathi.
  2. Sitemap ma hovu pan enough nathi - sitemap Google ne URL aape, pan internal link j authority aape.
  3. Banney taraf thi link karvu best: new page mathi pan related pages ne link aapo.

Orphan pages shodhva

  • Screaming Frog: XML sitemap sathe crawl karo (Configuration → Spider → Crawl Linked XML Sitemaps) → Crawl Analysis pachhi Sitemaps → Orphan URLs juo; GSC/GA4 pages list sathe pan compare karo
  • GSC ma pages je impressions nathi aapta - suspect list
  • Simple method: page nu URL site search karva try karo - kya thi link male te juvo

Nava page ni checklist (har vaar)

  • Page publish thaya pachhi 24 hours ma 2-3 internal links added
  • Related existing pages identify karela
  • Sitemap ma auto-add thay te confirm

Checklist

  • Ek pan orphan page nahi (quarterly audit)
  • New page → e j week ma internal linking complete
  • Old orphan pages ne links added ya removed/redirected

Step 36 / 111

AEO & GEO Content

Notebook: "Site content must be AEO & GEO content follow". AEO = Answer Engine Optimization (Google featured snippets, People Also Ask, voice search). GEO = Generative Engine Optimization (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity ma tamaro content cite thay). 2026 ma AI Overviews, AI Mode ane AI assistants ghana searches ma pehla answer aape chhe - etle visibility mate aa jaruri chhe.

AEO - Answer Engine mate

  1. Question-format na headings: H2/H3 ma real questions ("Eggless cake ketla divas fresh rahe?")
  2. Pehla direct answer: question pachhi 40-60 words ma direct answer, pachhi detail
  3. FAQ sections har important page par (users ane AI answers mate). Note: Google e 7 May 2026 thi FAQ rich results band karya chhe - FAQ schema thi have Google ma rich result nahi male
  4. Bolchal ni language: loko jem bole tem lakho
  5. Lists/steps/tables: Google snippet ma aava formats prefer kare

GEO - AI search mate (juna SOP doc na 3 factors merged)

  1. Data consistency, real data: facts accurate ane site bhar same (NAP, prices, claims)
  2. Trusted sources par presence: LLMs je sources trust kare tya tamaro content/presence hovo (Wikipedia, Reddit, Quora, industry sites, reviews)
  3. Machine-readable formats: Schema markup (Product, Article, Organization, LocalBusiness, Breadcrumb) - Technical SEO point 04 juvo

Priority na actions

  • Basic SEO pehla perfect, pachhi EEAT content, pachhi authority building, pachhi schema - aa order ma
  • Har page: brief answer first section → conversational language → FAQ section

Checklist

  • Top pages par FAQ + direct answers
  • Page type pramane schema implemented (FAQ schema optional)
  • Data ma consistency (site, GBP, socials)
  • Reddit/Quora/review sites par brand presence build karo

Step 37 / 111

Slug = Keyword (URL Structure)

Notebook point 8: "Slug all keyword + main". Slug = URL no last part. Clean, keyword-rich slug Google ne page nu topic instant samjave ane users ne pan trust aape.

Rules (niyamo)

  1. Primary keyword slug ma: /eggless-cake-delivery-surat (not /page?id=123 ke /post-45)
  2. Short ane clean: 3-5 words max. Stop words (and, the, nu, ke) remove karo.
  3. Hyphens use karo: /eggless-cake (not /eggless_cake, /egglesscake, /eggless%20cake)
  4. Lowercase j rakho: /About-Us nahi - /about-us
  5. Service/product slugs ma dates nahi: /best-cakes-2026 thay pan pachhi update karvu pade - evergreen rakho

Change karela slugs = redirects jaruri

Old URL change karo to 301 redirect must. Redirect vagar = 404 = rankings lost + backlinks waste.

Examples (udaharan)

  • Kharab: /index.php?cat=4&id=99 | Sara: /birthday-cakes
  • Kharab: /our-services-page | Sara: /seo-services
  • Kharab: /blog/how-to-do-keyword-research-for-your-new-website-in-2026 | Sara: /keyword-research-guide

Checklist

  • Badha slugs: keyword + nana + lowercase + hyphens
  • Badlela slugs par 301 redirects chhe
  • Main pages par parameter URLs na hova joie

Step 38 / 111

Title, Meta Description & H1-H6 Properly

Notebook point: "Title + description ma primary keyword + H1-H6 properly follow". Aa on-page SEO nu skeleton chhe - kharab hierarchy = Google confuse.

Title tag

  1. Primary keyword pehla (beginning ma)
  2. 50-60 characters (cutoff talva mate)
  3. Har page unique title
  4. Formula: Primary Keyword - Faydo/Modifier | Brand

Meta description

  1. 150-160 aksharo
  2. Primary keyword + faydo + CTA
  3. Unique per page, copy-paste nahi
  4. Direct ranking factor nathi - pan saru description CTR (clicks) vadhare chhe, ane vadhu clicks = vadhu traffic

Heading hierarchy (H1-H6)

  1. Ek j H1 per page - primary keyword sathe, page nu main topic
  2. H2 = mukhya sections (secondary keywords include karo)
  3. H3 = H2 na sub-sections of H2
  4. Levels kyarey skip na karo: H1 → H3 direct nahi, H1 → H2 → H3
  5. Styling mate headings use nahi: bold text joitu hoy to CSS, heading tag nahi

Udaharan structure

  • H1: Eggless Cake Delivery in Surat
  • H2: Why Choose Our Eggless Cakes
  • H3: 100% Vegetarian Ingredients
  • H2: Delivery Areas in Surat
  • H2: FAQ

Checklist

  • Darek page par primary keyword sathe 1 H1
  • Title 50-60 chars, pehla keyword, unique
  • Meta description 150-160 chars, CTA sathe
  • H1-H6 hierarchy clean, koi level skip nahi

Step 39 / 111

Keyword Mapping (Primary, Secondary, FAQ)

Notebook point 9: "Primary - secondary - FAQ all keywords properly follow in all pages". Har page ne clear keyword assignment hovi joiye - nahi to 2 pages same keyword mate fight kare (cannibalization) ane banna haray.

Darek page mate 3-tier structure

  1. Primary keyword (1): page nu main target. Aa aama aave: title, H1, slug, first paragraph, meta description.
  2. Secondary keywords (3-5): sambandhit variations. Aama aave: H2s, body content, image alt.
  3. FAQ keywords (3-5): question format ma. FAQ section ma sidha jawab sathe aave.

Mapping sheet banavo

  • Columns: Page URL | Primary keyword | Secondary keywords | FAQ keywords | Search intent | Status
  • Rule: 1 keyword = 1 page. Same keyword 2 pages par = problem.

Keyword Placement (density nahi)

  • Primary keyword title, H1, slug ane first paragraph ma aave - te enough chhe
  • Keyword density Google no ranking factor nathi - % target set na karo
  • Stuffing (same word vaar-vaar, unnatural) avoid karo - synonyms ane related terms naturally vapro.

Example (cake site)

  • Primary: "eggless cake delivery surat"
  • Secondary: "eggless birthday cake", "same day cake delivery", "order cake online surat"
  • FAQ: "eggless cake ketla divas fresh rahe?", "surat ma kaya area ma delivery chhe?"

Checklist

  • Keyword mapping sheet complete chhe
  • Koi 2 pages ma same primary keyword nahi
  • Natural keyword placement (stuffing nahi, % target nahi)
  • FAQ sections ma FAQ keywords

Step 42 / 111

Pre-publish QA

A. Content

  • EEAT: author name, real photo/example, date (published + updated).
  • Primary keyword title, H1, slug, first paragraph ma; secondary + FAQ keywords naturally.

B. On-page

  • Title ~50-60 char, meta description ~140-160 char, ek j H1, H2/H3 order.
  • Slug short + keyword. Canonical self-referencing.
  • 3-5 internal links; pillar ne link; page ne 2-3 jagya thi link (no orphan).
  • Images: real, compressed (WebP), alt text, filename keyword.

  • Index-able: noindex nathi, robots.txt block nathi (staging noindex hatavyu).
  • Mobile ma check; PageSpeed Insights ma real-user (field) data joyu.
  • Schema valid (Rich Results Test); product ma price + availability.
  • No broken links, no redirect chain.

  • GA4 / GTM fire thay chhe (Preview mode ma).
  • Key events (form, buy click) page par kaam kare chhe.
