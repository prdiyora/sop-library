Step 1 / 111

New SEO Project — Client Onboarding & Kickoff Master Doc

Sep 30, 2026 · Prepared by the team · Universal SOP — koi pan website mate: ecommerce, service, local, blog.

Aa doc kevi rite vaparvu

Navo project aave etle aa doc ni ek copy banavo, uper client nu naam ane date lakho, pachhi Phase 0 thi niche sudhi order-wise follow karo.

  • box ma kaam pura thay etle tick karo.
  • Phase 1 (Baseline) sauthi important chhe — koi pan kaam start karta pehla bharo. Aaje website kya chhe te lock kari devu, jethi 3–6 mahina pachhi "pehla aavu hatu, have aavu chhe" batavi sakay.
  • Har phase saathe rough time estimate aapel chhe (site size par depend kare).
  • Badhu badhi site par lagu nathi padtu — client no type joine (ecommerce / service / local) je lagu pade te j karo.

Order ni logic

Pehla client + access, pachhi baseline, pachhi audit, pachhi research, pachhi strategy. Aa flow follow karso to kadi confuse nahi thao.

PHASE 0 — Client Info & Access

Goal: client, business ane goal samjva + badha access ek saathe levaa. (Actual kaam 2–4 kalak, pan access aavta 2–4 divas jaay.)

0.1 Client Basic Info

  • Business name + website URL
  • Industry / niche
  • Business type: Ecommerce / Service / Local / SaaS / Blog
  • Contact person + WhatsApp/email
  • Decision maker kon? (changes kon approve karse)
  • Platform/CMS: Shopify / WordPress / Wix / Custom
  • Target area: local city / national / international
  • Languages

0.2 Business Understanding (loko aa skip kare — pan sauthi important)

  • Su vechay chhe / kai service? (top 3 money product/service)
  • USP — client bija karta su alag?
  • Ideal customer kon? (location, problem, budget)
  • Average order value / lead value (ROI samajva mate)
  • Seasonality? (koi mahina ma sales vadhe/ghate)

[Added] Related SOP: 01 Client Ask (R&D) → 01 Business Goal, 02 Target Customer, 03 USP, 04 Customer Problem

0.3 SEO Goals (spashta ane number ma)

  • Main goal: Leads / Sales / Traffic / Brand / Rankings — kayu?
  • Success ni definition: dakhla tarike "6 mahina ma organic leads double"
  • Timeline expectation
  • Budget (content, tools, backlinks)
  • Realistic expectation set karo — SEO 3–6 mahine result aape, overnight nahi

Senior note

Goal vague hoy ("traffic vadharvu") to project fail thavaano risk. Number ma convert karavo, nahi to result mapva nu koi base nahi rahe.

0.4 Competitor Info (client pase thi)

  • Client na hisaab e 3–5 business competitors kon?
  • Note: aa SEO competitors thi alag hoy sake — real SEO competitor Phase 3 ma SERP par thi nakki karis

0.5 Previous SEO History (chhupela problems ahi thi male)

  • Pehla koi agency/freelancer e kaam karyu? Su karyu?
  • Koi manual penalty ke moto traffic drop history?
  • Pehla backlink building thayu? (spammy links no risk)
  • Recent redesign / migration / URL change thayu?
  • Koi Google update thi affect thayu lage chhe?

[Added] Related SOP: 10 Reporting & Maintenance → 02 Traffic Drop Diagnosis; 08 Technical SEO → 07 Website Redesign & Migration SEO

0.6 Access Collection (badha ek saathe mango, delay avoid)

  • Google Search Console (GSC)
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4)
  • Google Tag Manager (jo hoy)
  • CMS admin (WordPress / Shopify admin)
  • Hosting / cPanel (technical fixes mate)
  • Google Business Profile (local hoy to)
  • Google Merchant Center (ecommerce hoy to)
  • Social media accounts
  • Ahrefs / Semrush (jo client pase hoy)
  • [Added] Bing Webmaster Tools ane Microsoft Clarity (na hoy to setup karvanu note karo)

[Added] Access hamesha client na account mathi user add karavi ne levo (GSC/GA4/GMC ma "Add user"), password share karavvano nahi.

[Added] Related SOP: 07 Setup & Tracking → 01 GSC, 02 GA4 & GTM, 04 Clarity, 05 Bing

PHASE 2 — First Full SEO Audit

Order ni logic

Pehla technical (foundation), pachhi on-page (page level), pachhi content (quality), pachhi links (authority). Foundation kharab hoy to uper nu badhu kaam waste jaay — etle technical first.

2.1 Technical SEO Audit (4–8 kalak, site size par)

  • Crawl karo — Screaming Frog (free 500 URLs) ke Ahrefs Site Audit
  • Robots.txt check
  • XML sitemap (submit thayu chhe? clean chhe?)
  • Indexing issues — GSC Pages report (kai pages index nathi ne kem)
  • Canonical tags
  • Redirects (301/302), redirect chains, loops
  • 404 / soft 404 errors
  • Duplicate content / duplicate URLs
  • HTTPS + mixed content
  • Mobile-friendly
  • Core Web Vitals / page speed
  • Structured data / schema
  • Orphan pages, crawl budget (moti site mate)
  • Pagination + filter/faceted URLs (ecommerce mate khaas)
  • hreflang (international hoy to)
  • [Added] GSC → Manual actions ane Security issues check

[Added] Related SOP: 08 Technical SEO → 01–06 (khaas 06 Technical SEO Audit)

2.2 On-Page SEO Audit (3–6 kalak)

  • Title tags (missing / duplicate / length / keyword)
  • Meta descriptions
  • H1 + heading structure
  • URL structure
  • Keyword placement + search intent match
  • Image alt text
  • Money pages par internal links

[Added] Related SOP: 05 On-Page SEO folder

2.3 Content Audit (3–6 kalak)

  • Kai pages traffic laave, kai nakami (GSC + GA4)
  • Thin / duplicate / outdated content
  • Keyword cannibalization (2 page same keyword par)
  • Content gaps
  • Decision per page: create / update / merge / prune

[Added] Related SOP: 10 Reporting & Maintenance → 03 Content Refresh, Merge ya Remove

2.4 Internal Linking Audit (2–3 kalak)

  • Money pages ne ketla internal links male chhe
  • Orphan pages (koi link nathi male evi)
  • Anchor text quality
  • Hub-and-spoke structure chhe ke nai

2.5 Off-Page / Backlink Audit (2–3 kalak)

  • Referring domains + DR
  • Backlink quality (relevant ke spammy)
  • Toxic/spam links — disavow ni jarurat? [Added: disavow fakt manual action ke paid/spam link history hoy to j; mota bhaag ni sites ne jarur nathi]
  • Anchor text distribution
  • Competitor saathe backlink gap

[Added] Related SOP: 06 Off-Page SEO → 03 Backlink Submission, 07 DA PA & Backlink Health

2.6 Local SEO (jo local business — 1–2 kalak)

  • Google Business Profile complete + verified
  • NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone badhe same)
  • Reviews count + rating
  • Local citations
  • Location pages

[Added] Related SOP: 06 Off-Page SEO → 04 Local SEO & Listings, 05 Global Business Submission

2.7 Ecommerce Extra (jo ecommerce)

  • Merchant Center errors
  • Product schema + review schema
  • Out-of-stock / discontinued product handling
  • Category vs product page structure

[Added] Related SOP: 09 Ecommerce SEO folder

2.8 Social + AI Search Quick Check (~30 min)

  • Social profiles active + site saathe linked
  • Brand ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google AI Overview ma dekhaay chhe? (baseline note)

Total audit time: nani site 1–2 divas, medium 3–5 divas, moti site 1–2 week.

PHASE 3 — Keyword & Competitor Research (audit pachhi)

3.1 Keyword Research (4–8 kalak)

  • Seed keywords (business samji ne)
  • Expand: GSC queries, Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs/Semrush, autocomplete + People Also Ask
  • Intent classify: informational / commercial / transactional / navigational
  • Volume + difficulty + priority nakki karo
  • Keyword clustering (related keywords na groups)
  • Keyword mapping (kayu keyword kai page par jase)

[Added] Related SOP: 03 Strategy → 06 Semrush Keyword to Content SOP; 05 On-Page SEO → 10 Keyword Mapping

3.2 Competitor SEO Analysis (3–5 kalak)

  • Real SEO competitor nakki karo — SERP ma actually kon rank thaay (business competitor thi alag hoy sake)
  • Temna top keywords + rankings
  • Temnu content (kai topics par, keva depth ma)
  • Temna backlinks (kya thi male chhe)
  • Content gap: te rank thaay ne client nathi thato — te opportunities note karo

Order kem aavo?

Keyword research PEHLA, competitor analysis PACHHI. Karan: competitor analysis thi tamari keyword list ma nava keywords add thaay chhe ane gaps male chhe — etle pehla potanu base banavo, pachhi competitor thi enrich karo.

[Added] Related SOP: 02 Domain Research → 02 Competitor Pool Analysis; 03 Strategy → 03 Competitor Gap + Keyword Gap

PHASE 4 — SEO Strategy & Roadmap (3–5 kalak)

  • Money pages nakki karo (business value sauthi vadhu je pages par)
  • Priority list — Impact × Effort framework: High impact + Low effort = pehla karo
  • Technical fixes priority order
  • Content plan — pillar pages + topic clusters + monthly calendar
  • Internal linking plan (money pages ne links kya thi male)
  • Off-page plan (quality > quantity)
  • Nava content nu sitemap/list
  • Timeline + milestones (Month 1, 2, 3…)
  • Reporting setup — Looker Studio dashboard + monthly report cadence

Senior Reminders (har project e yaad rakho)

  1. Baseline vagar result batavi nahi shakay — te sauthi pehla, kaam start karta pehla bharo.
  2. Access delay = project delay. Badha access Day 1 e ek saathe mango.
  3. Audit no order (technical → on-page → content → links) foundation-first logic chhe — na todo.
  4. Keyword research pehla, competitor analysis pachhi — competitor thi list enrich thaay.
  5. "Spam score", "DA/DR" jeva third-party metrics signal chhe, Google no ranking factor nathi — client ne evu na kaho ke aa Google no factor chhe.

Step 2: Business goal →

Business goal samajvu — 4 steps

1

▶Ask the client what they want

Sales, leads or branding? One main answer, not 'everything'.

2

⚲Ask who buys and how the money comes

B2B or B2C? Which product or service earns the most?

3

▤Put a number on success

Example: 100 leads a month in 6 months. A goal without a number is a wish.

4

✓Write the goal in one sentence

Every keyword, page and design decision must serve this one sentence.

Step 2 / 111

Business Goal samajvu

Client Ask (R&D) no pehlo point. Koi pan website par kaam chalu karta pehla client no main goal clear hovo joiye - bija badha decisions (keywords, design, content) aa goal par j depend kare chhe.

Kema important?

Goal vagar SEO karo to traffic aave pan business ne faydo na thay. Example: client ne leads joiye ane tame blog traffic vadharo - traffic aavyu, inquiry nahi. Etle pehla goal, pachhi strategy.

Client ne aa questions puchho

  1. Main goal su chhe? Sales (ecommerce), Leads/inquiries (service), ya Branding/awareness?
  2. Business model su chhe? B2B (business ne vechay) ke B2C (direct customer ne)?
  3. Revenue kya thi aave chhe? Kaya product/service thi sauthi vadhare kamai thay chhe?
  4. 6-12 mahina ma su achieve karvu chhe? Number ma - da. 100 leads/month, 2x sales.
  5. Atyare marketing kya thi chale chhe? Instagram, ads, referral, offline?
  6. Success kevi rite mapse? Calls, form fills, orders, store visits?

Checklist

  • Main goal ek vakya ma lakhelu chhe
  • B2B ke B2C clear chhe
  • Success na numbers fix chhe
  • Goal na adhare j aagal nu planning thay chhe

Khas Tip

Client bole "badhu j joiye" to priority puchho: "Jo next 3 mahina ma fakt ek j vastu improve thay, te kayi?" - te j real goal chhe.

1

☺Draw your customer

Age, job, what they type into Google, which problem they want solved.

3

Step 3 / 111

Target Customer & Location

Customer kon chhe ane kya chhe - be vastu clear hoy to j sacha keywords, content ane design bane. Generic website badha mate, pan sales targeted website thi j aave.

Customer Persona banavo

  1. Kon chhe? Umar group, gender, job, aavak nu level.
  2. Online shu search kare chhe? Customer Google ma su lakhe te exact words note karo.
  3. Temni samasya (problem) su chhe? Je solution mate te search kare chhe.
  4. Kya decision le chhe? Mobile par ke laptop? WhatsApp ke email?

Location decide karo

  • Local dhando (salon, repair, classes): city + area keywords, Google Business Profile jaruri chhe.
  • India-level (India ecom/services): India-first keywords, Hindi/Gujarati/English mix content kaam kare chhe.
  • International (US/UK clients): English content, tya ni spelling (color vs colour), tya na competitors check karo.

Udaharan

Surat ni bakery: target = Surat na 20-45 varas na loko, birthday/eggless cake search karta. Keywords: "eggless cake surat", "birthday cake delivery surat". USA client nu software: target = US small business owners, keywords in English, competitor US-based.

Checklist

  • 1 clear customer persona lakhyo chhe
  • Location: local / India / international decide karel chhe
  • Customer na exact search words collect karela chhe

USP shodho — 4 steps

1

⚲Open the top 5 competitors' websites

Note the main claim on each homepage.

2

⌕Find what the client offers that others don't

Free delivery, warranty, 10 years of experience — anything competitors can't say.

3

★Ask the client directly

Why do customers pick you? Check reviews for the same pattern.

4

✎Put the USP on the homepage

H1, title and meta description. Never 'Welcome to our website'.

Step 4 / 111

USP - Uniqueness shodho

USP etle Unique Selling Point - tamara client ma aevu su chhe je competitor ma nathi? USP clear hoy to title, content ane ads badha sharp bane. USP na hoy to website "beeja jevi j" lage ane Google pan ene special rank nahi aape.

USP kevi rite shodho

  1. Top 5 competitors ni website kholo ane temna homepage na main claims note karo.
  2. Su offer kare chhe je bija nahi karta? Free delivery, same-day service, 10 varas experience, lowest price, handmade, warranty?
  3. Customers sauthi vadhare su vakhan kare chhe? Reviews vanchi ne pattern shodho.
  4. Client ne seedho puchho: "Customer tamne j kema pasand kare chhe?"

USP ne kya vaparvo

  • Homepage na H1 ane title ma
  • Meta description ma (CTR vadharva)
  • Product/service pages na first paragraph ma
  • Schema ma (Offer, priceRange)

Example

Bija badha jewellers "best designs" lakhe chhe. Jo client 100% BIS hallmark ane lifetime exchange aape chhe - te j USP chhe. Homepage par "BIS Hallmark Gold | Lifetime Exchange" bolu joiye, "Welcome to our website" nahi.

Checklist

  • 1 main USP + 2 supporting USP lakhela chhe
  • USP homepage na H1/title ma dekhay chhe
  • USP competitor na claims thi different chhe (copy nathi)

Customer problem → solution — 4 steps

1

⌕Hunt for complaints

Reviews (client's and competitors'), People Also Ask, Reddit, YouTube comments.

2

☰List the top 10 problems

Real questions customers actually ask, in their own words.

3

➜Match each problem to an answer

Problem becomes a blog topic or FAQ; the solution becomes page content.

4

✓Add the real questions to the FAQ

Problem keywords are long-tail — easy to rank, great for AEO/GEO.

Step 5 / 111

Customer Problem & Solution

Customer koi product nahi, potani problem nu solution search kare chhe. Jo website problem first address kare ane pachhi solution aape, to Google ma pan rank saru male ane conversion pan.

Problem kevi rite shodho

  1. Customer reviews vanchho - client na ane competitor na be. Loko su complain kare chhe?
  2. Google "People Also Ask" ma keyword lakhine juvo - te real problems chhe.
  3. Quora/Reddit/YouTube comments ma niche na prashno juvo.
  4. Client ne puchho: "Customer phone par sauthi vadhare su puche chhe?"

Problem thi Solution nu mapping

Har problem mate ek answer:

  • Problem = blog topic / FAQ question
  • Solution = service/product page content
  • Example: "cake delivery late thay chhe" problem → "Same-day cake delivery in Surat - order before 4 PM" page

Aa data kya vaparvu

  • FAQ section (AEO/GEO mate pan best)
  • Blog topics - problem keywords long-tail hoy, rank karva easy
  • Service page headings ma customer nu j word vapru

Checklist

  • Top 10 customer problems ni list chhe
  • Har problem nu solution page/blog ma mapped chhe
  • FAQ ma real customer questions add karela chhe
