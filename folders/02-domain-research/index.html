Step 6 / 127

Competitor Pool Analysis

Tamara top competitors juvo etle shortcut: te loko shu rank kare chhe, su lakhe chhe, kya thi backlinks le chhe - badhu free ma roadmap male.

Steps

  1. Top 3-5 competitors shodho: main keyword Google ma search karo - first page par je consistency sathe dekhay te j real competitors.
  2. Temna top pages note karo: kaya pages sauthi vadhare traffic lave chhe (Ubersuggest/Semrush free trial).
  3. Temna keywords juvo: kaya keywords par rank kare chhe je tamara pase nathi - te gap list chhe.
  4. Content style juvo: ketlu lamba articles, images, videos, FAQ structure, schema.
  5. Backlink sources note karo: kya sites temne link kare chhe - je relevant sites (directories, associations, PR, suppliers) tamne pan naturally link aapi shake te target list ma lo; paid/spam placements skip.

Pool mathi su levu

  • Je temna ma chhe pan tamara ma nathi (features, pages, topics)
  • Temni weakness (slow site, old content, no FAQ) - tya jeeto
  • Temna best content thi 10x better content banavvo

Checklist

  • 3-5 competitors ni list + notes chhe
  • Keyword gap list banavi chhe
  • Temna 3 strengths ane 3 weaknesses lakhela chhe

Step 7 / 127

Target Country Selection

Kaya country ne target karvu te pehla decide karo - keywords, content language, currency, examples badhu aena par depend kare chhe.

Nirnay na factors

  1. Customer kya chhe? (Client Ask na point 2 juvo)
  2. Kaya country ma competition ocho ane budget fit? India ma CPC ochu, US ma high - pan US customers nu ticket size motu.
  3. Product/service shipping ke service possible chhe? Physical product ma delivery area j check karo.
  4. Payment ane language ready? US target to USD, card payments, English content.

Amal

  • Local: city name keywords + Google Business Profile + local listings
  • India: India-first keywords, pricing INR ma, Indian examples ane payment options
  • International: hreflang (jo multiple countries), country-specific domain ke subfolder (/us/), tya na competitors benchmark

Udaharan

Priyank nu rule Wibestone mate: "First priority India, then US and UK". Aetle keywords India volume pramane, pachhi US/UK pages alag optimize.

Checklist

  • Primary country fix chhe (1st priority)
  • Secondary countries listed chhe
  • Content language/currency country pramane set chhe

Step 8 / 127

Quick Win Finding

Quick win etle aevi opportunities je ocha effort ma jaldi result aape. Navi site par 3-6 mahina lage, pan quick wins 2-4 week ma movement batave.

Quick wins kevi rite shodho

  1. Position 5-20 vala pages: Search Console ma juvo kaya keywords par page 5-20 position par chhe - title, description, content thodu improve karo ne top 5 ma aavi shake.
  2. Low KD + sara volume keywords: je competitors e miss karela.
  3. People Also Ask na questions: FAQ add karo ne featured snippet male.
  4. Zero-click sudhara: title/description CTR improve karo - same position par pan traffic vadhare.
  5. Orphan ane thin pages fix karo: je pages ne koi link nathi, tene link aapo.

Priority nakki karvanu formula

Quick win = (saru potential traffic) x (ochu effort). Pehla te karo je 1 kalak ma thay ane 30 divas ma result batave.

Checklist

  • Search Console mathi top 10 opportunity pages listed
  • 5 low-KD keywords pasand karela
  • Dar quick win mate owner ane deadline fix chhe

Step 9 / 127

6 Month Planning & Execution

SEO marathon chhe, sprint nahi. 6 mahina nu realistic plan banavvo etle client pan wait kare ane kaam pan systematic chale.

Mahina-pramane plan

  1. Month 1: Full audit (on-page, technical, content) karo, quick wins implement karo, GSC/GA4 setup verify karo.
  2. Month 2: On-page fixes (title, meta, headings, internal links) karo, must-have pages complete karo.
  3. Month 3: Content engine chalu - week ma 2-4 blogs, keyword gap cover karva.
  4. Month 4: Off-page chalu - backlinks, Reddit/Quora, social signals.
  5. Month 5: Content update (old pages refresh), schema ane AEO/GEO optimization.
  6. Month 6: Review
  • su work karyu, su nahi. Double down on working pages, strategy adjust.

Tracking

  • Dar mahine: impressions, clicks, position (GSC), users (GA4), leads/sales note karo
  • Apeksha: Mahina 1-2 ma flat, Mahina 3-4 ma movement, Mahina 5-6 ma visible growth

Checklist

  • 6 mahina nu written plan chhe
  • Monthly metrics track thay chhe
  • Client ne timeline pehle divas e j samjavi chhe

Step 10 / 127

Domain Name Finding Strategy

Saru domain name = brand nu foundation. Lamba term ma SEO, branding ane marketing badha ma faydo kare.

3 rules (notebook pramane)

  1. Keyword pramane: main keyword domain ma hoy to relevance signal male (da. "suratcakes.in"). Pan exact-match domain par over-optimize nahi - brand + keyword mix best.
  2. Country pramane: India target to .in, global to .com. Local trust mate country domain better rank kare te country ma.
  3. Yaad rakhva easy: 2-3 words max, spelling easy, phone par bolva ma samjay aevu, no hyphens/numbers jo avoid thay to.

Check karvana steps

  1. Namecheap/GoDaddy ma availability check karo
  2. Same name Instagram/Facebook/YouTube handle ma pan male te juvo
  3. Google ma name search karo - koi existing brand/negative result to nahi?
  4. Trademark conflict to nahi (India ma ipindia.gov.in)

Example

"RadhaVision Infotech" - brand name short, yaad rahi jay evu, .com available, social handles same. Ekdam perfect combination.

Checklist

  • Short (2-3 words), spell-easy, bolva-easy
  • Sachu extension (.com / .in)
  • Social handles available chhe
  • Trademark conflict nathi

Step 11 / 127

Keyword Research Guide — Nava Page mate Keyword kevi rite Pick karvu

Priyank Diyora · 30 September 2026 · Semrush thi detail process: 03 Strategy → 06 Semrush Keyword to Content SOP

[Added] Team standard Keyword data mate team no main tool Semrush chhe (Global Business Submission SOP pramane). Existing site hoy to pehla GSC juo — e real data chhe. Niche na free tools tyare vaparo jyare Semrush na hoy ke quick idea joiye.

1. Keyword etle shu

Keyword etle e exact shabd ke phrase je loko Google ma type kare chhe. Tamara page nu content e search sathe match thay, to Google e page ne result ma batave chhe.

Ek example: koi Google ma type kare "ghar par easy science experiment" — aakhu e vakya j keyword chhe, fakt "science" nahi.

Ek vaat yaad rakho: keyword e fakt shabd nathi, e user ni need chhe. Same shabd pachhal alag-alag need hoi shake — "laptop" search karnar koi kharidva mange chhe, koi fakt compare karva mange chhe. Etle shabd ni sathe e need (search intent) pan jovi padshe.

2. Keyword kem important chhe

Keyword nakki kare chhe ke page kona mate lakhayu chhe. Keyword focus vagar page lakho to page kai search mate chhe e clear nathi rahetu — Google ane users banne mate.

  • Sacha loko aave chhe — je user ne kharekhar tamari service ke content joiye chhe e j aave, etle bounce ochho ane conversion vadhu.
  • Free traffic — ek vaar rank thay pachhi ad kharcha vagar mahino-mahino traffic aave.
  • Content ne disha male — keyword khabar hoy to shu lakhvu, ketlu lakhvu, kaya subtopic cover karva e badhu clear thai jay.
  • Page-page vachche takrav na thay — dar page ne potano alag keyword aapo to be page ek-bija sathe competition nathi karta.

3. Keyword na types

Be rite type padta hoy chhe — lambai pramane ane intent (user ni need) pramane. Nava page mate banne jova padshe.

Intent match na thay to page game tetlu saru hoy, rank nahi thay. Transactional keyword par blog mukso to Google e blog ne upar nahi lave.

4. Nava page mate keyword kevi rite shodhvu (7 steps)

Aa 7 step dar vakhte same order ma follow karo — koi pan nava page mate kaam aavshe. Aakhi process ma 30–45 minute thay chhe.

  1. Page nu kaam nakki karo (2 min): Ek line ma lakho — aa page kona mate chhe ane page par aavine user shu karshe (vanchshe / inquiry karshe / kharidshe). Aa line j intent nakki karshe.
  2. Seed keyword ni list banavo (5 min): Tool kholya vagar, page na topic par 5–10 shabd pote lakho — jem customer bolta hoy tem. Client ni website, juni inquiries, sales team na shabdo ane competitor na menu mathi pan seed male.
  3. Google parthi j expand karo (5 min): Free ane sauthi sacho source. Seed type karo ane autocomplete na suggestions note karo; result page niche "Related searches" juo; "People Also Ask" box na questions note karo — e FAQ ane H2 mate seedha kaam lage.
  4. Tool ma nakhi ne list moti karo (10 min): Seed ne Semrush Keyword Magic Tool (target country database) ma nakho ane volume, KD ane variations kadho. 30–50 keyword ni ek list banavo.
  5. Competitor pasethi shikho (5 min): Je 3 site aa topic par top ma rank kare chhe, temna URL Semrush Organic Research ma nakho. Temne kaya keyword par traffic male chhe e juo ane je tamari list ma nathi e umero. Temna title ane H1 pan juo — e temnu primary keyword j hoy chhe. (Content copy nahi — fakt keyword ideas.)
  6. Google ma search kari ne SERP check karo (5 min): Aa step mota bhaag na loko chhodi de chhe. Top 10 ma kaya type na page chhe? Badha blog hoy ane tame product page banavta ho to match nathi thatu. Top ma fakt moti authority sites (Amazon, Wikipedia, Government) hoy to e keyword hamna chhodo. Nana ke nava domain dekhay to e keyword tamara mate jitay evu chhe.
  7. Ek primary keyword final karo (3 min): Niche na 5 check lagu karo ane ek keyword primary tarike nakki karo. Baki na 3–5 keyword secondary tarike rakho — e H2 ane body ma vaprashe.
Short ma Topic → Seed → Google suggestions → Tool → Competitor → SERP check → Final pick.

Keyword par final tick tyare j lagavo jyare panche ma "haa" aave.

Ghani vaar 0 volume batavtu keyword pan kaam nu hoy chhe — khaas kari ne local ane B2B ma, jya tool pase data ocho hoy chhe. Jo keyword logical hoy ane competitor e vaparyu hoy, to te lai shakay.

7. Ek page = ek primary keyword

Dar page ne ek j primary keyword aapo, ane e keyword biji koi page ne na aapo. Sathe 3–5 secondary keyword rakho je same intent na hoy — e H2 ma ane body ma naturally aavshe.

Keyword cannibalization thi bachvu

Be page same keyword ane same intent par target kare to Google vaaramvar page badle (fluctuation) ane banne weak rahi shake. Aa thi bachva mate:

  • Ek Master Keyword Map sheet rakho (niche section 8 nu standard format).
  • Nava page nu keyword nakki karta pehla e sheet ma search kari lo ke e keyword pehla thi vaparelu chhe ke nahi.
  • Pehla thi hoy to navu page banavvana badle e junu page j update karvu vadhu saru chhe.
  • Quick rit: Google ma site:tamarisite.com "keyword" search karo — ketla page e keyword par chhe e dekhai jashe. GSC → Query → Pages tab pan juo.

10. Karvu ane na karvu

Karvu

  • Long-tail thi sharuat karo, pachhi authority vadhe tem short keyword taraf jao.
  • SERP dar vakhte pote juo — tool no number khoto hoi shake, SERP nahi.
  • Question-type keyword alag list ma rakho, e FAQ ane featured snippet mate kaam aave.
  • Local business hoy to shaher nu naam umero: "seo services in surat".
  • Dar 3–6 mahine Search Console ma juo ke page kaya keyword par aavi rahyu chhe — nava idea tyathi male.

Na karvu

  • Keyword stuffing — ek j shabd vaar-vaar ghusadvo. Google e pakde chhe ane ranking ghate chhe.
  • Ek page par 5–6 alag-alag intent na keyword target karva.
  • Fakt volume joine keyword pasand karvo, difficulty ane SERP joya vagar.
  • Exact match pachhal padvu — grammar todi ne lakhvani jarur nathi, natural vakya chalshe.
  • Competitor je keyword par chhe e badha j copy karva — temni authority tamara karta alag chhe.
  • Keyword nakki karya vagar content lakhi nakhvu ane pachhi keyword fit karvano prayas karvo.
