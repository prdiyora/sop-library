Step 43 / 127

Reddit & Quora Marketing

Notebook point 1: "Reddit & Quora". AA be platforms = trust building + referral traffic + GEO signals (LLMs Reddit/Quora ne source tarike use kare chhe). Direct link spam nahi - genuine help pehla.

Quora ni strategy

  1. Niche na questions shodho (search: tamaru keyword + Quora)
  2. Real, detailed answers lakho - 300+ words, helpfulness pehla
  3. Link naturally context ma - har answer ma link nahi (10 answers ma 2-3 ma)
  4. Profile complete rakho with credentials
  5. High-view questions target karo (existing traffic)

Reddit ni strategy

  1. Relevant subreddits join karo (r/SEO, niche subs, local subs)
  2. 90/10 rule: 90% genuine help/participation, self-promotion maximum 10% sudhi
  3. Account karma build karo pehla - new account + links = instant ban
  4. AMA, case studies, "here's what I learned" posts sauthi saras kaam kare chhe
  5. Subreddit rules vanchho - koi subs ma links banned

GEO bonus

ChatGPT/Perplexity na answers ma Reddit/Quora threads frequently cite thay. Authentic helpful presence = AI search ma pan visibility.

Checklist

  • 5-10 target subreddits/Quora topics ni list banaveli

  • Dar hafta participation schedule (30 min/day purtu chhe)

  • Pehla helpful answers, links ochha use karo

  • Account profiles real info sathe complete rakho

  • Added 8 Nov 2026 — AI citation context

  • 2026 industry studies ma Reddit AI answers (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews) ma sauthi vadhare cite thata sources ma chhe — genuine Reddit presence GEO mate vadhu valuable. [LIKELY — industry studies]

  • Pan perspective: AI referral traffic overall web traffic no ~0.3% chhe — Reddit/Quora nu main value trust + brand + referrals, direct traffic machine nahi. 90/10 rule jovano.

Step 45 / 127

Backlink Submission (Quality Over Quantity)

Notebook point 3: "Backlink submission". Backlink = trust no vote. (Juna SOP doc mathi merged: "jyare ghani high quality sites tamara content ne vote aape chhe, tyare search engines ne signal male chhe ke page important ane relevant chhe.")

Good backlink na criteria (junu SOP merged)

  1. Relevant site: tamari niche/industry related site mathi
  2. High authority: DA/DR vadhare (Moz/Ahrefs check)
  3. Real traffic vali site: jene potanu traffic chhe
  4. Natural anchor text: Ek j keyword anchor vaaramvaar = spam signal
  5. Do-follow preferred: pan no-follow pan natural profile mate jaruri

Backlink types (junu SOP mathi)

  • Editorial: natural rite mention thay - best quality
  • Guest post: relevant blog par saacho useful article (fakt link mate nahi); paid hoy to link rel="sponsored"
  • Business listings: directories, local citations
  • Profile links: forums, communities
  • Web 2.0 / Social bookmarks: ochhi value, ochha praman ma

Free methods je work kare

  1. Guest posting: niche blogs ne pitch karo
  2. Journalist query platforms / digital PR: expert quotes = authority links (platform active chhe te pehla check karo)
  3. Broken link building: competitors na broken links shodho, tamaro content offer karo
  4. Directory submissions: fakt relevant, quality directories (10-15 max)
  5. Competitor backlinks study: temna relevant, quality sources mathi j target list banavo (paid/spam/PBN skip)

Avoid (penalty risk)

  • Paid link schemes, Fiverr "1000 backlinks for $5"
  • Link farms, PBNs, spam comments
  • Darek jagyae same anchor text

Checklist

  • Darek mahine backlink target (5-10 quality > 100 spam)
  • Competitor backlink analysis thai gayu
  • Anchor text ni variety maintain thayeli chhe
  • Disavow fakt manual action ke paid/spam link history hoy to j (mota bhaag ni sites ne jarur nathi)

Step 47 / 127

Global Business Submission (All Countries)

Source: Divy nu SOP (Nov 6, 2026). New website ane Existing website banne mate. Keyword research fakt Semrush thi.

Senior ni note Business submission directly ranking nathi vadhartu. Eno sacho fayado che brand trust, entity signals (Google ne samjay ke business real che), referral traffic ane AI search ma brand mentions. Etle quantity nahi, quality ane consistency par focus rakhvo.

Step 0: Master Business Sheet banavo

Ek Google Sheet ma badhi details ek j format ma lakho, pachhi badhe copy-paste karo.

  • Business Name (exact same spelling, badhe)
  • Address (jo physical office hoy to), Phone with country code, Email
  • Website URL (https, ek j version, e.g. https://example.com/)
  • Short description (150 chars) ane Long description (500–750 chars)
  • Category: primary + 2–3 secondary
  • Logo (square), Cover image, 5–10 real photos
  • Opening hours, Social profile links, Founded year

Why: NAP (Name, Address, Phone) badhe same hovu joie. Alag-alag hoy to Google confuse thay ane trust ghate.

Step 1: Semrush thi keyword research (description mate)

  1. Semrush ma Keyword Magic Tool kholo.
  2. Database country select karo (US, UK, AU, CA, IN — je country ma submit karo cho e j).
  3. Seed keyword nakho (e.g. kids skincare, diesel repair).
  4. Filter: Intent = Commercial / Transactional, KD% low–medium.
  5. 3–5 main keywords choose karo ane description ma naturally vaparo, stuffing nahi.
  6. Same country ma competitor domain Organic Research ma nakhi jovo ke e kaya keywords par rank kare che.

Step 48 / 127

YouTube Channel Managing (SEO support mate)

Source: Divy nu SOP (Nov 6, 2026). New ane Existing website banne mate. Keyword research Semrush thi.

Senior note YouTube directly website ranking nathi vadhartu. Fayado: brand trust, video SERP ma extra jagya, referral traffic ane AI search ma brand mentions. Consistency ane website sathe connection j asli value che.

Step 0: Channel setup (ekvar)

  • Channel name = brand name (bilkul same)
  • Handle (), logo, banner
  • About section: Semrush keywords sathe 300–500 words description + website link + country
  • Links section: website, Instagram, LinkedIn
  • Settings ma channel keywords: 5–10 main keywords
  • Playlists = website ni main categories / services pramane

Step 1: Semrush thi video keyword research

  1. Keyword Magic Tool ma target country database select karo.
  2. Questions tab vaparo (how, what, why, best) — videos mate best.
  3. Intent: Informational (tutorials) + Commercial (best X, X vs Y, review).
  4. SERP Features column ma Video feature jovo. Je keywords par Google already video batave che e priority.
  5. Final check: YouTube search bar ma keyword type kari autocomplete jovo.
Honest note Semrush ni volume Google search ni che, YouTube ni nahi. Etle Semrush thi topic ane intent nakki karo, YouTube autocomplete thi demand confirm karo.

Step 2: Dar video publish checklist

  • Title: main keyword shuruat ma, 60 chars ni andar, click-worthy
  • Description: pehli 2 line ma keyword + value, pachhi summary, related website page ni link, timestamps
  • Chapters / timestamps 0:00 thi shuru (Google ma key moments aave)
  • Tags: 5–10 relevant (minor factor)
  • Custom thumbnail: readable text, brand color
  • Captions check ane sudhaaro; global mate English + target language
  • Playlist ma add, end screen + cards set
  • Pinned comment ma website link

Step 3: Website sathe connect karo (asli SEO fayado)

  • Video ne related blog / product / service page par embed karo
  • Ae page par video ni short summary ke transcript lakho
  • VideoObject schema add karo ane Rich Results Test thi check karo
  • Video thi page par ane page thi video par, banne baju link

Step 4: New website ane Existing website mate

Navi website / channel:

  • Mahine 4 videos (week ma 1); pehla consistency
  • Topics: brand intro, top 3 product/service explain, top 5 FAQ
  • Dar long video mathi 2–3 Shorts kaadho

Pehlethi chalu website:

  • GSC Performance → Queries ma "how to / what is" questions sodho jema impressions vadhu che; aej video topics
  • Top 10 traffic wala blogs mate video banavo ane embed karo
  • Ochha views wala juna videos na title, description, thumbnail update karo
  • Semrush Organic Research ma competitor na video-SERP keywords sodho

Step 5: Mahinani reporting

  • YouTube Studio: views, watch time, impressions CTR, subscribers, traffic sources
  • GA4: youtube.com referral sessions ane key events
  • GSC: embed karela pages na clicks / impressions badlaya ke nahi

Common mistakes (aa bhool na karo)

  • Title ma keyword stuffing
  • Description ma website link j nahi
  • Video website par embed j na karvo
  • Regular upload na karva
  • Fakt Shorts banavva ane long videos bhulva

Practical task (amal ma karvanu)

  • Ek client (e.g. lav-kids.com, UK) mate Semrush Questions tab thi 3 YouTube video topics sodho

Step 49 / 127

DA PA Vadharvani & Backlink Health (Monthly SOP)

Source: "DA PA Vadharvani Guide (SOP)" — Client C, myhealinghands, valonudairyfarm na real data par thi. Ahiya general bhaag chhe je koi pan client mate kaam aave.

Mahatva ni note (CONFIRMED) DA ane PA Moz na metric chhe, Google eno use ranking mate nathi kartu (Moz ane Google banne e public ma kahyu chhe). Pan je kaam DA vadhare chhe (relevant ane quality backlinks), ae j kaam Google ranking ane traffic ma pan madad kare chhe. Etle focus "DA number" par nahi, "saara unique relevant domains" par rakhvo. Client ne report karta DA saathe GSC clicks, impressions ane sales pan batavva.

1. Saar

DA PA vadharvano asli rasto ek j chhe: vadhu alag-alag, relevant ane strong websites (linking root domains) thi backlinks melavva, ane malela links ne sachavva. Traffic, domain age ane internal links Moz na DA formula ma direct count nathi thata.

2. DA ane PA shu chhe

  • DA (Domain Authority): Moz no score (1 thi 100) je batave chhe ke aakhu domain ketlu strong chhe, Moz na backlink data mujab. Aa Google no score nathi.
  • PA (Page Authority): Ek specific page (URL) no score. Ae page ne ketla ane kevi quality na links male chhe te par aadhar rakhe chhe. Etle PA, DA karta vadhu pan hoi shake.
  • Linking root domain: Je unique website link aape chhe te. Ek site thi 50 links male to pan ae 1 j root domain ganay.
  • Log scale: DA no scale saman nathi. 0 thi 20 sudhi pahochvu saral chhe, 20 thi 30 mate ghana vadhu strong links joie, ane 40 pachhi dar point bahu mehnat thi aave.

3. Su matter kare ane su nathi kartu

5. Spam link pattern olkho

Jo ek j low-quality site (jem ke art-inpa.com) tamara ghana domains na top linking sites ma dekhay, to samjo ke tya ek j rite links banavaya chhe. Aava links DA vadharta nathi ane Spam Score vadhari shake chhe — aa type ni sites par navu link building band karo. Auto-generated links (jem ke Shopify stores ne track karti sites, price-comparison sites) tame banavya nathi — ene ignore kari shakay.

6. Step-by-step monthly SOP

Week 1: Audit

  • Moz Link Explorer (free account) par DA, PA, Spam Score ane linking root domains note karo.
  • GSC: Links report mathi external links export karo.
  • Ahrefs free backlink checker thi top linking domains check karo.
  • Competitor (same niche) na top backlinks jovo ane target list banavo.

Week 2 ane 3: Outreach ane link building

  • Dar domain mate mahine ochha ma ochha 5 thi 10 navi unique relevant sites target karo.
  • Guest post, listing, review, digital PR, journalist query platforms (active chhe ke pehla check karo): badha type mix karo — pan fakt link mate banavela guest posts ke paid reviews nahi (Google link spam policy).
  • Links fakt homepage par nahi — mukhya collections, top products ane saara blogs par pan lavo.
  • Dar link tracking sheet ma note karo.

Week 4: Verify, reclaim ane report

  • Badha links live chhe ke nahi te check karo. Lost thayela links tracking sheet ma "Lost" mark karo.
  • Lost link mate te site ne contact karine link pacho mukavvanu try karo (link reclamation).
  • Moz par DA/PA ane linking root domains fari note karo.
  • Manager/client mate short report: ketla navi domains, ketla lost, kai quality, DA change.
Note Moz ne navi link crawl karta ane DA update thata ghani vaar 3 thi 6 athvadiya ke vadhu lagi shake chhe. Etle result mahina-mahina compare karo, roj nahi.

7. Saari link kevi rite olakhvi (checklist)

  • Site same ke related niche ni chhe.
  • Site par real content ane real traffic chhe.
  • Site no Spam Score ochho chhe.
  • Site "link sale" ke "free ad post" mate j bani nathi.
  • Link content ni andar naturally aave chhe, footer ke sidebar ma nahi.
  • Site e pehla aapna domain ne link nathi aapyo (navo root domain chhe).

8. Anchor text ane target page mix

Anchor text mix (industry practice, Google no official rule nahi):

  • Brand name (jem ke Client C, healing hands): sauthi vadhu.
  • Naked URL (Client C.com): saaro bhaag.
  • Generic (click here, visit website, buy now): thodo.
  • Keyword anchor (a2 gir cow ghee, soft sandals for kids): thodo j. Vadhu exact keyword anchor unnatural lage chhe.

Target page mix: Homepage, mukhya collections, top products ane saara blogs. Fakt homepage par links lavva thi baki pages nu PA nahi vadhe.

10. DA PA ghati shake? Haa: kem ane su karvu

DA ane PA permanent score nathi. Etle sirf links banavva puratu nathi, malela links ne sachavva pan jaruri chhe.

DA kem ghate

  • Backlinks lost thai jay: link aapti site no page delete thai jay, site band thai jay, ke te link hatavi de. Profile ane directory links ma aa vadhu thay chhe.
  • Linking sites pote weak thai jay: tamne link aapti site no DA ghate, to tena link ni value pan ghate.
  • DA comparative (relative) score chhe: tamari site same rahe pan bija sites vadhu strong thai jay, to tamaro DA 1-2 point ghati shake.
  • Moz na index ke formula ma update: Moz update kare tyare ghani sites na DA ek saathe upar-niche thai jay (2019 na Moz DA 2.0 update ma moto farak aavyo hato).
  • Spam links ni value ochhi gane: free ad forum jeva links par aadhar hoy to aa risk chhe.

DA drop aave to su karvu

Drop ketlo?MatlabSu karvu
1-2 pointNormal upar-nicheKai khaas nahi. Regular link building chalu rakho.
3-4 pointDhyan aapva jevuMoz ma Lost Links ane Linking Domains no trend jovo. Kai mukhya sites na links gaya te shodho.
5+ pointMoto dropLost links ni list banavo ane link reclamation karo. Check karo ke Moz e koi update karyo chhe ke nahi (te vakhte ghani sites ma ek saathe drop dekhay).

DA stable kevi rite rakhvo

  • Links ghana alag-alag, strong ane relevant sites thi lavo. Aava links lambo samay tike chhe.
  • Free profile ane classified links par aadhar na rakho. Ae jaldi aave chhe ane jaldi jay pan chhe.
  • Dar mahine thoda-thoda navi links banavta raho, ek j mahine 100 links banavi ne band na karo.
  • Moz Link Explorer ma Lost Links ane Linking Domains dar mahine jovo, ane tracking sheet nu Status update karo.

11. Common mistakes

  • Free classified ane ad forum sites par saikdo links banavva.
  • Ek j site thi ghana links lai ne khush thavu, unique domains na badle.
  • Fakt homepage par links lavva.
  • Dar link ma exact keyword anchor vaparvo.
  • Paid link packages kharidva je "DA 50 in 30 days" jeva promise kare.
  • Links banavi ne bhuli javu, lost links check na karva.
