  1. Keyword Research

> Keyword research is the process of which keyword you mostly use and analysing the search volume of the keyword then after using this data to use for specific purposes like blogging and marketing.

> Keyword is a phrase or query that people type into the search engine to find and what they are looking for.

> Keyword Volume and Search Volume are the same.

>Keyword Volume : The number of times people search for a keyword in a month

>Ex. “Best flutter class’ Search volume is 10k so keyword volume is 10k, which means 10k user aa query google ma find kare chhe.

# Density ( Keyword Count * Total Word ) / 100

>How often a keyword appears in a text compared to the total number of words.

>You write a blog post with 100 words and use the keyword “digital marketing” 3 times. So the density is 3%.

# Frequency ( Frequency = exact count )

>This is just how many times a keyword is used in the content.

>In a 500-word article, you use “fitness tips” 7 times. So the frequency is 7.

# Keyword Difficulty

>How difficult it is to rank for this keyword.

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) - Comprehensive Guide (2024)

GA4 Overview

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is the latest version of Google’s analytics platform, offering enhanced tracking, better insights, and improved data accuracy compared to Universal Analytics (UA).

Key Areas in GA4

  1. Acquisition (Avyo Kyathi) - How users are arriving on your website/app.
  2. Engagement (Kam Su Karu) - How users interact with your content.
  3. Monetization (Kai Work Karu Ke Nai?) - Tracking revenue-generating activities.
  4. Retention (Return User Avo Ke Nai) - Measuring returning visitors.

Realtime Overview

  • Old Name: Universal Analytics (UA)
  • New Name: Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

Demographics

  • Age: Tracks user age distribution.
  • City: Identifies users’ locations based on city-level data.

Technology Tracking

  • Device: Tracks user activity across different device types (Mobile, Desktop, Tablet).

History of Google Analytics

  • 2005: Introduction of Urchin Analytics.
  • 2013: Launch of Universal Analytics (UA).
  • 2020: Web + App integration introduced.
  • 2024: Google Analytics 4 (GA4) becomes the default analytics tool.

Acquisition Reports

  • Acquisition Overview: A summary of how users find your website.
  • User Acquisition: Tracks new users and their traffic sources.
  • Traffic Acquisition: Monitors overall site traffic, including returning users.

Engagement Metrics

  • Engagement Session: A session is considered "engaged" if it lasts at least 10 seconds.
  • Engagement Rate: The percentage of engaged sessions out of total sessions.
  • Events & Conversions: User interactions tracked as events, such as clicks, form submissions, or purchases.
  • Cohorts: Groups users based on shared characteristics over a specific time period.

User Reports

  • User Overview: Provides insights into user behavior, demographics, and engagement levels.
  • User Details: Analyzes individual user data, including retention and activity patterns.

Event Parameters in GA4

  • Event (User Actions): Tracked as "work" within GA4.
  • Automatic & Custom Events: GA4 automatically tracks major events, while custom events can be defined based on business needs.
  • Common Events:
  • Page_View: Tracks views on a specific page.
  • Page_Location: Captures the URL of the visited page.

Data Structure in GA4

Dimensions (Qualitative Attributes)

  • Definition: Attributes of your data.
  • Examples:
  • City: Surat, Delhi, etc.
  • Device Type: Mobile, Desktop, Tablet.

Metrics (Quantitative Data)

  • Definition: Numeric data used to measure performance.
  • Examples:
  • User Count: 1,000 users.
  • Conversion Rate: 1%.

GA4 & Google Tag Manager (GTM)

  • GA4: A reporting tool for tracking user behavior and interactions.
  • GTM (Google Tag Manager): A tag management tool used to fire tracking codes and send data to GA4.
  • Process Flow:
  1. Capture Data: GA4 collects event-based data.
  2. Processing: Data is processed into meaningful insights.
  3. Reporting: Generates detailed analytics reports.
  4. Data Analysis: Provides deep insights for business decisions.

Conclusion

GA4 is a powerful analytics tool that provides a deeper understanding of user behavior. With advanced features like event-based tracking, AI-powered insights, and cross-platform analysis, it enables businesses to make data-driven decisions effectively.

3. Learning — AEO samajvanu

AEO etle: user na question no clear, direct answer aapvo, jethi Google na answer-box / featured snippet ma dekhaay. Featured snippet ane zero-click aa j family chhe.

Analogy — busy teacher: Google ne busy teacher samjo. "Aaje homework kone anyu?" — 10 min ni story kaho to teacher confuse. "Sir, homework aa file ma chhe" — instant clear. AEO aa j: answer first, story later.

Search intent — AEO nu foundation: same keyword, alag intent. Answer aapta pehla user shu karva mange chhe e pakdo:

  • Informational → "What is piles?" → definition
  • How-to → "How to treat at home?" → numbered steps
  • Comparison → "Medicine vs surgery" → table
  • Commercial → "Best piles medicine" → options + proof
  • Transactional → "Buy X" → product + CTA
  • Local → "Best doctor in Surat" → local + reviews

Golden rule: keyword nahi, intent vadhu important.

Featured snippet: Google no highlighted answer box. Guarantee nathi, pan clean structure thi chance vadhe. Analogy: answer "plate saaf" kari ne aapo, spoon-feed nahi — machine mate extract easy bane.

Zero-click: user ne SERP par j answer mali jaay, click na kare. Aa failure NATHI — visibility, brand ne authority pan value chhe.

AEO formula: ASK → ANSWER → FORMAT → PROVE → MEASURE

4. Learning — GEO samajvanu

GEO etle: AI systems (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI search) tamaru brand + content samje, trust kare ane answer ma cite/mention kare.

Analogy — smart waiter: customer puche "aa area ma best healthy meal kya?" — tamari info (menu, location, reviews, mentions) badhe clear ne consistent hoy to waiter confidently recommend kare; adhuri/contradictory hoy to "khabar nathi bhai." AI ne pan evi j clear story joiye.

AI ne 4 vaat joiye:

  1. ENTITY — tame kon chho (brand, service, location).
  2. ANSWER — user na questions solve thaay.
  3. ORIGINAL VALUE — internet par same na hoy evi useful info (data, case study, cost breakdown).
  4. TRUST — author, proof, reviews, honest claims + third-party mentions.

Analogy 2: AI ne "guess the movie" na ramadvo — character, location, story badhu clearly aapo.

GEO formula: ENTITY → ANSWER → ORIGINAL → TRUST → SOURCES → TEST

  • Authoritativeness — field ma brand/author ni reputation (biju kon tamne reference kare?).

  • Update — facts badle tyare content update karo (updated date batavo).

  • Fake expertise ke fake credentials.
  • Fake statistics / fake reviews / fake citations.
  • Unsupported medical / legal / financial claims.

Google Discover Guide (Gujlish)

A complete step-by-step master guide to understanding, optimizing, and tracking content performance on Google Discover using conversational Gujlish and English.

Key Takeaways

  • Zero-Search Traffic: Discover delivers content based on user interests automatically without search queries.
  • Image First: Large, high-quality images (minimum 1200px width) significantly improve presentation and click-throughs.
  • No Clickbait: High-quality, honest titles perform better long-term and maintain policy compliance.
  • Fluctuations are Normal: Traffic surges and drops are expected due to changing user interests and core algorithm updates.

1. Google Discover Shu Chhe? (What is Discover?)

Google Discover acts as a personalized feed tailored directly to every user's interests, similar to social media feeds on Instagram or Facebook.

  • Personalized Feed: Jem Instagram ke Facebook par tamaru Personal Feed / Scroll hoy chhe, evuj aa Google nu Feed chhe.
  • Query-less Discovery: Ahiya user e kashu Search nathi karvu padtu. Google pote user na interest ane search history pramane articles, videos ane blogs samethi batavse.
  • Surge Potential: Etle j amathi aavto traffic achanak (surprise traffic) aavi shake chhe.

2. Website Discover ma Keviri Rite Aave? (Eligibility Requirements)

Getting into Discover requires zero special code or plugins—it operates automatically once technical and content standards are met.

  • Automatic Process: Aa ekdam automatic chhe, koi special setting ke code ni jarur nathi.
  • Two Fundamental Rules (Be basic sharato):
  1. Indexing Required: Website Google ma Index thayeli hovi joie (Google ne khabar hovi joie ke site exist kare chhe).
  2. Policy Compliance: Content Google ni Content Policies (spam na hovu joie, dangerous content na hovu joie) nu palan kartu hovu joie.
  • Important Note: Dhyan rakho: Eligible hovano matlav guarantee nathi ke tame Discover ma dekhasho j.

3. Discover ma Aavvana Chances Keviri Rite Vadharva? (Optimization Action Steps)

Follow these technical and editorial best practices to optimize your content visibility:

1. Clear & Catchy Titles Vapro

  • Title evu lakho je article nu sachu varnan kartu hoy.
  • Clickbait thi bacho (khoota shocking shabdo vapari loko ne lalachavva nahi).

2. High-Quality & Large Images Muko

  • Discover moti ane attractive images ne vadhare pasand kare chhe.
  • Main image ni width ochha ma ochhi 1200 pixels hovi joie.
  • Website no logo kyarey main image tarike na vapro.

3. Honest Previews Aapo

  • Matra click melavva mate bhamrak title, summary ke photo na vapro.

4. People-First Content Banavo

  • Trending Topics: Je topics atyare trending ma chhe tena par lakho.
  • Good Storytelling: Vanchak ne maja aave tevi rite saral vat karo.
  • Unique Insights: Bije kyay na male tevi potani navi mahiti uméro.
  • Avoid Outrage: Gussa ke outrage felave tevu content na banavo.

4. "Follow" Feature Setup (Extra Advantage)

Chrome browser allows users to directly follow websites to receive newly published updates directly into their Discover feed.

Implementation Checklist

2. Technical SEO & Site Architecture

Q: Technical SEO ma su su check karo cho?

Answer: "Screaming Frog / SEMrush thi complete crawl karu, ane issues ne Errors → Warnings → Notices priority ma fix karu."

    1. Crawlability:
  • robots.txt directive blocks evaluation
  • XML sitemap validation & indexability check
  • Server response status codes handling (200, 301, 404, 5xx)
    1. Indexing Directives:
  • Meta robots tag inspection (noindex, nofollow)
  • Incorrect canonical tag mapping & self-referential validation
  • Identification and resolution of orphan pages
    1. Core Web Vitals & Performance Metrics:
  • LCP (Largest Contentful Paint): < 2.5s
  • INP (Interaction to Next Paint): < 200ms
  • CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift): < 0.1
    1. Redirect Management:
  • Elimination of 301/302 redirect chains
  • Zero tolerance for active redirect loops
    1. Mobile-First Optimization:
  • Mobile viewport configuration, tap targets spacing, and responsive layout integrity
    1. Security Protocols:
  • HTTPS/SSL certificate enforcement & mixed content error resolution
    1. Structured Data:
  • Schema.org syntax validation & rich snippet eligibility checks
    1. Duplicate Content & Architecture:
  • Faceted navigation canonicalization & duplicate content mitigation
    1. Social Metadata:
  • Open Graph and Twitter Card tags (og:title, og:image, og:description)

Q: Crawl Budget etle su ane XML Sitemap vs Robots.txt no farak?

  • Crawl Budget:
  • Googlebot site par limited pages crawl kare chhe. Jo low-value, duplicate ke 404 pages vadhi jaay to important money pages crawl na thay. Unwanted URL parameters ane low-quality pages noindex or disallow karva pade.
  • XML Sitemap vs Robots.txt:
  • XML Sitemap: Google ne kehavu "Aa pages exist kare chhe, aane crawl/index karo."
  • Robots.txt: Google ne kehavu "Aa specific folder ke URL path par crawl na karo (Disallow)."

5. Advanced Practical & Interview Scenarios

Q: Jo 20,000 pages koi site ma hoy ane ee index na thay to tamaro step-by-step approach su rahe?

    1. Google Search Console Deep-Dive Analysis:
  • 'Discovered
  • currently not indexed': Indicates crawl budget constraints, server response latency, or weak internal link hierarchy.
  • 'Crawled
  • currently not indexed': Points to perceived low content quality, thin content, or internal duplication.
  • Excluded by noindex / Canonical mismatch: Technical directive misconfigurations.
    1. Technical Crawlability Checks:
  • Audit robots.txt for unintended disallow rules. Analyze Time to First Byte (TTFB) and resolve intermittent 5xx server failures.
    1. XML Sitemap Segmentation:
  • Segment 20,000 pages into smaller sitemaps of 1,000–2,000 URLs grouped by category or directory to isolate indexation drop-offs.
    1. Internal Link Architecture:
  • Pass link equity from high-authority pages using contextual internal links, optimized breadcrumb paths, and static HTML sitemaps to resolve orphan pages.

Q: Blue Bottle sell karvi hoy ane Google Top 10 ma rank karavvi hoy to strategy su rese?

    1. Buyer-Intent Long-Tail Targeting:
  • Target precise long-tail queries like "insulated matte blue stainless steel gym bottle 1 litre" alongside broader terms.

    1. Topical Authority Content Clusters:

    1. Google Merchant Center Integration:
  • Configure optimized Google Merchant Center product feeds to capture organic shopping tab placements.
    1. User-Generated Content (UGC) & Off-Page Social Proof:

Q: Koi domain 1 month ma off thavanu hoy ane navi site par transfer karvanu hoy, to DA/PA ane traffic maintain karva su karso?

    1. Direct 1-to-1 URL Mapping Matrix:
  • Map every single historical, traffic-generating legacy URL to its exact structural equivalent on the new domain (avoid blanket homepage redirects).
    1. Staging & Environment Verification:
  • Complete full site architecture setup, assets, and self-referential canonical tags on the new domain before site migration.
    1. Page-Level 301 Permanent Redirects:
  • Apply server-level (.htaccess or Cloudflare edge level) 1-to-1 301 redirects to transfer 90–95% of accrued link juice.
    1. Google Search Console Change of Address:
  • Execute the official Change of Address tool within GSC and submit the updated XML sitemap immediately post-launch.
    1. Infrastructure Continuity:
  • Maintain active registration and hosting for the old domain for a minimum of 6–12 months to allow search engines to consolidate redirect signals.

Q: DA (Domain Authority) ane PA (Page Authority) vadharva mate su strategy use karo cho?

  • Expert Note: DA and PA are third-party metrics developed by Moz. Search engines operate on internal algorithms like Google PageRank and Topical Authority models.
  • Execution Framework:
    1. High-Authority Relevance: Earn contextually relevant backlinks via targeted Digital PR campaigns and competitor link gap analysis.
    1. Spam Cleanups: Perform continuous backlink profile audits to mitigate incoming link toxic risks.
    1. Strategic PageRank Passing: Use contextual internal linking to funnel equity from high-authority informational blog posts to high-value money pages.
    1. Broken Link Reclamation: Identify inbound traffic pointing to dead 404 pages and recover value via strategic 301 redirects.
    1. Link Magnet Asset Creation: Produce data-driven reports, comprehensive industry statistics, and infographics that naturally earn editorially placed backlinks.
