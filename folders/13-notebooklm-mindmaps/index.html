  1. NAVI Website - New Site Flow (pehli vaar site thi sharuat)

SEO Architecture - NAVI Website (New Site Flow) v2

* Golden Rules (badha SOP par lagu)  [SOP 00 Start Here]

* Data pehla, opinion pachhi: decision GSC/GA4 thi; Semrush/Moz = estimate

* Intent > volume: search intent + business value pehla, volume pachhi

* 1 keyword-intent = 1 URL: navu page pehla Master Keyword Map ma check

* White-hat only: paid links, PBN, fake reviews, copied content kyarey nahi

* Rules of thumb = guideline, law nahi (50-60 char title, 3-5 links, KD 0-30)

* Speed = Core Web Vitals (real user data); PSI 90+ goal chhe, ranking rule nahi

* Claim label: CONFIRMED (Google official) / LIKELY (industry) / SPECULATION

* Kyarey number banavvo nahi: dar report ma period + source

* Security: passwords/tokens fakt password manager ma - sheet/doc/chat ma nahi

* Measure everything: dar change ni date log ma; result 4-6 week ma juvo

* Phase 0: Business Goals & Domain  [SOP 01 Client Ask + 02>06]

* Client info & access  [01>00 Onboarding Master Doc]

* Basic info: business name, URL, niche, type (ecom/service/local/blog), CMS, target area, languages

* Business understanding: top 3 money products, USP, ideal customer, avg order/lead value, seasonality

* SEO goals number ma: '6 mahina ma organic leads double' - vague goal = project fail risk

* Access badha ek saathe Day 1: GSC, GA4, GTM, CMS admin, hosting, GBP, Merchant Center, social, Semrush/Ahrefs

* Access = client na account mathi 'Add user' karavi ne - password share karavvano nahi

* Previous SEO history: old agency work, penalty, traffic drops, redesign/migration, spammy backlinks

* Time: kaam 2-4 kalak, access aavta 2-4 divas - etle pehla divas e mango

* Business goal samajvu  [01>01]

* Main goal ek vakya ma: Sales (ecom) / Leads (service) / Traffic / Branding

* B2B ke B2C; revenue sauthi vadhu kaya product/service thi aave

* 6-12 mahina nu number target fix (da. 100 leads/month, 2x sales)

* Success kevi rite mapse: calls, form fills, orders, store visits

* Client bole 'badhu joiye' to puchho: '3 mahina ma fakt ek vastu improve thay to kayi?'

* Target customer & location  [01>02]

* Persona: age group, gender, job, income level

* Customer Google ma je exact words lakhe te note karo

* Temni problem su chhe je solution mate search kare

* Local: city + area keywords + Google Business Profile jaruri

* India: India-first keywords, INR, Hindi/Gujarati/English mix

* International: English content, tya ni spelling, tya na competitors

* USP shodho  [01>03]

* Top 5 competitors na homepage na main claims note karo

* Su offer kare je bija nahi: free delivery, same-day, warranty, experience, handmade

* Reviews ma customers su vakhan kare - pattern shodho

* 1 main USP + 2 supporting USP final karo

* USP vaparvo: homepage H1, title, meta description, product first paragraph, schema

* Customer problem & solution  [01>04]

* Client + competitor na reviews vanchho - loko su complain kare

* Google 'People Also Ask' + Quora/Reddit/YouTube comments na real questions

* Top 10 customer problems ni list banavo

* Har problem = blog/FAQ topic; solution = service/product page content

* Domain name  [02>06]

* Brand + keyword mix best; exact-match domain par over-optimize nahi

* India target = .in, global = .com

* 2-3 words max, spelling easy, phone par bolta samjay, no hyphens/numbers

* Check: availability (Namecheap), same social handles, Google search, trademark (ipindia.gov.in)

* Phase 1: Research (Keyword, Competitor, Country)  [SOP 02]

* Keyword research - 7 steps (dar nava page mate, 30-45 min)  [02>07]

* 1. Page nu kaam ek line ma lakho - te j intent nakki kare

* 2. 5-10 seed keywords pote lakho (customer jem bole tem)

* 3. Google expand: autocomplete + People Also Ask + Related searches (free ane sauthi sacho source)

* 4. Semrush Keyword Magic Tool (target country database): 30-50 keyword list, volume + KD

* 5. Top 3 competitor URLs → Semrush Organic Research → je keywords tamara ma nathi te umero

* 6. SERP check (skip na karvo!): top 10 ma page type match? nana domains dekhay to jitay

* 7. Final: 1 primary + 3-5 secondary keywords

* Final karta pehla 5 checks  [02>07]

* Volume: navi site 50-500/month pan chale; established 500+

* KD: navi site 0-30; authority vadhe to 40 sudhi

* Intent match: top 10 ma je page type chhe e tamara page jevu?

* SERP ma jagya: forums/nana blogs dekhay = chance; fakt Amazon/Wikipedia = chhodo

* Business value: keyword inquiry/sale sudhi lai jay evo?

* 0-volume keyword pan chale local/B2B ma jo logical + competitor e vaparyu hoy

* Master Keyword Map (ek sheet per client)  [02>07 section 8]

* Columns: URL, page type, primary KW, secondary KWs, FAQ KWs, intent, volume, KD, GSC position, priority, status, owner/date

* Rule: navu keyword nakki karta pehla Ctrl+F thi sheet ma check - pehla thi vaparelu nahi ne

* Already hoy to navu page na banavi, junu page update karo (cannibalization avoid)

* Quick check: Google ma site:domain.com 'keyword' search

* Keyword na types  [02>07]

* Lambai: head (1-2 shabd, homepage), body (2-3, category), long-tail (3+, blog/navi site mate best)

* Intent: informational = blog; commercial = comparison/review; transactional = product/service page; navigational = brand page

* Intent match na thay to page game etlu saro hoy, rank nahi thay

* Competitor pool analysis  [02>02]

* Top 3-5 competitors: main keyword search ma first page par consistently je dekhay

* Temna top pages, keywords (je tamara pase nathi = gap list), content style, backlink sources note karo

* Temni weakness (slow site, old content, no FAQ) = tamara opportunity

* Output: 3 strengths + 3 weaknesses + keyword gap list

* Target country selection  [02>03]

* Customer kya chhe te pehla; competition + budget + shipping possible check karo

* Semrush ma database = target country j select karo (Global volume par decision nahi)

* International: hreflang, country subfolder (/us/), currency, tya na competitors benchmark

* Phase 2: Strategy & 6-Month Plan  [SOP 03]

* Strategy formulation  [01>00 Phase 4]

* Money pages nakki karo (business value sauthi vadhu vala pages)

* Priority = Impact x Effort: high impact + low effort pehla

* Technical fixes priority order + content plan (pillar + clusters)

  • internal linking plan + off-page plan

* Client ne kaho: 'Pehlo mahino = audit + strategy. Kaam Month 2 thi, result Month 3-6 ma visible'

* 6-month execution plan  [02>05]

* Month 1: full audit (on-page, technical, content) + quick wins + GSC/GA4 verify

* Month 2: on-page fixes (title, meta, headings, internal links) + must-have pages

* Month 3: content engine chalu - week ma 2-4 blogs (keyword gap cover)

* Month 4: off-page chalu - backlinks, Reddit/Quora, social signals

* Month 5: old content refresh + schema + AEO/GEO optimization

* Month 6: review - su work karyu te double down, strategy adjust

* Expectation: M1-2 flat, M3-4 movement, M5-6 visible growth - pehla divas e client ne samjavo

* Content calendar  [03>05]

* Week 1-2: money pages pehla; Week 3-4: supporting blogs je money pages ne link kare

* Week ma 2-4 pieces, same days fixed (da. Mon + Thu)

* Seasonal content (festival, sale) 1 mahina pehla publish

* Topic sources: keyword gap sheet, PAA, customer questions, competitor new posts

* Monthly SEO plan method  [03>08]

* GSC last 3 months (Queries + Pages) → opportunity list → priority pages select

* Position 5-20 + good impressions = striking distance priority (intent + business value strong hoy to)

* Keyword → correct page map (buy = collection; how-to = blog; service = service page)

* Blog formula: priority money page → related info query → helpful blog → internal link → commercial page

* Month-end: position, clicks, impressions compare (same length period, 28 vs 28 days; YoY pan)

* Phase 3: Design & Web Development  [SOP 14 + 04]

* Design philosophy: AI-made na lage  [14>01]

* AI tells avoid: purple/blue gradient hero, glowing blobs, badha section same 3-card layout, emoji icons

* Stock smile/handshake/fake team photos avoid - real photos (mobile camera + daylight pan chale)

* Banned words: 'Unlock the power', 'Elevate', 'Seamless', 'Revolutionary', 'In today's fast-paced world'

* 7 human rules: 3 personality words, real content pehla, imperfection ok, ek style system, whitespace, specific text ('925 silver, hallmark sathe'), competitor thi alag ek thing

* 5-minute AI check: navi person ne 5 sec batavo - 'AI ye banavyu lage?' + 'aa shu vechhe?'; 2+ tells = fix

* Fonts, colors & spacing (ek var fix, badhe same)  [14>02]

* Colors 60/30/10: 60% neutral background, 30% brand main, 10% accent (CTA); max 5 colors, gradient avoid

* Text contrast 4.5:1 minimum (WCAG AA); light gray on white = fail

* 2 fonts only (heading + body), 3 weights; free Google Fonts; Gujarati/Hindi mate Noto family

* Type scale: H1 44-56px (ek page ma ek j), body 17-18px (min 16), line height 1.55+

* Spacing: 8px base unit; section gap 96px desktop / 56px mobile; container max 1200px; corner radius ek j (6px)

* Niche mood: jewellery = cream+deep green/gold; B2B = off-white+navy; kids = bright pan 2 colours j

* Header & navigation  [14>03]

* 3 rows: top strip (offer/contact) + main header (logo-search-cart) + nav bar (5-7 categories max)

* Menu order: Shop/Categories pehla → New/Best Sellers → Collections → Sale → Blog → About → Contact

* Category + sub-category: ek item niche max 6-8 sub-items (mega menu 3-4 columns + featured photo)

* Top category pote clickable category page hovi joiye (SEO) - fakt dropdown label nahi

* Menu links crawlable HTML <a href>; JS-only menu nahi; dropdown max 2 level

* Fixed items: logo (Home link), search, cart badge, active page highlight, mobile ma hamburger drawer (accordion, 44px tap targets)

* Sticky header compact; logo SVG/WebP preload; header height CLS na kare

* Homepage section by section  [14>04]

* Hero (jya homepage khule): real photo + H1 ek specific vaakya ('Handmade silver jewellery, Surat thi') + 1 primary CTA; slider/carousel avoid

* Trust line hero ni niche: free shipping, easy returns, secure payment (real policy j)

* Ecommerce order: hero → shop by category tiles → best sellers grid → brand story → new arrivals → offer banner → reviews (real name+city) → gift guide → blog → newsletter → footer

* Service site order: hero → services → process 4 step → real results → founder note → testimonials → FAQ → final CTA

* Darek section nu layout ALAG (same 3-card row repetition = AI tell)

* Rules: max 1 primary CTA per screen; icons ochha photos vadhu; animations subtle (fade 200-300ms); hero preload baki lazy-load

* Footer  [14>05]

* 4 columns: Brand (logo+real address), Shop (top categories), Help (shipping/returns/track/FAQ/contact), Company (about/blog/reviews)

* Bottom bar: copyright + Privacy, Terms, Refund policy links + real payment icons

* Legal pages compulsory: Privacy, Terms, Refund/Return, Shipping

* Real contact: ek email + WhatsApp; fake trust badges nahi; mobile ma accordion

* Imagery, icons & copywriting  [14>06]

* Real photos first: product, packaging, workshop, team; stock ochha ane ek j style ma

* AI photo joiye to fakt potana ChatGPT thi, product real ne match kare, misleading nahi

* Sizes: hero 1920x1080 WebP, product same ratio (1:1 / 4:5), compress <200KB, alt text real

* Ek icon set (Lucide/Phosphor), ek thickness; emoji UI ma nahi

* Copy: specific facts (material, size, delivery days); button text specific ('Add to cart', 'Get free audit')

* Mobile friendly design  [04>02]

* India ma 75%+ traffic mobile; Google mobile-first indexing - mobile version j real site

* Touch targets min 44px; no horizontal scroll; zoom vagar vanchay evu font

* 4G par 3 second ma load; images WebP compressed

* Real device test jaruri: 1 Android + 1 iPhone (menu, forms, buttons)

* Mobile par full-screen popup = penalty risk

* Theme, motion & consistency  [04>05]

* Ek motion language badha pages par; 2-3 animation types max; 300-500ms speed

* Consistency: 1 primary + 1 secondary + 1 accent color; max 2 fonts; same button style; header/footer identical

* Animation pachhi pan Core Web Vitals pass te check; prefers-reduced-motion support

* Competitor design study + new features  [04>03, 04>04]

* Top 5 competitor sites (mobile+desktop): layout, navigation, checkout flow, features, trust signals, UX mistakes note karo

* Feature table: must have vs nice to have vs differentiator; 1 week ma thay eva 3 quick improvements

* Interactive tools (calculator, quiz, configurator) = engagement + backlinks; tool ne dedicated indexable page + schema

* Checklist & reusable template  [14>07]

* Doc copy kari ne dar website mate bharo: brand brief, header/menu plan, homepage plan, footer plan

* Navi site order: brand brief → competitor study → wireframe → real photos/text → build with style tokens → mobile test → QA → launch pachhi 2 week Clarity thi fix

* Final QA: AI tells <=2, 2 fonts/5 colours/ek radius, hero real photo + 1 CTA, reviews real, legal pages, contrast, LCP <2.5s, 5-sec test 3 loko par

* Phase 4: Setup & Tracking  [SOP 07 + 11>01A]

* Google Search Console  [07>01]

* Domain property add + DNS verify (best); sitemap.xml submit → Success status check

* Email alerts on (coverage issues, manual actions)

* Weekly 10 min: Performance trend, Pages report (indexed vs not), new queries

* Monthly: position 4-10 ane 10-20 pages list (quick wins mate), CWV status

* 2026 features: Search Generative AI report (AI Overviews clicks alag), multimodal filter, platform properties

* GA4 & GTM  [07>02]

* GA4 property + data stream; Measurement ID install (GTM thi)

* Enhanced measurement ON: scrolls, outbound clicks, site search, video

* Key events mark karo: form submit, purchase, call click, WhatsApp click (3-5 events)

* Internal traffic filter (potana visits exclude)

* GTM: container, GA4 tag, custom events; preview ma test pachhi publish

* Looker Studio dashboard  [07>03]

* GA4 + GSC banne connect; pages: Overview | SEO Performance | Traffic | Conversions

* Widgets: scorecards, organic clicks trend, top pages + queries tables, device breakdown

* Client ne view-only link (weekly auto-email option); weekly 5-min trend review

* Microsoft Clarity  [07>04]

* Weekly 15 min: heatmaps (CTA par clicks?), scroll maps, 5-10 recordings, rage/dead clicks

* Action: non-clickable par click kare → clickable banao; 50% scroll drop → content upar lao

* Bing & search engines  [07>05]

* Bing Webmaster: GSC import option (1 click ma site + sitemap)

* IndexNow: navu/updated URL turant Bing/Yandex ne notify (Google use nathi kartu)

* Nava pages: GSC URL Inspection → Request Indexing (priority pages)

* Tracking sheet copy (Report 1)  [11>01 + 01A template]

* Template → File → Make a copy → '[Client] - SEO Tracking Sheet'

* Dashboard tab: domain + pehlo mahino; Monthly Goal baseline + target % set

* Blue = tame bharo, Black = formula (haath na lagadvo); data na hoy to khali, 0 nahi

* Phase 5: Technical SEO Foundation  [SOP 08]

* Indexing check  [08>01]

* GSC → Indexing → Pages: indexed vs not-indexed count + trend

* Not-indexed reasons vanchho - badha problem nathi ('Alternate page with canonical' normal)

* Top 20 money pages URL Inspection thi indexed confirm

* 'Crawled - not indexed' = quality/demand: content improve + internal links; 'Discovered - not indexed' = internal links + sitemap check

* Canonical & duplicate content  [08>02]

* Ek j preferred version (https + www/non-www), baki site-wide 301

* Dar indexable page par self-canonical; duplicates main URL taraf canonical

* Noindex: thank-you, internal search, login pages (robots.txt block sathe noindex nahi - vanchi j na shake)

* Sitemap + internal links + canonical - teno URL match hovo joiye

* Redirects, 404 & broken links  [08>03]

* 301 = permanent (value pass thay); 302 = temporary; 410 = kayam mate gayu

* Redirect chains max 1 hop (A→C direct); loops tarat fix

* 404 page par backlinks/traffic hoy → nearest relevant page par 301; value nathi → 404/410 rehva do

* Monthly Screaming Frog crawl: 0 internal broken links target

* Schema markup  [08>04]

* Badhi sites: Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList (JSON-LD)

* Page type pramane: Product+Offer (ecom), Article (blog), LocalBusiness (local)

* FAQ rich results 7 May 2026 thi band - FAQ section users/AEO mate rakho, schema optional

* Validate: Rich Results Test + validator.schema.org; GSC Enhancements ma errors 0

* Page par na dekhaye tevi info schema ma nahi (fake rating = manual action risk)

* Image SEO  [08>05]

* File name descriptive (eggless-chocolate-cake.webp, IMG_4521.jpg nahi)

* Alt text 5-15 words, natural (keyword stuffing nahi); decorative = empty alt

* WebP/AVIF compressed (100-200KB); display size jetli j image; width/height attributes (CLS avoid)

* Lazy load fakt fold niche ni images; main/LCP image lazy NAHI

* Full technical audit  [08>06]

* Screaming Frog crawl (free 500 URLs): response codes, indexability, titles/meta, H1, images, crawl depth

* Money pages 3 clicks thi deep na hova joiye

* GSC cross-check: Pages report, CWV, manual actions, security issues

* Priority = ketla URLs affected x business value; Critical (indexing/noindex bhool) 1 week ma fix

* Output sheet: Issue | URLs | Count | Impact | Effort | Priority | Owner | Status

* Redesign & migration SEO  [08>07]

* Launch pehla: old site full crawl + top pages + backlink pages export; redirect map old→new 1:1 (badhu homepage par nahi)

* Titles, meta, content, internal links navi site ma carry - content loss nahi

* Launch divas: Disallow:/ nathi te check, noindex remove, redirect map test, sitemap submit, GA4 live

* Pachhi 6-8 week: weekly GSC check; 20%+ drop to tarat redirects check - raah na jovo; redirects min 1 varas rakho

* Phase 6: On-Page SEO Page by Page  [SOP 05]

* EEAT badha pages par  [05>01]

* Experience: real photos, personal examples, 'ame aa try karyu' proof

* Expertise: author bio with credentials har blog par; facts mate sources cite

* Authority: backlinks, mentions, reviews, certifications

* Trust: HTTPS, clear contact, privacy/refund policy, real address/phone, secure payment

* Test: visitor puche 'aa site par bharoso kemu?' - je answer page par dekhay te j EEAT

* Title, meta & H1-H6  [05>09]

* Title: primary keyword pehla, 50-60 chars, unique per page; formula: Keyword - Faydo | Brand

* Meta description: 150-160 chars, keyword + faydo + CTA; direct ranking factor nahi pan CTR vadhare

* Ek j H1 per page (primary keyword sathe); H2 = sections, H3 = sub-sections; levels skip nahi

* Slug = keyword  [05>08]

* Short 3-5 words, lowercase, hyphens, primary keyword; stop words kadho

* Evergreen rakho (dates nahi); URL change karo to 301 redirect must

* Keyword mapping per page  [05>10]

* 1 primary (title, H1, slug, first para) + 3-5 secondary (H2, body) + 3-5 FAQ keywords

* 1 keyword = 1 page; Master Keyword Map sheet update

* Keyword density % target nahi; stuffing avoid - synonyms naturally

* Internal linking  [05>03]

* Har page par 3-5 contextual links (content ma naturally; footer links count nahi)

* Descriptive keyword-rich anchor text, variety sathe ('click here' nahi)

* Authority flow: Blog → Category → Product/Service (money pages taraf)

* Money pages ne sauthi vadhu internal links; monthly broken link check

* No orphan pages  [05>06]

* Darek new page → 24 hours ma 2-3 internal links (related blog/category mathi)

* Sitemap ma hovu enough nahi - internal link j authority aape

* Shodhva: Screaming Frog sitemap crawl → Orphan URLs; quarterly audit

* Page speed & Core Web Vitals  [05>04]

* Target (real user data): LCP <2.5s, INP <200ms, CLS <0.1; GSC CWV report + PSI field data

* PSI 90+ saro goal pan ranking rule nahi; mobile score pehla juvo

* Fixes: WebP images + lazy load, caching, unused JS/plugins remove, defer JS, hosting (TTFB <600ms), Cloudflare CDN

* Must-have pages  [05>05]

* 7 essential: About (real story+photos), Contact (form+phone+WhatsApp+map), Privacy Policy, Terms, Refund/Return, Shipping, FAQ

* Badha footer ma linked; real content (Lorem ipsum/thin page worst); NAP consistent badhe

* AEO & GEO content  [05>07 + 05>12 Playbook]

* AEO: question-format H2/H3, pehla 40-60 words ma direct answer pachhi detail, FAQ section, bolchal ni language, lists/tables

* GEO: data consistency site bhar, Reddit/Quora/review sites par presence, schema, original value (data, examples)

* Order: basic SEO pehla → EEAT → authority → schema; AI visibility aena thi j aave

* Measure: GSC 'Search Generative AI' report; GA4 'AI Assistants' channel

* Content quality & depth  [05>11]

* Search intent fully satisfy; user ne pachho Google par javab shodhvu na pade

* Original value: unique data, own photos, real examples, personal experience - je copy na thay

* Thin signals avoid: 300-word page, duplicate content, value-add vagar AI text, keyword stuffing

* Test: 'aa page na hot to konu nuksan?' - 'na' hoy to improve/merge/remove

* Phase 7: Ecommerce SEO (jo shop hoy)  [SOP 09]

* Category & collection pages  [09>01]

* Category = ecommerce nu main money page (buying keywords category par rank kare)

* 1 transactional keyword = 1 collection; title/H1 clear ('Kids Makeup Kits', 'Shop All' nahi)

* Grid upar 1-3 line intro; buying guide/FAQ grid niche; best sellers pehla, out-of-stock last

* Sub-collections + related blogs ne internal links; breadcrumbs with BreadcrumbList schema

* Product pages  [09>02]

* Title: product name + main attribute, brand end ma; unique description (manufacturer copy nahi)

* Multiple real images + alt; price, stock, delivery, return fold ni najik; real reviews visible

* Product schema: name, image, price, currency, availability (site ane schema same)

* Google Merchant Center connect → free Shopping listings; feed errors 0

* Filters, variants & duplicate URLs  [09>03]

* Filters/sort hajaro URLs banave = index bloat; GSC Pages ma count track karo

* Search demand valo filter → dedicated optimized collection page; baki → canonical main collection par

* Shopify: product links /products/ format ma (collections/.../products/ mixed signal aape)

* Variants (?variant=) canonical main product par; alag page fakt jo variant nu potanu demand hoy

* Out-of-stock & discontinued  [09>04]

* Temporary: page LIVE rakho (delete karvu = ranking+backlinks+reviews jay) + notify-me + similar products

* Availability schema/feed banne ma update (same hova joiye)

* Discontinued: replacement hoy → 301; replacement nahi pan value chhe → 301 relevant collection; value nahi → 404/410

* Seasonal products na URLs varas-varas same rakho (date vagar slug)

* Phase 8: Off-Page SEO  [SOP 06]

* Backlink submission  [06>03]

* Quality > quantity: 5-10 quality links/month > 100 spam

* Good link criteria: relevant niche, high authority, real traffic vali site, natural anchor, do-follow preferred (no-follow pan natural)

* Free methods: guest posting, digital PR/journalist queries, broken link building, quality directories (10-15 max), competitor backlink study

* Avoid: paid schemes, PBN, link farms, same anchor badhe; disavow fakt manual action/spam history hoy to j

* Reddit & Quora  [06>01]

* 90/10 rule: 90% genuine help, self-promotion max 10%; karma pehla build karo

* Quora: 300+ word detailed answers; link 10 answers ma 2-3 ma j

* GEO bonus: AI answers ma Reddit/Quora sauthi vadhare cite thay - genuine presence = AI visibility

* 30 min/day purtu; target subreddits ni list; subreddit rules vanchho

* Social media  [06>02]

* 2-3 platforms select (badha ma thin presence nahi): B2C = Instagram/FB, B2B = LinkedIn, tutorials = YouTube, ecommerce = Pinterest

* Profiles complete: website link, bio keywords, NAP consistent

* Repurposing: 1 blog → 5 social posts → 1 video script

* Consistency > perfection: 3 posts/week regular > 15 ek week ma pachhi silence

* Local SEO & listings  [06>04]

* GBP 100% complete: categories, services, photos, hours; weekly posts

* Reviews: 30+ real reviews target, har review nu reply; fake/incentivised nahi

* NAP (Name, Address, Phone) exact same badhe - ek digit different = ranking drop

* Citations: Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps

* Note: GBP Q&A Dec 2025 thi band (AI Ask aavyu) - description/services complete rakho

* Global business submission  [06>05]

* Step 0: Master Business Sheet - name, address, phone, URL, descriptions, logo, photos ek j format ma (NAP same)

* Priority platforms: GBP, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, LinkedIn, Facebook, Crunchbase, Trustpilot

* Country-wise directories (USA: Yelp/BBB; UK: Yell; India: Justdial) - fakt jya sachu business kare

* Semrush thi 3-5 keywords description ma naturally; fake address kyarey nahi (GBP suspension)
