01 Design Philosophy - Website AI-made na lage

2026 ma ghani websites same dekhay chhe. Visitor 3 second ma pakdi le chhe 'aa AI/template chhe' ane trust ghate chhe. Aa doc ma badha 'AI tells' ane teno upay chhe.

AI-look na tells (aa avoid karo)

AI tellKem kharabTena badle su
Purple / blue-pink gradient hero, glowing blobsHajaro AI sites ma sameEk solid brand color + real photo background ya clean plain colour
Badha section same: icon + heading + 3 cardsRobotic, boringAlag alag layouts: photo+text split, big number strip, testimonial wall, product grid
Emoji ke generic line-icons har bullet parToy jevuReal product photos; icons ochha, ek set, ek thickness
Perfect glossy 3D/AI faces, fake team photos, stock handshakesFake lage, trust nathiReal team/owner/office/product photo (mobile thi pan chale, light saru hoy)

Human feel ni 7 rules

  1. Ek brand personality: 3 shabd lakho (jem ke warm, trusted, handmade). Design badhu e 3 shabd sathe match karvu joiye.
  2. Real content pehla: pehla real photos + real text taiyar karo, pachhi design. Lorem ipsum ke AI filler nahi.
  3. Imperfection ok: thodu asymmetric layout, hand-written signature, real founder note - human lage.
  4. Ek style system: colors, fonts, spacing, button style badhe page par same (consistency = pro).
  5. Whitespace: screen bharelu nahi. Section vachche 64-96px (desktop), 40-56px (mobile).
  6. Specific bano: 'Best quality' nahi; 'Pure 925 silver, hallmark sathe'. Specific = human.
  7. Competitor thi alag: 5 competitor sites juo (SOP 04-03); je badha same kare e nahi karvu, ek thing alag rakhvi.

5-minute AI-check (design pachhi)

  1. Homepage ek navi person ne 5 second batavo, pucho: 'AI ye banavyu lage chhe?' ane 'Aa shu vechhe?'
  2. Screenshot AI-tells table sathe match karo - 2 thi vadhu tell male to fix.
  3. Text copy ChatGPT-detector ke nahi, pan vanchi ne pucho: 'Aa hu bolu to aavu bolu?' Na hoy to fari lakho.
  4. Mobile par juo - ahin AI-templates sauthi vadhu khulle.
Checklist: 3 AI-tells thi vadhu nathi ☐ Real photos ☐ Real reviews ☐ Banned words nathi ☐ 2 fonts only ☐ 5-sec test pass ☐

02 Fonts, Colors & Spacing

Aa ek var fix karo ane website na badha page par same vapro. Free Google Fonts j vapro (speed + licence safe).

A. Colors - 60 / 30 / 10 rule

RoleShareRuleSample (badlo)
Background / neutral60%Off-white ke warm cream (pure #FFFFFF nahi hamesha), dark text #1F1F1F#FAF7F2
Brand main (header, buttons, headings)30%Business ni feel pramane ek j colourDeep teal #0B5C5A
Accent (CTA, sale, highlight)10%Ek j accent; ketlik jagya e jBurnt orange #C8651B
  • Gradient avoid (ke bahu halku, same family). Neon, purple-blue default nahi.
  • Text contrast 4.5:1 ochhu nahi (WCAG AA). Light gray on white = fail.
  • Error red / success green alag, UI maate j.
  • Niche pramane mood: Jewellery/handmade = cream + deep green/maroon + gold touch; Home/kitchen = warm white + terracotta; B2B/service = off-white + navy/slate; Kids = bright pan 2 colours j.
  • Palette 5 colours thi vadhu nahi. Banavva: coolors.co ya logo/product photo mathi colour pick.

B. Fonts - 2 j

StyleHeading fontBody fontMate
Handmade / jewellery / premiumCormorant Garamond ya Playfair DisplayLato ya Source Sans 3Warm, elegant
Ecommerce generalDM Serif Display ya Lora (ke bold sans: Work Sans)Source Sans 3 ya Nunito SansClean, trusted
B2B / service / SEO agencyWork Sans / Archivo (semi-bold)Source Sans 3Professional
Devotional / content blogMerriweather ya LoraNoto Sans (Gujarati/Hindi support: Noto Sans Gujarati / Devanagari)Readable
  • Avoid: badhe jagya e Inter + Poppins default + bahu vadhare weights. Fonts self-host ya font-display: swap (speed).
  • Max 2 families, 3 weights (400, 600, 700).
  • Gujarati/Hindi content hoy to Noto family ma test karo (conjunct letters).

D. Mega menu rules

  • Hover (desktop) / click (touch). Hover ma 150-200ms delay, flicker nahi.
  • Columns 3-4; ek column ma featured photo (real product).
  • Top category pote clickable page chhe (category page, SEO mate) - sirf dropdown label nahi.
  • Header links crawlable HTML <a href>; JS-only menu nahi (SEO).
  • Dropdown 2 level thi vadhu nahi (Category → Sub-category).

E. Fixed items - kyarey ghati na jay

  • Logo (Home link), Search, Cart, Account
  • Phone/WhatsApp ya Contact (service business ma CTA button: 'Get quote')
  • Active page highlight (underline/bold)
  • Skip-to-content link (accessibility)
  • Breadcrumb header ni niche inner pages par

F. Mobile header & menu

  • Header: hamburger (left) · logo (center/left) · search icon · cart (right). Height 56-64px.
  • Hamburger click: full-height drawer, top ma search, pachhi categories (accordion, tap = expand), niche Account, Track order, WhatsApp.
  • Bottom sticky bar (ecommerce, optional): Home, Categories, Search, Cart.
  • Tap target min 44x44px. Menu ma 7 thi vadhu item nahi; sub-items accordion.
  • Mobile par pop-up/chat widget header cover na kare.

G. Sticky header ane speed

  • Sticky karo pan compact (logo nano, top strip hide).
  • Header height CLS (layout shift) na kare - fixed height rakho.
  • Logo SVG ya WebP, preload.
Header checklist: Max 7 top items ☐ Mega menu categories crawlable ☐ Search dekhay ☐ Cart badge ☐ Mobile drawer test ☐ Active highlight ☐ Sticky compact ☐ Contrast pass ☐

D. Rules jethi 'AI-made' na lage

  • Dareka section alag layout; same 3-card row ni repetition nahi.
  • Hero ma gradient/blob nahi; real photo ya solid colour.
  • Copy: specific, 2-3 line. Banned: 'unlock', 'elevate', 'seamless', 'cutting-edge', 'in today's fast-paced world'.
  • Maximum 1 primary CTA per screen; section nu heading ek vaakya.
  • Icons ochha; photo vadhu. Emoji nahi.
  • Animations subtle (fade 200-300ms); parallax/scroll-hijack nahi (speed + mobile).
  • Image alt text real (SEO + accessibility); hero image preload, baki lazy-load.
  • Homepage H1 ek j, keyword natural; internal links categories ane best sellers par.

E. Homepage QA

5-second test pass ☐ Hero real photo ☐ Section layouts alag ☐ Reviews real ☐ Mobile 2 col grid ☐ LCP < 2.5s ☐ Links badha work ☐ Banned words nathi ☐

B. Rules

  • Mobile: columns accordion (tap to open), social + contact upar.
  • Legal pages compulsory: Privacy, Terms, Refund/Return, Shipping. GST/business detail jya lagu (invoice/legal pramane).
  • Footer links crawlable; important pages (categories, about, contact) internal links tarike.
  • Background: brand dark colour ya warm neutral; contrast pass.
  • 'Made with AI/template' credit ke generic 'Powered by' nahi.
  • Fake trust badges (Norton etc.) nahi - je sachu hoy e j.
  • Contact: ek real email + WhatsApp. Phone hoy to click-to-call.
Footer checklist: 4 columns ☐ Legal pages ☐ Contact real ☐ Payment icons sachu ☐ Mobile accordion ☐ Links work ☐ Copyright year auto ☐

06 Imagery, Icons & Copywriting

A. Photos

  • Real first: product photos, packaging, workshop/office, owner/team. Mobile camera + day light + clean background chale.
  • Stock photo ochha; vaparo to authentic (handshake/laptop-smile avoid). Ek style: same light, same background colour.
  • AI-generated photo vapro to: potana ChatGPT thi j, product real ne match kare (kharab hands/text nahi), ane customer ne product na bahana ma misleading nahi.
  • Sizes: hero 1920x1080 (WebP), product 1:1 ya 4:5 same ratio badhe, category tile 4:5. Compress <200KB; alt text real.
  • Face/people hoy to real; testimonials ma real photo ya initials.

B. Icons

  • Ek icon set (Lucide / Phosphor / Feather), ek thickness, ek colour.
  • Icon ochha - jya jaruri (cart, search, account, shipping). Feature list ma icon-for-everything nahi.
  • Emoji UI ma nahi.

C. Copywriting

Avoid (AI words)Vapro
Unlock the power of...Seedhu: 'Tamara ghar mate silver jewellery'
Elevate your style'Daily wear mate light 925 silver rings'
Seamless experience'3 click ma order'
Cutting-edge / revolutionarySpecific fact (material, size, delivery days)
In today's fast-paced worldSidhi vaat thi sharu karo
Em-dash ane perfect 3-point lists badhe jagyaVary karo; kyarek 2, kyarek 4
  • Tone: ek brand voice (friendly ke formal) - badhe page par same.
  • Product description: 1 line benefit + specifics (material, size, care) + short story. Badha products same template-word ma nahi.
  • Button text specific: 'Add to cart', 'Get free audit' - 'Click here' nahi.
  • Error/empty states ma pan human text: 'Cart khali chhe - chalo kai gamtu shodhiye'.

D. Motion

  • Fade/slide 200-300ms; hover subtle. Auto-playing video background, parallax, cursor effects nahi.
  • prefers-reduced-motion respect karo.
Checklist: Real photos ☐ Ek photo style ☐ Ek icon set ☐ Banned words nathi ☐ Alt text ☐ Images <200KB ☐
